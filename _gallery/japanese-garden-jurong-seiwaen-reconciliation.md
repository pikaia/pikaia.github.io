Five more photographs of the Japanese Garden, spanning its pre-2019 form and its 2024 relaunch as part of Jurong Lake Gardens.

*The Double Beauty Bridge linking the Chinese and Japanese Gardens, photographed in September 2024, shortly after the redeveloped gardens reopened. (Photo: FN-082 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
