More images of the people, places and money connected with the $50 million "gift" of 1942.

### Lim Boon Keng in 1929

![Formal portrait of Lim Boon Keng with a beard and spectacles, in a suit and tie, with Chinese characters below](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cd/%E6%9E%97%E6%96%87%E6%85%B6.jpg)

*Lim Boon Keng as president of Amoy University, from a university commemorative publication of 1929. (Amoy University commemorative publication, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Where the association was formed

![The Singapore Chinese Chamber of Commerce and Industry building on Hill Street, with Chinese-style roofs and a red gateway](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e7/Singapore_Chinese_Chamber_of_Commerce_%26_Industry_Building%2C_Aug_06.JPG)

*The Singapore Chinese Chamber of Commerce and Industry on Hill Street in 2006. The Oversea Chinese Association was formed at the Chamber's earlier building on this site on 2 March 1942. (Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### General Yamashita

![Formal portrait of General Tomoyuki Yamashita in uniform with medals, holding a sword](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f5/Yamashita_Tomoyuki.jpg)

*General Tomoyuki Yamashita, who received the $50 million cheque on 25 June 1942. (Unknown Japanese Army photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Malayan campaign, 1941

![Yamashita and Japanese officers in helmets studying maps in the jungle](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bf/Tomoyuki_yamashita_malaya.png)

*Yamashita with his officers during the invasion of Malaya, December 1941. (Unknown photographer, Japanese war history office, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Stamps for the fall of Singapore

![Two Japanese postage stamps, one red and one green, each with a portrait and an overprint in Japanese](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/1942_Fall_of_Singapore_for_Japanese_stamps.JPG)

*Japanese stamps issued in 1942 to mark the fall of Singapore. (Japanese Ministry of Communications, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Syonan-to on the map

![A Japanese map of Singapore island in colour, titled in Japanese, with districts and roads marked](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4a/SyonanSingapore.jpg/1920px-SyonanSingapore.jpg)

*A Japanese map of Singapore, renamed Syonan-to, dated 12 October 1942. (Magyer Lohasa, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The first anniversary of the Occupation

![A large crowd seated in rows inside a theatre, facing a stage, seen from above](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/ff/Commemoration_of_anniversary_of_occupation_in_Asia_Raya_theatre%2C_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._5_%281943-03-01%29%2C_p31.jpg)

*A gathering in the Asia Raya theatre in Singapore to mark the first anniversary of the Occupation, from the Japanese propaganda magazine Djawa Baroe, 1 March 1943. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A victory parade in Syonan

![A procession of people marching along a street in Singapore past parked cars and onlookers](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8d/%27Celebration%27_of_Japanese_victory_in_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._7_%281943-04-01%29%2C_p28.jpg)

*A procession in Singapore described as a "celebration" of Japanese victory, from Djawa Baroe, 1 April 1943. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Occupation money: five dollars

![Front and back of a Japanese Occupation five-dollar note for Malaya, in red and purple](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/51/MAL-M6c-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-Five_Dollars_ND_%281942%29.jpg/1920px-MAL-M6c-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-Five_Dollars_ND_%281942%29.jpg)

*A five-dollar note issued by the Japanese government for Malaya in 1942. (National Museum of American History, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Occupation money: one hundred dollars

![Front and back of a Japanese Occupation hundred-dollar note for Malaya, in brown](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d2/MAL-M8b-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-100_Dollars_ND_%281944%29.jpg/1920px-MAL-M8b-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-100_Dollars_ND_%281944%29.jpg)

*A hundred-dollar note issued in 1944, as the occupation currency lost its value. (National Museum of American History, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Occupation money: one thousand dollars

![Front and back of a Japanese Occupation thousand-dollar note for Malaya, in green](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d6/MAL-M10b-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-1000_Dollars_ND_%281945%29.jpg/1920px-MAL-M10b-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-1000_Dollars_ND_%281945%29.jpg)

*A thousand-dollar note issued in 1945, the largest denomination of the occupation currency in Malaya. (National Museum of American History, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The war crimes trials, 1946

![Japanese defendants standing in a wooden dock in a courtroom, with lawyers seated at tables in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5f/War_crimes_trials_at_Singapore.jpg)

*Japanese defendants in the dock of the Supreme Court in Singapore at the start of a war crimes trial, 21 January 1946. Shinozaki later gave evidence at the trials. (Pilot Officer Vasey, Royal Air Force official photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Lim Boon Keng's grave
