Photographs of Syonan and the people behind the Japanese-language drive, and more pages from the Occupation's newspaper that carried its lessons and notices.

### A street in Syonan, 1943

![Shophouses with large Japanese signboards above the five-foot way, rickshaws and cars in the street and men in uniform walking past](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fc/Street_in_Shonan.JPG)

*A street in Syonan in February 1943, with signboards in Japanese above the shops. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Queen Street before the war

![A quiet tree-lined road with low buildings behind walls and a few figures in the distance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f6/%27Queen_Street._Singapore.%2C_KITLV_1404750.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-%27Queen_Street._Singapore.%2C_KITLV_1404750.tiff.jpg)

*Queen Street, Singapore, in a photograph dated 14 April 1928. The Syonan Nippon Gakuen opened in Queen Street in May 1942. (Anonymous, KITLV, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Cathay Building

![A tall pale building with stepped upper storeys rising above low buildings and trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/57/The_Cathay_Building_in_Singapore_1945.jpg)

*The Cathay Building around 1945. Its cinema, renamed the Dai Toa Gekizyo, held the Syonan Nippon Gakuen's first graduation ceremony on 2 August 1942. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Ibuse Masuji

![A black and white photograph of a Japanese man in round glasses and a dark kimono, seated in front of a microphone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b1/Masuji_Ibuse_01.jpg)

*The novelist Ibuse Masuji in 1952. In February 1942 he ran the English-language Syonan Times, which printed the first Nippon Lesson. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Jinbo Kotaro

![A black and white photograph of a Japanese man in a dark kimono seated by a window](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/00/Jimbo_Kotaro.jpg/1280px-Jimbo_Kotaro.jpg)

*The poet Jinbo Kotaro, principal of the Syonan Nippon Gakuen, in a photograph published in 1955. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Downtown Syonan, 1943

![A black and white photograph of rows of parked cars beside a colonnaded building, with a domed building in the distance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/58/Downtown_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._4_%281943-02-15%29%2C_p18.jpg/1280px-Downtown_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._4_%281943-02-15%29%2C_p18.jpg)

*Downtown Syonan in a photograph from the Japanese-run magazine Djawa Baroe, 15 February 1943. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### "Nippon Lessons Appreciated", February 1942

![Page 4 of the Syonan Times of 26 February 1942, with an article headed Nippon Lessons Appreciated in the centre column](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-02-26-page-4.jpg)

*Page 4 of the Syonan Times, 26 February 1942. The report "Nippon Lessons Appreciated", in the centre column, describes families picking up their first Japanese phrases. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Lesson No. 24, March 1942

![Page 2 of the Syonan Times of 20 March 1942, a page of advertisements with a boxed word list headed Nippon Lesson No. 24 at bottom right](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-03-20-page-2.jpg)

*Page 2 of the Syonan Times, 20 March 1942. "Nippon Lesson No. 24", at bottom right, gives each English word with its Japanese and a pronunciation guide. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The first graduation, August 1942

![Page 4 of the Syonan Times of 1 August 1942, with an article headed Nippon Gakuen Graduation Ceremony To-morrow in the centre and a radio Nippon-go lesson on the right](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-08-01-page-4.jpg)

*Page 4 of the Syonan Times, 1 August 1942, announcing the Syonan Nippon Gakuen's first graduation ceremony (centre) beside the day's radio Nippon-go lesson (right). (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### 734 applicants, October 1942

![Page 4 of the Syonan Times of 8 October 1942, with an article headed Popularity Of Syonan Nippon Gakuen Course Amply Demonstrated at top right](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-10-08-page-4.jpg)

*Page 4 of the Syonan Times, 8 October 1942. The report at top right says 234 of 734 applicants were chosen for the Gakuen's third term. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Nippon-go allowances, November 1943

![The front page of the Syonan Sinbun of 11 November 1943, with an article headed Nippon-Go Allowances Increased in the second column and a banner reading Speak Nippon-Go And Make Yourself Dai Toa-Minded at the foot](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-11-11-page-1.jpg)

*The front page of the Syonan Sinbun, 11 November 1943. "Nippon-Go Allowances Increased" is in the second column, and the banner at the foot reads "Speak Nippon-Go And Make Yourself Dai Toa-Minded". (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### How to write katakana, 1944

![Page 2 of the Syonan Shimbun of 2 February 1944, with a large chart headed Syonan Shimbun Nippon-Go Lessons, How To Write Katakana, in the centre](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1944-02-02-page-2.jpg)

*Page 2 of the Syonan Shimbun, 2 February 1944, with a supplementary Nippon-go lesson on how to write katakana. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*
