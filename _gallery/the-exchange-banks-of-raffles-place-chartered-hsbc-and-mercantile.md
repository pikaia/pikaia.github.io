Eleven more pictures of Raffles Place, Battery Road and Collyer Quay, from the 1890s to today.

### Raffles Place, around 1890

![A wide, unpaved square lined with two-storey shops and offices, with a large tree at the far end](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8d/KITLV_-_103753_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_103753_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Raffles Place around 1890, with John Little and Co., the Singapore Dispensary and McAlister and Co. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Chartered Bank's palazzo

![A three-storey stone bank building with rows of arched windows on a street corner](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/79/Chartered_Bank_Building_Singapore.jpg)

*The Chartered Bank's building at Battery Road and Flint Street, opened in 1895, around 1908. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Battery Road, around 1905

![A street of ornate buildings with turrets, with a fountain and trees in the foreground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/42/Battery_Road%2C_c._1905.jpg)

*Battery Road around 1905, with the Hongkong and Shanghai Bank, the Medical Hall and Gresham House, and the Tan Kim Seng Fountain at bottom left. (G. R. Lambert and Co., public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Johnston's Pier and the Hongkong Bank

![A tinted postcard of boats at a pier in front of a large turreted bank building](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0f/Johnston_%27s_Pier_and_Hongkong_Shanghai_Bank_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404895.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Johnston_%27s_Pier_and_Hongkong_Shanghai_Bank_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404895.tiff.jpg)

*Johnston's Pier and the Hongkong and Shanghai Bank on an early postcard. (G. R. Lambert and Co., KITLV, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Raffles Place, around 1910

![A square with a garden in the middle, rickshaws and carriages, and tall colonial buildings on both sides](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/da/KITLV_-_79891_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79891_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

### A fountain in the square

![A wide road junction with a fountain on a round base, rickshaws, and large bank buildings on each side](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/22/KITLV_-_79888_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Square_at_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79888_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Square_at_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

### Battery Road, around 1910

![A busy street of rickshaws and carriages between ornate buildings, with a flag flying on one roof](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/89/KITLV_-_79890_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Battery_Road_at_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79890_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Battery_Road_at_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)
