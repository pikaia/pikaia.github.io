More photographs from the oil-shock winter — Singapore's refining shore as it looks today, the embargo's principals, and the American crisis that Singapore, by taxing and exhorting instead of rationing, never had.

### The refining shore today

![Jurong Island and the Jurong shore from Jurong Hill](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f4/Jurong_Hill_aerial_panorama.jpg)

*Jurong Island and the Jurong shore seen from Jurong Hill: the refining coast that received the embargoed world's crude, as it looks today. (Bob T, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Oil-fired power

![Senoko Power Station](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d2/Senoko_Power_Station.JPG)

*Senoko Power Station on the northern shore — oil-fired generation of the kind that, in 1973, produced every unit of Singapore's electricity from imported fuel. (Terence Ong, CC BY 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The king whose oil it was

![King Faisal welcomed to the White House, 1971](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/Arrival_ceremony_welcoming_King_Faisal_of_Saudi_Arabia_05-27-1971.jpg)

*King Faisal of Saudi Arabia is welcomed to the White House on 27 May 1971 — two years before the embargo made his kingdom's oil a weapon of war. (Robert L. Knudsen, White House Photo Office, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1971)*

### Oregon's flag system

![A service station flying Oregon's allocation flags](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/93/THE_STATE_OF_OREGON_HAD_A_FLAG_SYSTEM_IN_ADDITION_TO_ITS_ODD-EVEN_SYSTEM_OF_ALLOCATING_GASOLINE._A_GREEN_FLAG_MEANT..._-_NARA_-_555485.jpg)

*Oregon's flag system in January 1974: green meant petrol for all, yellow meant regulars and commercial users only, red meant none left. (David Falconer, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1974)*

### Rationing by number plate

![Odd-even rationing in Oregon, 1973](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e9/THE_STATE_OF_OREGON_WAS_THE_FIRST_TO_GO_TO_A_SYSTEM_OF_ODD_AND_EVEN_NUMBERS_DURING_THE_GASOLINE_CRISIS_IN_THE_FALL..._-_NARA_-_555489.jpg)

*Oregon was the first American state to ration petrol by number plate — odd numbers on some days, even on others — in the fall of 1973. (David Falconer, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1973)*

### Reading the rules

![A motorist reads the odd-even plan in a newspaper](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/65/GAS_RATIONING_SYSTEM_%28ODD-EVEN_PLAN%29_IS_ANNOUNCED_IN_AN_AFTERNOON_NEWSPAPER_BEING_READ_AT_A_SERVICE_STATION_WITH_A..._-_NARA_-_555474.jpg)

*A motorist reads the odd–even plan in an afternoon newspaper at an Oregon service station. (David Falconer, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1973)*

### Never on Sunday

![A "Never on Sunday" sign at an Oregon station](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/28/%22NEVER_ON_SUNDAY%22_WAS_THE_THEME_SONG_OF_THE_OREGON_GASOLINE_DEALERS_ASSOCIATION_DURING_THE_GASOLINE_SHORTAGE_DURING..._-_NARA_-_555477.jpg)

*"Never on Sunday" was the Oregon dealers' answer to the shortage: stations closed on Sundays to stretch the week's supply. (David Falconer, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1973)*

### Dry by midday

![Stations running dry along Highway 101](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/01/GASOLINE_SHORTAGE_HIT_THE_STATE_OF_OREGON_IN_THE_FALL_OF_1973_BY_MIDDAY_GASOLINE_WAS_BECOMING_UNAVAILABLE_ALONG..._-_NARA_-_555405.jpg)

*By midday, petrol was becoming unavailable along Oregon's Highway 101 — the daily reality of the embargoed countries that Singapore's tax-and-exhort winter avoided. (David Falconer, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1973)*
