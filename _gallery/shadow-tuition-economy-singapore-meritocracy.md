Seven more images tracing the physical world behind Singapore's exam-driven education system — the street that grew into its book district, the classrooms, labs, and dormitories where generations of students studied for their exams, and the exam halls and bookshops the system still runs through today.

### The street that became Singapore's book district

![Bras Basah Road, Singapore, early 20th century](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Singapore._Brass_Bassa_Road.%2C_KITLV_1404985.tiff/lossy-page1-960px-Singapore._Brass_Bassa_Road.%2C_KITLV_1404985.tiff.jpg)

*Bras Basah Road, Singapore, photographed sometime between 1906 and 1930 — decades before it became the strip of bookshops and stationers, selling everything from schoolbooks to assessment guides, that generations of exam-taking students would come to know. (Photo: KITLV collection / Leiden University Library / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0)*
