More pictures of the islands Ya'acob Mohamed represented, the places he grew up and served in, and the people in his story.

### Pulau Brani's water village

![An old postcard of a large village of houses on stilts over the sea, with wooded hills behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/06/Malay_-_village_Pulo_Brani_Singapore_%28NYPL_Hades-2359734-4044499%29.jpg/1280px-Malay_-_village_Pulo_Brani_Singapore_%28NYPL_Hades-2359734-4044499%29.jpg)

*A postcard of the Malay village on Pulau Brani, from the early twentieth century. (Scan by the New York Public Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Johor Bahru, where he grew up

![A sepia photograph of a canal with a balustraded bridge and two-storey shophouses along a street](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/04/Johor_Bahru_town_in_the_1920s.jpg)

*Sungai Segget in Johor Bahru in the 1920s or 1930s. Ya'acob's family moved to Johor when he was an infant. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Lee Kuan Yew in 1965

![A black and white photograph of a man in a dark suit and tie](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/13/Lee_Kuan_Yew%2C_1965_%28cropped%29.jpg)

*Lee Kuan Yew in 1965, the year he asked for Ya'acob's speech on Malays and migration to be broadcast three times. (Australian News and Information Bureau, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Gamal Abdel Nasser

![A black and white portrait of a smiling man in a suit and tie](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/db/Gamal_Abdel_Nasser_Portrait_1962_%283x4_cropped%29.jpg/1280px-Gamal_Abdel_Nasser_Portrait_1962_%283x4_cropped%29.jpg)

*President Gamal Abdel Nasser of the United Arab Republic in 1962, the year he awarded Ya'acob the Order of the United Arab Republic. (Stevan Kragujević, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Al-Azhar in Cairo

![An old photograph of students seated in groups on the floor of a mosque's arched hall](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f1/V.M._Doroshevich-East_and_War-Arab_University_in_Cairo_enhaced.jpg)

*Students at Al-Azhar in Cairo, in a photograph first published in 1905. As ambassador to Egypt, Ya'acob raised funds for a hostel for Singapore students at Al-Azhar University. (Uncredited photographer, derivative work by V2k, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The southern islands on a map

![A colour map of Singapore island and the small islands to its south](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Singapore_-_DPLA_-_2c6cbbcc6e4ff6930e06b68da922e07e.jpg/1280px-Singapore_-_DPLA_-_2c6cbbcc6e4ff6930e06b68da922e07e.jpg)

*A Central Intelligence Agency map of Singapore, showing the southern islands including Pulau Brani, Pulau Bukom, Pulau Sebarok and Pulau Semakau. (Central Intelligence Agency, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Pulau Brani today

![A view across the water to an island covered with container cranes](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d0/Pulau_Brani%2C_Aug_06.JPG/1280px-Pulau_Brani%2C_Aug_06.JPG)

*Pulau Brani in 2006, by then a container terminal. (User:Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Pulau Bukom

![A view across a harbour full of ships towards an island with an oil refinery and smoke](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f5/Pulau_Bukom_from_Sentosa.JPG/1280px-Pulau_Bukom_from_Sentosa.JPG)

*Pulau Bukom and its oil refinery, seen from Sentosa in 2006. (Terence Ong, CC BY 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Pulau Sebarok

![A low island with white oil storage tanks seen across the sea](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Pulau_Sebarok%2C_Nov_06.jpg/1280px-Pulau_Sebarok%2C_Nov_06.jpg)

*Pulau Sebarok in 2006, now an oil storage terminal. Ya'acob opened a mosque there for Esso's Muslim workers in 1964. (User:Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Pulau Semakau

![A wide view of a rock-lined shore and calm sea under a pale sky](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/Pulau_Semakau%2C_panorama%2C_Nov_06.jpg/1280px-Pulau_Semakau%2C_panorama%2C_Nov_06.jpg)

*Pulau Semakau in 2006. Its villages, and the nearby Pulau Seking, were cleared for the Semakau landfill. (User:Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*
