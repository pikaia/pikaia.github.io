More pictures of Kuching, Sandakan, Kudat and Jesselton in the days of the Rajahs and the North Borneo Company, and of the traces of Borneo in Singapore today.

### Kuching in 1844

![An engraving of a wide river winding between wooded banks, with a small town of wooden houses on the left and a mountain in the distance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fa/Kuching_1844.jpg/1280px-Kuching_1844.jpg)

*The Sarawak River and the town of Kuching in 1844, five years after James Brooke first arrived. (Frank Samuel Marryat, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Rajah James Brooke

![An oil portrait of a young man with dark curly hair in an open-necked white shirt and dark jacket, standing against a dark background](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/73/Sir_James_Brooke_%281847%29_by_Francis_Grant.jpg)

*James Brooke, the first Rajah of Sarawak, painted in 1847. He set out for Sarawak from Singapore in 1839. (Francis Grant, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The river at Kuching, 1876

![A painting of a calm river framed by tall palms and broad-leaved trees, with a small town on the far bank](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e2/Marianne_North_%281830-1890%29_-_View_of_Kuching_and_River%2C_Sarawak%2C_Borneo_-_MN543_-_Marianne_North_Gallery.jpg)

*A view of Kuching and the Sarawak River, painted by the English naturalist Marianne North in 1876. (Marianne North, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Public Offices, Kuching

![A black-and-white photograph of a long, low white government building with a colonnaded veranda behind a lawn and a fence](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2d/Kuching%2C_Sarawak%3B_the_Public_Offices._Photograph._Wellcome_V0037395.jpg/1280px-Kuching%2C_Sarawak%3B_the_Public_Offices._Photograph._Wellcome_V0037395.jpg)

### The Borneo Company in the 1950s

![A black-and-white photograph of a modern white office building with long horizontal windows on a riverbank, with dark trees behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3a/The_Borneo_Company_Limited_building_between_1950-1959.jpg/1280px-The_Borneo_Company_Limited_building_between_1950-1959.jpg)

*The Borneo Company's building on the river at Kuching in the 1950s. (Ho Ah Chon, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Sandakan, about 1895

![A black-and-white photograph of a harbour town of wooden houses and godowns on stilts over the water, with a jetty and hills behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/Sandakan-Sabah-Sandakan-about1895.PNG/1280px-Sandakan-Sabah-Sandakan-about1895.PNG)

*Sandakan, the capital of British North Borneo, around 1895. (Dr. Johnstone and A. J. West, officers of the British North Borneo Company, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Kudat in the 1890s

![A black-and-white photograph of a small town of timber buildings with pitched roofs among trees, with the sea in the distance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3a/OldKudat-1.gif)

*Kudat in the 1890s. Elizabeth Choy was born here in 1910. (Dr. Johnstone and A. J. West, officers of the British North Borneo Company, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Jesselton, about 1911

![A black-and-white panoramic photograph of a seaside town of wooden houses, a railway line curving along the shore and hills behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/81/Jesselton%2C_circa_1911.JPG)

*Jesselton, today's Kota Kinabalu, around 1911, about twelve years after it was founded. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
