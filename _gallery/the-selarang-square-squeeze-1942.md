Twelve more photographs of the Selarang square in September 1942, most of them taken in secret by George Aspinall, and of Changi before and after the war.

### The square on the 3rd of September

![Selarang Barracks square crowded with prisoners, shelters and stores, between two barrack blocks](/assets/images/awm-132933-selarang-square-1942.jpg)

*The square on the 3rd of September 1942, the second day. (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial 132933, public domain)*

### Shelters in the square

![Makeshift tents and shelters filling the Selarang square, with men among them and barrack blocks behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e3/Australian_War_Memorial_132935.JPG)

*Prisoners in the square on the 4th of September 1942, with makeshift shelters put up by the cooks in the foreground. The dark mark at the top is damage to the negative. (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial 132935, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Makeshift tents

![Rows of low makeshift tents covering the square, photographed from above](/assets/images/awm-p02569-158-selarang.jpg)

*Makeshift tents in the square in September 1942. (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial P02569.158, public domain)*

### Between the barrack blocks

![The square crowded with shelters and men, with two barrack blocks behind](/assets/images/awm-p02569-161-selarang.jpg)

*The square from one of the barrack blocks. (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial P02569.161, public domain)*

### The far side of the square

![Shelters and crowds in the square in front of a long barrack block](/assets/images/awm-p02569-164-selarang.jpg)

*Shelters packed against a barrack block on the far side of the square. (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial P02569.164, public domain)*

### Barrack Square, September 1942

![A dense crowd of prisoners in the square between two barrack blocks, with a printed label reading "2 - Barrack Sq., Sept., 1942"](/assets/images/awm-p02569-166-selarang.jpg)

*The square in an album print that Aspinall labelled "Barrack Sq., Sept., 1942". (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial P02569.166, public domain)*

### Shelters and stores

![Tents and shelters in the foreground of the crowded square, with a label reading "4 - Barrack Sq., Sept., 1942"](/assets/images/awm-p02569-168-selarang.jpg)

*Another of Aspinall's labelled album prints of the square. (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial P02569.168, public domain)*

### From the water tower

![A wide view over scrubland towards the long low blocks of Selarang Barracks](/assets/images/awm-p02569-153-selarang.jpg)

*Selarang Barracks from the Selarang water tower, during the prisoners' time at Changi. (George Aspinall, Australian War Memorial P02569.153, public domain)*

### Changi Gaol in 1936

![A long, low prison wall with a tower, on open ground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/31/Changi_Prison_in_1936.jpg/1280px-Changi_Prison_in_1936.jpg)

*Changi Prison soon after it was built in 1936. During the Occupation it held civilian internees and later prisoners of war, and Aspinall's negatives were hidden in a bore-hole inside it in 1945. (The Straits Times, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Release, 1945

![A crowd of thin men in shorts and slouch hats around an ambulance at Changi](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c7/The_Release_of_Allied_Prisoners_of_War_From_Changi_Prison%2C_1945_CF722.jpg)

*The first sick prisoners leaving Changi Prison in 1945, after the Japanese surrender. (Flying Officer Ashley, Royal Air Force, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Selarang after the war

![An aerial view of the Selarang barrack blocks among trees, with the sea and islands in the distance](/assets/images/awm-p02569-199-selarang.jpg)

*Selarang Barracks after the war, from Aspinall's collection. (Australian War Memorial P02569.199, public domain)*

### Kranji War Cemetery

![Rows of white headstones on a lawn, seen through a diamond-shaped opening in a concrete wall](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Kranji_War_Memorial_-_Tombs.jpg)

*Headstones at the Kranji War Cemetery in 2004. The four men shot on the 2nd of September 1942 are buried at Kranji. (yann, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
