More pictures of Tanjong Katong's old seafront, from the coconut plantations and the beach to the land reclaimed since 1966.

### Moonlight at the point, 1879

![A watercolour of a moonlit bay with a junk under sail and a dark headland](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/db/John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_The_Point._Tanjong_Katong._%281879%2C_Wellcome_V0037485%29.jpg/1280px-John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_The_Point._Tanjong_Katong._%281879%2C_Wellcome_V0037485%29.jpg)

*A moonlit view across the bay at Tanjong Katong, painted in 1879. (John Edmund Taylor, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Looking towards town, 1879

![A watercolour of a sandy beach with figures carrying baskets, a boat drawn up on the sand and a junk on the water](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6f/John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_Tanjong_Katong%2C_Looking_towards_Singapore_%281879%2C_Wellcome_V0037481%29.jpg/1280px-John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_Tanjong_Katong%2C_Looking_towards_Singapore_%281879%2C_Wellcome_V0037481%29.jpg)

*The beach at Tanjong Katong looking towards the town, painted in July 1879. (John Edmund Taylor, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The fort on the beach, 1880

![A sepia photograph of a low earthwork fort among coconut palms on sandy ground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4f/John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_A_Fort_at_Tanjong_Katong%2C_Singapore_%281880%2C_Wellcome_V0037520%29.jpg/1280px-John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_A_Fort_at_Tanjong_Katong%2C_Singapore_%281880%2C_Wellcome_V0037520%29.jpg)

*Fort Tanjong Katong among the palms in 1880. It was abandoned by 1901, and Katong Park was later laid out over it. (John Edmund Taylor, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
