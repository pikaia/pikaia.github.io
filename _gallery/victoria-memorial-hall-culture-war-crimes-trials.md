Five more photographs of Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall, spanning its civic life from the 1960s to today.

### The theatre, May 1960

![Victoria Theatre, Singapore, photographed in May 1960](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3d/Victoria_Theatre_Singapore_May_1960.jpg)

*Victoria Theatre in May 1960 — the sign advertises the Raffles Players performing "Twelfth Night," thirteen years after the war crimes trials held in the same complex. (Photo: Don Christie / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### The complex, 1965

![Victoria Theatre and Memorial Hall, Singapore, photographed in 1965](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/29/VictoriaTheatreandMemorialHall-Singapore-1965.jpg)

*Victoria Theatre and Memorial Hall photographed in 1965, before the Memorial Hall's 1979 renovation and renaming to Victoria Concert Hall. (Photo: Steve Swayne / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0)*

### The rear entrance, 2014

![The rear entrance of Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall, Singapore, photographed in 2014](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/19/Rear_entrance_of_Victoria_Theatre_and_Concert_Hall%2C_Singapore_-_20141101.JPG)

*The rear entrance of Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall, photographed in November 2014, shortly after it reopened following its S$158 million restoration. (Photo: Jacklee / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### The front facade, 2014

![The front of Victoria Theatre, Singapore, photographed in 2014](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/54/Front_of_Victoria_Theatre%2C_Singapore_-_20141101-01.JPG)

*The front of Victoria Theatre, photographed in November 2014, its facade retained through the restoration much as it looked in the postwar decades. (Photo: Jacklee / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### Victoria Memorial Hall, 2023

![Victoria Memorial Hall, Singapore, photographed in 2023](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c9/Victoria_Memorial_Hall%2C_Singapore%2C_August_2023.jpg)

*Victoria Memorial Hall, photographed in August 2023, still home to the Singapore Symphony Orchestra. (Photo: Ralff Nestor Nacor / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
