More images of Change Alley and the two places it joined, Raffles Place and the Collyer Quay waterfront, from about 1890 to today.

### "The Heart of the Lion City", 1931

![Page 10 of the Singapore Free Press, 25 February 1931, with advertisements for Bols liqueurs, Cold Storage milk and beer on the left and a column headed The Heart of the Lion City, Change Alley, on the right](/assets/images/singapore-free-press-1931-02-25-page-10.jpg)

*Page 10 of the Singapore Free Press, 25 February 1931. The column at top right, "The Heart of the Lion City", describes a noon walk through Change Alley. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### A Chettiar moneylender, about 1890

![A studio portrait of a Chettiar man standing barefoot in a white cloth wrapped around his body and over one shoulder](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e2/KITLV_-_6539_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Chetti_or_Coromandel_lender_in_the_Straits_Settlements_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_6539_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Chetti_or_Coromandel_lender_in_the_Straits_Settlements_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*A Chettiar moneylender photographed in Singapore around 1890. Moneylending was a separate trade from money-changing, but Indian financiers of both kinds were a familiar part of the business district. (G. R. Lambert and Co., KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
