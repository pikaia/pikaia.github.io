More photos of the Occupation's lottery tickets, the banknotes their prizes were paid in, and wartime Syonan.

### The first draw

![A lottery ticket with a red Japanese flag, large Chinese characters, a red cross and decorative borders](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9f/JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_05.jpg/1280px-JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_05.jpg)

*A ticket for the 1st draw of Konan Saiken, the monthly lottery that began in 1943. (Malai Gunseikanbu, photographed at the National Museum of Singapore by Zenwort, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The 4th draw

![A grey lottery ticket printed with a map of Southeast Asia, a Japanese flag and the words Konan Saiken](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/be/JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_08.jpg/1280px-JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_08.jpg)

*A ticket for the 4th draw, the first of the 400,000-ticket issues, sold from August 1943. (Malai Gunseikanbu, photographed at the National Museum of Singapore by Zenwort, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The 6th draw

![A pink lottery ticket printed in red with a map, a Japanese flag, large Chinese characters and the words Konan Saiken](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0a/JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_09.jpg/1280px-JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_09.jpg)

*A ticket for the 6th draw, held at the Syonan Kokaido on 2 November 1943. (Malai Gunseikanbu, photographed at the National Museum of Singapore by Zenwort, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The 16th draw

![A blue and green lottery ticket printed with a map of Malaya and the words Konan Saiken and Malai Gunseikanbu](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c6/JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_03.jpg/1280px-JapLotteryTicket-SIN-Occupation_SINMuseum_20171213_03.jpg)

*A ticket for the 16th draw of Konan Saiken. (Malai Gunseikanbu, photographed at the National Museum of Singapore by Zenwort, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A one-dollar note

![A purple banknote reading The Japanese Government One Dollar, with a bunch of fruit at each side](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/27/One_dollar_note_issued_by_the_Japanese_Government_during_the_occupation_of_Malaya%2C_North_Borneo%2C_Sarawak_and_Brunei_%281942%2C_obverse%29.jpg/1280px-One_dollar_note_issued_by_the_Japanese_Government_during_the_occupation_of_Malaya%2C_North_Borneo%2C_Sarawak_and_Brunei_%281942%2C_obverse%29.jpg)

*A $1 note issued by the Japanese government for Malaya in 1942, the price of one lottery ticket. (Kevin Dooley, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A hundred-dollar note

![A red banknote reading The Japanese Government One Hundred Dollars](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9d/One_hundred_dollar_note_issued_by_the_Japanese_Government_during_the_occupation_of_Malaya%2C_North_Borneo%2C_Sarawak_and_Brunei_%281944%2C_obverse%29.jpg/1280px-One_hundred_dollar_note_issued_by_the_Japanese_Government_during_the_occupation_of_Malaya%2C_North_Borneo%2C_Sarawak_and_Brunei_%281944%2C_obverse%29.jpg)

*A $100 note issued in 1944, as prices rose. (Scanned by Jacklee, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A thousand-dollar note

![A green banknote reading The Japanese Government One Thousand Dollars, shown front and back](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d6/MAL-M10b-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-1000_Dollars_ND_%281945%29.jpg/1280px-MAL-M10b-Malaya-Japanese_Occupation-1000_Dollars_ND_%281945%29.jpg)

*A $1,000 note issued in 1945, the largest the administration printed. (National Museum of American History, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A ration book

![A pink card ruled into columns, filled in by hand with dates and amounts, with printed headings in English and Chinese](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fd/RationBook_SIN_1942_20171213.jpg/1280px-RationBook_SIN_1942_20171213.jpg)

*A household ration book from Singapore in 1942. (Malai Gunseikanbu, photographed by Zenwort, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Syonan Sinbun, 1943

![The front page of a newspaper titled The Syonan Sinbun, with war headlines and a photograph](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9c/ST3February1943.jpg/1280px-ST3February1943.jpg)
