More images connected with the Progressive Party, from the candidates on the night of the 1951 results to the newspaper pages that followed the party from its first election to its last.

### John Laycock, 1948

![Newspaper portrait of John Laycock, an older man in a jacket and tie](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/83/John_Laycock.png)

*John Laycock, one of the party's three founders, in The Singapore Free Press, 27 July 1948. (The Singapore Free Press, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### John Laycock at the lectern, 1951

![John Laycock in a white suit speaking at a lectern](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5a/John_Laycock%2C_1951.jpg)

*John Laycock speaking to the University of Malaya Historical Society, April 1951. (Singapore Press Holdings, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Thio Chan Bee, 1951

![Thio Chan Bee in a white shirt and tie standing at a microphone on a stage](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/69/Thio_Chan_Bee.jpg)

*Thio Chan Bee, elected for the Progressives in Balestier, speaking on the night the 1951 results were declared. (Ministry of Information and the Arts, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Lim Yew Hock, 1951

![Lim Yew Hock in a white suit standing at a microphone on a stage](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/99/Lim_Yew_Hock%2C_1951.jpg)

*Lim Yew Hock of the Labour Party, who defeated the Progressive vice-president A. P. Rajah in Keppel, speaking at the 1951 Legislative Council election. (Ministry of Information and the Arts, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Vilasini Menon, 1951

![Vilasini Menon in a checked sari standing at a microphone on a stage](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b9/Vilasini_Menon.jpg)

*Vilasini Menon, who won Seletar as an independent in 1951 and became the first woman elected to the Legislative Council. (Ministry of Information and the Arts, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
