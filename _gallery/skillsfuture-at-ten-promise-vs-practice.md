Five more images tracing the story behind SkillsFuture — the industrial estate that first made workforce training a matter of state, the campus where the movement was announced, the older training system it was built on top of, an early festival meant to drive sign-ups, and the Parliament where its unclaimed credits are still being debated.

### The industrial estate that made workforce training a national concern

![Signs pointing to Jurong Industrial Estate, Singapore, June 1964](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/04/Signs_pointing_to_Jurong_Industrial_Estate_in_Singapore_June_1964.jpg)

*Directional signs to the newly-developing Jurong Industrial Estate, photographed in June 1964 — the industrialisation drive that first made a trained, adaptable workforce a matter of state policy, five decades before SkillsFuture. (Photo: Don Christie / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### The campus where the movement was announced

![ITE College Central, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2b/ITE_College_Central.jpg)

*ITE College Central in Ang Mo Kio — the venue Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong chose for his 2014 National Day Rally speech announcing SkillsFuture, and again for the 2015 rally held the year it actually launched. (Photo: Deoma12 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### The older system SkillsFuture was layered on top of

![Institute of Technical Education headquarters, Singapore, November 2006](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/42/Institute_of_Technical_Education_Headquarters%2C_Nov_06.JPG)

*The Institute of Technical Education's headquarters, photographed in November 2006 — nearly a decade before SkillsFuture existed, ITE was already Singapore's institutional answer to vocational retraining, and the base the newer, broader movement built on. (Photo: Sengkang / Wikimedia Commons, copyrighted free use)*

### An early SkillsFuture Festival, three years into the movement

![SkillsFuture Festival event at St Joseph's Institution, Singapore, 2018](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3c/2018-skillsfuture-festival.jpg)

*The SkillsFuture Festival at St Joseph's Institution in July 2018 — part of the movement's early push to turn "national movement" framing into actual course sign-ups, years before the credit take-up numbers in this post. (Photo: Smartcocloud / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### Where the unclaimed-credit questions eventually landed

![Old Parliament House, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7c/Old_Parliament_House%2C_Singapore%2C_Feb_06.JPG)

*Old Parliament House, Singapore's oldest surviving government building, dating to 1827 — a reminder of how long parliamentary questions, like the one MPs raised in January 2026 over unclaimed SkillsFuture credits, have been this institution's daily business. (Photo: Sengkang / Wikimedia Commons, copyrighted free use)*
