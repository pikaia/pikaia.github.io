### The first advertisement, October 1935

![Page 3 of The Malaya Tribune, 2 October 1935, a financial page with share prices and advertisements for several banks](/assets/images/malaya-tribune-1935-10-02-page-3.jpg)

*Page 3 of The Malaya Tribune, 2 October 1935. The United Chinese Bank's advertisement, in the left-hand column, gives its address as 31-A Chulia Street, in the Bonham Building; the Yokohama Specie Bank's notice runs directly below it. (Malaya Tribune, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### "New bank opens," October 1935

*Page 13 of The Malaya Tribune, 2 October 1935, reporting the opening of the United Chinese Bank. (Malaya Tribune, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*
