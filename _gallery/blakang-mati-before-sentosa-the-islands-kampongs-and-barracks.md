More pictures of Blakang Mati's harbour, forts and neighbouring islands, and of Sentosa as it is today.

### The harbour around 1890

![A sepia photograph looking down over trees to a calm harbour with wooded islands beyond](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7b/KITLV_-_377455_-_Port_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_377455_-_Port_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Singapore's harbour around 1890, a decade after the first forts on Blakang Mati were begun to guard it. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The new port

![A sepia photograph from a hillside over treetops to a harbour, with low wooded islands and ships in the distance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/38/KITLV_-_38151_-_The_new_port_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_38151_-_The_new_port_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*"The new port at Singapore", the wharves of New Harbour, later Keppel Harbour, around 1890. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### New Harbour, around 1910

![A sepia photograph of a harbour framed by trees, with a steamship on the water and a wooded shore beyond](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/63/KITLV_-_79927_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_New_Harbour_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79927_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_New_Harbour_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

*New Harbour around 1910. The army ferries to Blakang Mati crossed this water from Jardine Steps. (C. J. Kleingrothe, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A kampong on Pulau Brani

![A black-and-white photograph of a crowded village of attap-roofed houses on stilts over the water, with hills behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/50/KITLV_-_105809_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Malay_village_on_the_island_of_Pulau_Brani_near_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_105809_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Malay_village_on_the_island_of_Pulau_Brani_near_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif.jpg)

*A Malay village on Pulau Brani, Blakang Mati's neighbour across a narrow strait, around 1900. (G. R. Lambert & Co., KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
