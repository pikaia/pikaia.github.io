The former Malayan Railway terminus at Tanjong Pagar, its platforms, and the green corridor that replaced the tracks.

### The main hall

![The interior public hall of the former Tanjong Pagar Railway Station](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8e/Tanjong_Pagar_Railway_Station_interior_-_public_hall_%281%29.jpg)

*The barrel-vaulted main hall, photographed in June 2011. The ceramic panels around the walls are the Doulton depictions of Malayan industry. (Photo: EquatorialSky2 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### The platforms

![The platforms and tracks at the former Tanjong Pagar Railway Station](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fe/Platforms_and_tracks%2C_Tanjong_Pagar_Railway_Station%2C_Singapore_-_20100619-01.jpg)

*The platforms in 2010. Trains from here ran the length of the island and across the Causeway to Johor Bahru and on to Kuala Lumpur. (Photo: alicereneztay / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0)*

### A train at rest

![A disused KTM train standing at Tanjong Pagar Railway Station at night in 2009](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fb/Disused_train%2C_Tanjong_Pagar_Railway_Station%2C_Singapore_-_20090822.jpg)

*A KTM train standing at the terminus at night, 2009. (Photo: William Cho / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0)*

### The Rail Corridor: an old bridge, reused

![The Rail Corridor path crossing a former railway bridge over Hindhede Drive](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b9/Rail_Corridor_running_on_a_former_railway_bridge_over_Hindhede_Drive.jpg)

*The green route now runs across the former railway bridges. This one crosses Hindhede Drive near Bukit Timah. (Photo: Wzhkevin / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### The Rail Corridor today

![Cycling along the Rail Corridor in northern Singapore in 2024](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/19/Rail_Corridor%2C_Singapore_in_2024-09-27_3.jpg)

*The Rail Corridor in 2024. The twenty-four-kilometre strip of former railway land is being reopened in sections as a continuous green route. (Photo: Renek78 / Wikimedia Commons, CC0)*
