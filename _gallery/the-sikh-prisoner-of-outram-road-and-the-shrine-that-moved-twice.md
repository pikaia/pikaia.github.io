A painting of Bhai Maharaj Singh from Punjab, the shrine on the grounds of Singapore General Hospital, and the Silat Road gurdwara where the shrine was moved in 1966.

### Holding court

![A 19th-century painting of a white-bearded Sikh leader seated on a gilded throne, surrounded by armed attendants](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Painting_of_the_Sikh_warrior%2C_Bhai_Maharaj_Singh%2C_holding_court_in_his_darbar.jpg/1280px-Painting_of_the_Sikh_warrior%2C_Bhai_Maharaj_Singh%2C_holding_court_in_his_darbar.jpg)

*A 19th-century painting of Bhai Maharaj Singh holding court. (Image: unknown artist, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The shrine at the General Hospital

![A grainy photograph of a small white domed shrine standing in a grassy field](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f3/Photograph_of_the_Samadhi_of_Bhai_Maharaj_Singh_%28then_referred_to_as_%27Baba_Karam_Singh%27%29_on_the_grounds_of_Singapore_General_Hospital.jpg/1280px-Photograph_of_the_Samadhi_of_Bhai_Maharaj_Singh_%28then_referred_to_as_%27Baba_Karam_Singh%27%29_on_the_grounds_of_Singapore_General_Hospital.jpg)

*The shrine on the grounds of Singapore General Hospital, where it stood from the 1940s until 1966, when it was known locally as the shrine of "Baba Karam Singh." (Photo: unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Silat Road gurdwara in 1924

![The Silat Road gurdwara soon after it was completed in 1924, a two-storey white building with a central dome](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e3/Gurdwara_Sahib_Silat_Road_in_Singapore_after_construction_was_just_completed_in_1924.jpg/1280px-Gurdwara_Sahib_Silat_Road_in_Singapore_after_construction_was_just_completed_in_1924.jpg)

*The Silat Road gurdwara soon after it was completed in 1924. The shrine was moved to its grounds in 1966. (Photo: unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Sikh Police Contingent at Silat Road, 1931

![Members of the Sikh Police Contingent seated and standing in rows in front of the domed Silat Road gurdwara, 1931](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0a/Photograph_of_members_of_the_Sikh_Police_Contingent_in-front_of_Gurdwara_Sahib_Silat_Road_in_1931.jpg)

*Members of the Sikh Police Contingent in front of the Silat Road gurdwara in 1931, the temple where the shrine was moved in 1966. (Photo: unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
