A few more images of Singapore's rickshaws and the men who pulled them, from the 1900s to the present, alongside the building that once licensed the whole trade.

### A studio party, around 1909

![A group of Europeans in white tropical suits and dresses posed around a rickshaw in a photography studio, with the barefoot puller bent over the shaft at the edge of the frame](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a4/Europees_gezelschap_in_en_naast_een_riksja_in_een_fotostudio_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_10712.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Europees_gezelschap_in_en_naast_een_riksja_in_een_fotostudio_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_10712.tiff.jpg)

*A European group posed with a hired rickshaw in a Singapore photo studio, around 1909. The puller is at the left of the frame, demonstrating the pose. Leiden University Library (KITLV collection), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).*

### Two children and their puller

![A bare-chested rickshaw puller with a shaven head holds the shafts of a rickshaw carrying two European children in white dresses and straw hats, in front of a bungalow](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0d/Twee_meisjes_in_een_rickshaw%2C_vermoedelijk_in_Singapore%2C_KITLV_43535.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Twee_meisjes_in_een_rickshaw%2C_vermoedelijk_in_Singapore%2C_KITLV_43535.tiff.jpg)

*Two children in a rickshaw, Singapore, probably 1910–1920. Leiden University Library (KITLV collection), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).*

### At the door of the Teutonia Club, around 1910

![A single rickshaw and its puller cross the driveway in front of the grand Teutonia Club building, framed by trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4d/KITLV_-_79905_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Teutonia_Club_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79905_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Teutonia_Club_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

*A rickshaw at the Teutonia Club (later the Goodwood Park Hotel), around 1910. Photograph by C.J. Kleingrothe; Leiden University Library (KITLV collection), public domain.*

### Finlayson Green, around 1910

![A wide view across Finlayson Green, a triangular ornamental garden at the junction of Robinson Road, Cecil Street and Collyer Quay, with rickshaws, bullock carts and an electric tram passing around it, around 1910](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/84/KITLV_-_79924_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Cecil_Street%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79924_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Cecil_Street%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

*Finlayson Green, at the junction of Robinson Road, Cecil Street and Collyer Quay, around 1910 — a triangular garden laid out on 1890s reclaimed land, its site now deep in the financial district. The tram ran on the line towards Anderson Bridge. Photograph by C.J. Kleingrothe; Leiden University Library (KITLV collection), public domain.*

### The Jinrikisha Station's tower today

![The corner of the Jinrikisha Station building, a brick tower with an octagonal cupola above a curved white pediment reading "one Neil Road", with construction cranes behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e5/Jinrikisha_Station_Singapore.jpg/1280px-Jinrikisha_Station_Singapore.jpg)

*The Jinrikisha Station's corner tower and cupola, trading now as "1 Neil Road". Photo by Philip Nalangan, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*
