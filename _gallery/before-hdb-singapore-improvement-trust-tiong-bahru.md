More on the Trust that built Singapore's first public housing, and the estate it left behind.

### The Trust's own crest

![A circular crest reading "The Singapore Improvement Trust" around a lion holding a shield](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ed/Singapore_Improvement_Trust_logo.png)

*The Singapore Improvement Trust's own emblem — the body that built Tiong Bahru, dissolved in 1960 when its housing mandate passed to the newly created HDB. (Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.)*

### The estate today

![A street lined with low-rise Art Deco flats and shops on Tiong Bahru Road, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8f/Tiong_Bahru_Road_2.jpg/1280px-Tiong_Bahru_Road_2.jpg)

*Tiong Bahru Road, 2024 — the estate's original low-rise scale has survived, even as its shopfronts have turned over. (Photo: Fanz226, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.)*

![Another block of SIT-era flats in Tiong Bahru, Singapore, with balconies and awnings](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Tiong_Bahru_10%2C_Jul_06.JPG/1280px-Tiong_Bahru_10%2C_Jul_06.JPG)

*Another Tiong Bahru block, 2006. (Photo: Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons.)*

![A paved footpath running alongside a canal between Tiong Bahru Road and Boon Tiong Road, lined with trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1a/Footpath_running_alongside_canal_between_Tiong_Bahru_Road_and_Boon_Tiong_Road.jpg/1280px-Footpath_running_alongside_canal_between_Tiong_Bahru_Road_and_Boon_Tiong_Road.jpg)

*The canal and footpath between Tiong Bahru Road and Boon Tiong Road — part of the drainage infrastructure the SIT spent from 1926 to 1931 putting in before it built a single flat. (Photo: Wzhkevin, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.)*

### Getting there today

![The entrance to Tiong Bahru MRT Station, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a8/Tiong_Bahru_MRT_Station_202407_3.jpg/1280px-Tiong_Bahru_MRT_Station_202407_3.jpg)

*Tiong Bahru MRT Station, 2024 — one reason the estate became so easy to gentrify. (Photo: Fanz226, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.)*

### The market's courtyard

![A courtyard garden inside Tiong Bahru Market, with greenery and seating between food stalls](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9b/Tiong_Bahru_Market%2C_courtyard_garden.jpg/1280px-Tiong_Bahru_Market%2C_courtyard_garden.jpg)
