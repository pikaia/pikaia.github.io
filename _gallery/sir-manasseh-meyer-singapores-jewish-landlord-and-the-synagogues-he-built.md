More pictures of the buildings Sir Manasseh Meyer built, owned or paid for, and of the places connected with his life and legacy.

### Meyer's chair at Chesed-El

![A wooden chair with a cane seat and a small brass plaque on its back, beside a man standing in a synagogue](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b4/Manasseh_Meyer%27s_chair%2C_Chesed-El_Synagogue%2C_Singapore_-_20100815.jpg/1280px-Manasseh_Meyer%27s_chair%2C_Chesed-El_Synagogue%2C_Singapore_-_20100815.jpg)

*The chair at Chesed-El Synagogue where Manasseh Meyer sat with his family, with a plaque bearing his name, in 2010. (Smuconlaw, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The memorial plaque

![A white marble plaque with an inscription in capital letters, on a wall](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/87/Memorial_plaque_to_Menasseh_Meyer%2C_Chesed-El_Synagogue%2C_Singapore_-_20100815.jpg/1280px-Memorial_plaque_to_Menasseh_Meyer%2C_Chesed-El_Synagogue%2C_Singapore_-_20100815.jpg)

*A memorial plaque in Chesed-El Synagogue recording that the synagogue and the adjoining land were built and donated by Sir Manasseh Meyer, in 2010. (Smuconlaw, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Inside Maghain Aboth

![The interior of a synagogue with tall white columns, red carpets and dark wooden benches](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/28/Interior_of_Maghain_Aboth_Synagogue.jpg)

*The interior of Maghain Aboth Synagogue on Waterloo Street in 2009. (Esther Lim, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Raffles Place in the 1880s

![A sepia photograph of a street of arcaded two- and three-storey shop buildings, with a few people and carriages](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9f/KITLV_-_103746_-_Lambert_%26_Co._-_Raffles_Place_in_Singapore_-_circa_1885.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_103746_-_Lambert_%26_Co._-_Raffles_Place_in_Singapore_-_circa_1885.tif.jpg)

*Raffles Place around 1885, the year Meyer began buying property. (Lambert & Co., KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Raffles Place around 1920

![A black-and-white photograph of large ornate commercial buildings around an open square, with cars parked in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a4/Raffles_Place_c._1920.jpg)

*Raffles Place around 1920, where Meyer owned much of the property. (National Museum of Singapore, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Collyer Quay

![A sepia photograph of a long row of arcaded waterfront buildings along a sea wall, with small boats on the water](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/50/KITLV_-_103744_-_Collyer_Quay_in_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_103744_-_Collyer_Quay_in_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Collyer Quay around 1890; Meyer Brothers later had its office at No. 14. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Adelphi Hotel

![A black-and-white postcard of a long three-storey corner hotel with arcades and turrets](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7d/Adelphi_Hotel_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_34127.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Adelphi_Hotel_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_34127.tiff.jpg)

*The Adelphi Hotel, built by Meyer, in an undated postcard. It closed in 1973 and was demolished in 1980. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

*Crescent Flats at Tanjong Katong, built by Meyer in 1909, in an undated postcard. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
