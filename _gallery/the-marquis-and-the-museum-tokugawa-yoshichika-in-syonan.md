More photographs and newspaper pages of Marquis Tokugawa, the Raffles Museum and the Botanic Gardens, from before the war to today.

### Raffles's statue at Empress Place

![A large group of men in white suits seated and standing in front of the Victoria Memorial Hall, with the statue of Raffles on its pedestal behind them](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/KITLV_A1219_-_Artsen_voor_Victoria_Memorial_Hall_te_Singapore_met_op_de_achtergrond_het_standbeeld_van_Sir_Stamford_Raffles_tijdens_het_vijfde_congres_van_de_Far_Eastern_Association_of_Tropical_Medici%2C_KITLV_48031.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-thumbnail.tiff.jpg)

*Doctors at the fifth congress of the Far Eastern Association of Tropical Medicine in front of the Victoria Memorial Hall, with Raffles's statue behind them. The statue stood here until September 1942, when it was moved into the museum. (Unknown author, KITLV collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Tanakadate's museum, June 1942

![A full newspaper page with war news and a report headed Syonan To Be Centre Of Scientific Research In Malayan Archipelago at the bottom left](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-06-27-page-4.jpg)

*The Syonan Times of 27 June 1942, page 4. The report "Syonan To Be Centre Of Scientific Research In Malayan Archipelago", at the bottom left, describes Professor Tanakadate's work at the Syonan Museum. (The Syonan Times, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The statue moves, September 1942

![A full newspaper page with a large photograph of Raffles's statue wrapped in scaffolding, under the headline Statue Of Founder Removed To Museum](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-09-13-page-4.jpg)

*The Syonan Times of 13 September 1942, page 4. The report "Statue Of Founder Removed To Museum", in the second column, and the photograph beside it show the statue being taken down at Empress Place. (The Syonan Times, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The Marquis's scholarships, December 1942

![The front page of The Syonan Sinbun with the headline Marquis Tokugawa Grants Five Scholarships To Malaians at the top left](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-12-16-page-1.jpg)

*The Syonan Sinbun of 16 December 1942, page 1. The lead report at the top left, "Marquis Tokugawa Grants Five Scholarships To Malaians", announces the five Johor students he paid to send to Tokyo. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Koriba arrives, January 1943

![The front page of The Syonan Sinbun with war news and a short report headed Malai And Sumatra Plants To Be Used For Research in the third column](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-01-26-page-1.jpg)

*The Syonan Sinbun of 26 January 1943, page 1. The short report "Malai And Sumatra Plants To Be Used For Research", in the middle of the third column, is an interview with the newly arrived director of the Gardens, Kwan Koriba. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Six hundred visitors a day, January 1943

![A full newspaper page with a photograph of wooden clogs being made and a boxed report headed Syonan Museum, Library Popular With Scientists on the left](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-01-25-page-2.jpg)

*The Syonan Sinbun of 25 January 1943, page 2. The boxed report "Syonan Museum, Library Popular With Scientists", in the second column, gives the museum's visitor numbers and its plan to replace English labels with Japanese ones. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Koriba's lecture, August 1944

![A full newspaper page with a report headed 7,000 Varieties Of Vegetation In Nampo in the right-hand column](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1944-08-26-page-2.jpg)

*The Syonan Shimbun of 26 August 1944, page 2. The report "7,000 Varieties Of Vegetation In Nampo", in the right-hand column, covers a lecture by Koriba to about 200 botany students. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*
