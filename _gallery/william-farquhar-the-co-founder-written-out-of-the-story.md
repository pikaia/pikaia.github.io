More from the William Farquhar Collection of Natural History Drawings, and a look at how Fort Canning and the Singapore River remember (or don't remember) the two men at the centre of this story.

### A colugo and its young

![Watercolour of a flying maucauco (colugo) with its young, from the William Farquhar Collection](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/76/Flying_maucauco_with_its_young_%28William_Farquhar_Collection%2C_1819%E2%80%931823%29.jpg)

*"Flying maucauco with its young" — a Sunda colugo, one of the 477 watercolours Farquhar commissioned from anonymous Chinese artists in Malacca and Singapore. (Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The durian, painted whole

![Watercolour of a durian branch with fruit, flower and a bird, from the William Farquhar Collection](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4b/Durio_zibethinus%3B_Doorian%3B_Boorong_Brass_Brass_%28William_Farquhar_Collection%2C_1819%E2%80%931823%29.jpg)

*Durio zibethinus, complete with flower, fruit, a bird and a caterpillar on the same branch. (Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The mangosteen

![Watercolour of a mangosteen branch with fruit, from the William Farquhar Collection](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c1/Garcinia_Mangostana%3B_Booah_Mangies%3B_Boorong_Merbo_%28William_Farquhar_Collection%2C_1819%E2%80%931823%29.jpg)

*Garcinia mangostana, the mangosteen — another of the plants Farquhar had recorded during his Malacca years. (Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Raffles' landing site today

![Close-up of the English-language plaque at Raffles' Landing Site on the Singapore River](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/RafflesStatue-EnglishPlaque-RafflesLandingSite-Singapore-20100803.jpg/1280px-RafflesStatue-EnglishPlaque-RafflesLandingSite-Singapore-20100803.jpg)

*The English-language plaque at Raffles' Landing Site, one of four (English, Malay, Mandarin, Tamil) that credit him alone with the settlement's founding. (Photo: Jacklee, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
