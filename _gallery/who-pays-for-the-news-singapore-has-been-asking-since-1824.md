Three more views of Raffles Place, the square where the Chronicle and the Straits Times were first printed, as it looked from the 1890s to about 1910.

### Raffles Place, circa 1890

![Raffles Place, Singapore, circa 1890, with John Little & Co., the Singapore Dispensary and McAlister & Co. along the street](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8d/KITLV_-_103753_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_103753_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Raffles Place around 1890, with John Little & Co., the Singapore Dispensary and McAlister & Co. along the street. (Photo: unknown photographer, KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Before 1900

![Raffles' Square before 1900, with horse-drawn taxis in front of the John Little & Co. department store](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Photographic_Views_of_Singapore_Plate_03_Raffles%27_Square.jpg)

*Raffles' Square before 1900, horse-drawn taxis waiting in front of the John Little & Co. department store, from the 1900 book* Photographic Views of Singapore. *(Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co., public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Raffles Place and King Street, circa 1910

![Raffles Place and King Street, Singapore, circa 1910, with insurance offices, a dentist, Katz Brothers and the Dispensary](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/85/KITLV_-_79893_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place_and_King_Street_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79893_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place_and_King_Street_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

*Raffles Place and King Street around 1910, with the Shanghai Life Insurance Co., a dentist's practice, G. Otomune & Co., Katz Brothers and the Dispensary from left to right. (Photo: Carl Josef Kleingrothe, KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
