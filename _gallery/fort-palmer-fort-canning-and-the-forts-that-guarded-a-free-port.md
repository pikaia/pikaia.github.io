More images of the town, harbour and hills the colonial forts were built to guard, and of what remains of them today.

### Fort Canning from the river

![An album print of a hill crowned with long fort buildings, rising behind shophouses and boats on the Singapore River](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9a/Gezicht_op_Fort_Canning_met_de_Singapore_River%2C_RP-F-F01025-BS.jpg/1280px-Gezicht_op_Fort_Canning_met_de_Singapore_River%2C_RP-F-F01025-BS.jpg)

*Fort Canning on its hill above the Singapore River, photographed between 1867 and 1880. (Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The harbour in 1860

![A long panorama of Singapore harbour in 1860, with ships at anchor and low buildings along the shore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/79/KITLV_-_29175_-_View_of_the_harbor_of_Singapore_-_1860.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_29175_-_View_of_the_harbor_of_Singapore_-_1860.tif.jpg)

*The harbour of Singapore in 1860, the year after work began on Fort Canning. (Unknown photographer, KITLV 29175, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The view from Fort Canning

![A postcard view from Fort Canning hill over trees and rooftops to the harbour](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/18/KITLV_-_1404879_-_Ludwig%2C_Deutsche_Buchhandlung_Max_-_Singapore_-_View_from_Fort_Canning%2C_Singapore_-_1895-1903.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_1404879_-_Ludwig%2C_Deutsche_Buchhandlung_Max_-_Singapore_-_View_from_Fort_Canning%2C_Singapore_-_1895-1903.tif.jpg)

*"View from Fort Canning," a postcard published between 1895 and 1903, a few years before the fort was demolished. (Max Ludwig, Deutsche Buchhandlung, Singapore, KITLV 1404879, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Johnston's Pier, beside the old Fort Fullerton

![Johnston's Pier around 1890, a long covered pier with small boats crowded around it and ships in the roads beyond](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/98/KITLV_-_150812_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Johnston%27s_pier_in_the_harbor_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_150812_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Johnston%27s_pier_in_the_harbor_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Johnston's Pier around 1890. Fort Fullerton had been enlarged as far as the pier in the 1850s. (G. R. Lambert & Co., KITLV 150812, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Town Hall

![The Town Hall and Victoria Theatre in Singapore, a classical building with a tall clock tower, with people and rickshaws in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7d/KITLV_A740_-_Stadhuis_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_90268.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_A740_-_Stadhuis_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_90268.tiff.jpg)

*The Town Hall and Victoria Theatre, on a postcard of the early twentieth century. The protest meeting of March 1891 was held in the Town Hall. (Unknown photographer, KITLV 90268, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The entrance to New Harbour

![The calm water of the entrance to New Harbour, with wooded islands on either side](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/78/Gezicht_op_de_ingang_van_de_nieuwe_haven_te_Singapore_Entrance_to_New-harbour._S.pore_%28titel_op_object%29%2C_RP-F-F01104-X.jpg/1280px-Gezicht_op_de_ingang_van_de_nieuwe_haven_te_Singapore_Entrance_to_New-harbour._S.pore_%28titel_op_object%29%2C_RP-F-F01104-X.jpg)

*The entrance to New Harbour, later Keppel Harbour, between about 1870 and 1910. The forts at Pasir Panjang and on Blakang Mati were built to guard this passage. (Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### New Harbour on a postcard

![A postcard of the entrance to New Harbour, with a steamship in the channel between two wooded headlands](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/KITLV_-_1404881_-_Singapore._Entrance_at_New_harbor._-_1895-1908.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_1404881_-_Singapore._Entrance_at_New_harbor._-_1895-1908.tif.jpg)

*"Singapore. Entrance to New-harbour," a postcard published between 1895 and 1908. (Unknown photographer, KITLV 1404881, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The docks at Tanjong Pagar

![An album panorama of the Tanjong Pagar docks seen from a clock tower, with wharves, warehouses and a tall chimney](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/98/Gezicht_op_de_gebouwen_en_dokken_van_de_Tanjong_Pagar_Dock_Co._Ltd._in_Singapore_Panorama_of_six_sheets_taken_from_clock_tower_%28titel_op_object%29%2C_RP-F-F01140-S.jpg/1280px-thumbnail.jpg)

*The buildings and docks of the Tanjong Pagar Dock Company, photographed from the clock tower between about 1890 and 1905. The company's docks and coal stores were what the New Harbour forts protected. (Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The protest of 1895

![Page 3 of The Straits Times, 10 January 1895, a page of text columns and advertisements including an article headed The Military Contribution](/assets/images/straits-times-1895-01-10-page-3.jpg)

*Page 3 of The Straits Times, 10 January 1895. "The Military Contribution: Former Public Protests," in the fourth column, recalls the 1891 meeting and reports the resignations of the unofficial members of the Legislative Council. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*
