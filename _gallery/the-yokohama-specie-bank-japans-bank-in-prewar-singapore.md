More images of the Yokohama Specie Bank's Singapore, its branches elsewhere in Asia, and the papers that recorded its war.

### The Hongkong and Shanghai Bank, about 1900

![The Hongkong and Shanghai Bank building beside the Singapore River around 1900, a tall ornate building with turrets above boats on the water](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/81/Photographic_Views_of_Singapore_Plate_05_Hongkong_and_Shanghai_Bank.jpg)

*The Hongkong and Shanghai Bank on the waterfront around 1900. The Yokohama Specie Bank moved into the bank's premises in March 1942. (G. R. Lambert & Co., public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Collyer Quay in the 1900s

![Collyer Quay in the 1900s, with Johnston's Pier and a row of large commercial buildings](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/53/Collyer_Quay%2C_Singapore_1900s.jpg)

*Collyer Quay in the 1900s, with Johnston's Pier and the Hongkong and Shanghai Bank building. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The waterfront in the 1930s

![A postcard of Singapore's waterfront in the 1930s, with motor launches in the harbour and large buildings along the shore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/96/KITLV_A1112_-_Haven_van_Singapore%2C_KITLV_142600.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_A1112_-_Haven_van_Singapore%2C_KITLV_142600.tiff.jpg)

*The harbour and waterfront, on a postcard of 1933 to 1939, the years the bank was in Meyer Chambers. (Unknown photographer, KITLV 142600, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The reopening notice, March 1942

![Page 4 of The Syonan Times, 17 March 1942, with war news and, in the right-hand column, a notice headed Reopening of Banks](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1942-03-17-page-4.jpg)

*Page 4 of The Syonan Times, 17 March 1942. "Reopening of Banks," in the right-hand column, announces that the Yokohama Specie Bank and the Bank of Taiwan would reopen on 20 March. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The branches of the Southern Regions, 1943

![Page 2 of The Syonan Shimbun, 11 February 1943, with news columns and, at the lower right, an advertisement for the Yokohama Specie Bank's Syonan branch listing its branches](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-02-11-page-2.jpg)

*Page 2 of The Syonan Shimbun, 11 February 1943. The bank's advertisement, at the lower right, gives its address as 21 Collyer Quay and lists its branches in Malaya, Sumatra, Borneo and beyond. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The deposits of the "enemy banks", 1944

![Page 2 of The Syonan Shimbun, 29 September 1944, a page of official notices headed Malai Kokuji No. 52, listing the banks in charge of payment](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1944-09-29-page-2.jpg)

*Page 2 of The Syonan Shimbun, 29 September 1944. Notice No. 52, at the top centre, names the Yokohama Specie Bank as the bank paying the depositors of the Hongkong and Shanghai Bank, the Chartered Bank and others. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*
