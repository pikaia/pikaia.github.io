More photographs of the streets where Singapore's first Chinese banks opened and of the people behind them.

### Kling Street in the 1870s

![A quiet street of two-storey shophouses with verandas, photographed in the 1870s and mounted in an album](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/44/Kling_Street_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BM.jpg/1280px-Kling_Street_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BM.jpg>)

*Kling Street in the 1870s, a generation before the first Chinese banks opened there. (Photo: unknown photographer, Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Kling Street in 1862

![A stereograph pair showing a street of colonial buildings with a tree in the foreground](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/da/25_stereographs_depicting_Singapore_town_views_Or._27.423_-_photo_007.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-25_stereographs_depicting_Singapore_town_views_Or._27.423_-_photo_007.tif.jpg>)

*A stereograph of the town in 1862. (Photo: Thomas Heritage, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Raffles Place and Kling Street, around 1910

![Raffles Place around 1910, a busy junction of ornate commercial buildings with rickshaws and carts](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/85/KITLV_-_79893_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place_and_King_Street_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79893_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place_and_King_Street_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg>)

*Raffles Place and the entrance to Kling Street around 1910. (Photo: Carl Josef Kleingrothe, KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Lee Choon Guan

![A studio portrait of Lee Choon Guan in a suit and tie](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/Lee_Choon_Guan.png>)

*Lee Choon Guan, the first chairman of the Chinese Commercial Bank, around 1920. (Photo: Lee Brothers Studio, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Oei Tiong Ham

![A portrait of Oei Tiong Ham in a dark jacket and high collar](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b6/Oei_Tiong_Ham.jpg>)

*Oei Tiong Ham, the Hokkien industrialist of Java who bought shares in the Oversea-Chinese Bank. (Photo: unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Eu Tong Sen

![A colour portrait of Eu Tong Sen in a dark suit and tie](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3a/Eu_Tong_Sen.jpg>)

*Eu Tong Sen, founder of the Cantonese Lee Wah Bank, in or before 1941. (Photo: unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Lee Kong Chian

![A portrait of Lee Kong Chian in a light jacket and striped tie, wearing glasses](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e4/Lee_Kong_Chian%2C_1946.png>)

*Lee Kong Chian, who helped lead the 1932 merger and chaired OCBC from 1938 to 1964, around 1946. (Photo: Tan Kah Kee Memorial Museum, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![The OCBC building on Beach Street, George Town, an Art Deco facade with a central tower](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e2/Oversea-Chinese_Banking_Corporation_Building%2C_Beach_Street%2C_George_Town%2C_Penang.jpg>)

*The OCBC building on Beach Street, George Town, Penang. (Photo: Vnonymous, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
