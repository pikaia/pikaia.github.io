### The Eng Aun Tong building today

![The former Eng Aun Tong building at 89 Neil Road, Singapore, in 2025](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bf/89_Neil_Road%2C_Singapore_%282025%29_-_img_04.jpg)

*89 Neil Road, the former Tiger Balm factory and headquarters, in 2025. The hexagonal rooftop pavilion is said to echo the shape of the balm jar; the ground floor is now a restaurant. (Photo: Chainwit. / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0)*
