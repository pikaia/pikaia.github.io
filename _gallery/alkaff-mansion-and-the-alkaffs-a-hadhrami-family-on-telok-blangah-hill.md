*The Arcade, on an early postcard. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The waterfront from the harbour

![A long row of large buildings along a waterfront, seen across the harbour with small boats in the foreground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0f/Gebouwen_langs_de_kade_in_de_haven_van_Singapore%2C_KITLV_29186.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Gebouwen_langs_de_kade_in_de_haven_van_Singapore%2C_KITLV_29186.tiff.jpg)

### The Hotel de l'Europe, 1897

![A long two-storey hotel with verandahs and a portico, behind a lawn and trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2a/KITLV_-_29176_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Hotel_de_l%27Europe_at_Singapore_-_1897.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_29176_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Hotel_de_l%27Europe_at_Singapore_-_1897.tif.jpg)

*The Hotel de l'Europe in 1897. The Alkaffs owned the hotel for a time. (G. R. Lambert and Co., KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The rebuilt Europe Hotel, around 1914

![A large arcaded hotel building on a wide street, with rickshaws and carriages in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/57/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_03_-_Europe_Hotel.jpg)

*The Europe Hotel around 1914, after it had been rebuilt. It closed in 1932, and the old Supreme Court building stands on its site. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Tarim's palaces

![Large ornate mud-brick houses with tall windows and decorated parapets, on a dusty street](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Yemen_Tarim_Hadhramaut_1994_-_167531378_63715937ff_o.jpg/1280px-Yemen_Tarim_Hadhramaut_1994_-_167531378_63715937ff_o.jpg)
