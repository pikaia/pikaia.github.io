### A giant clam from Singapore, 1883

![An engraving of visitors in Victorian dress looking at a huge open clam shell in a glass case](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/53/FMIB_33102_%22Great_Oyster%22_%28giant_clam%3B_Tridacna_gigas%29_from_Singapore%2C_International_Fisheries_Exhibition%2C_London%2C_1883.jpg)

### Hawksbill hatchlings
