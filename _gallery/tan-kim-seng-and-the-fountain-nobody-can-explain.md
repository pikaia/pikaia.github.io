### His grandson, Tan Jiak Kim

![Portrait of Tan Jiak Kim, 1900](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/23/Tan_Jiak_Kim.jpg)

*Peranakan merchant and philanthropist Tan Jiak Kim, photographed in 1900. He personally donated $12,000 toward founding the King Edward VII College of Medicine in 1904. (Photo: National Museum of Singapore, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A second bridge, back in Malacca
