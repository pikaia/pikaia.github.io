More views of the gantries that priced Singapore's roads for a generation, and of the equipment that is replacing them.

### The gantry years

![An ERP gantry over a multi-lane Singapore road](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5c/ERP_in_Singapore.jpg)

*An ERP gantry spanning a multi-lane road, the standard sight over Singapore's expressways and city entries since 1998. (ProjectManhattan, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![An ERP toll station gantry](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/35/ERP_toll_station.jpg)

*An ERP toll station: cameras and antennas on the frame read the in-vehicle unit of every passing car and debit its card. (Ypsilon from Finland, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![An ERP gantry over a city street](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/dc/Singapore%27s_ERP_gantry.jpg)

*A gantry at street level in the city, where ERP charges guard the old Restricted Zone's entries. (Kalleboo, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![An ERP gantry at Bugis](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7a/ERPBugis.JPG)

*The gantry at Bugis, one of the city-area charging points. (mailer_diablo, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![An early ERP gantry](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c7/027_ERP_gantry.jpg)

*An early photograph of an ERP gantry, from the system's first years. (VK35, CC BY 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![An ERP gantry in portrait orientation](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/90/Electronic_Road_Pricing_gantry_in_Singapore.jpg)

*A gantry seen from below, its camera and antenna housings slung under the frame. (Tim Adams, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
