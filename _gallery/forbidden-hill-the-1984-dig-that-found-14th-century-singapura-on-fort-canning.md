More views of Fort Canning Hill, the finds from its excavations, and the places on the hill that remember old Singapura, from the 1820s to today.

### The hill on the 1825 survey

![A hand-coloured survey map of Singapore with the river winding to the sea, the town on both banks, a hill behind it and old earthworks running inland](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9f/Part_of_Singapore_Island_%28British_Library_India_Office_Records%2C_1825%2C_detail%29.jpg/1280px-Part_of_Singapore_Island_%28British_Library_India_Office_Records%2C_1825%2C_detail%29.jpg)

*The 1825 survey of Singapore, showing the hill behind the town and the line of the old earth wall that Crawfurd walked in 1822. (Unknown author, British Library India Office Records, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The view from Government Hill, 1844

![A faded oval daguerreotype in a green mount showing rooftops and a river with boats](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ef/Jules_Itier%E2%80%99s_1844_daguerreotype_of_Boat_Quay_and_Singapore_River_from_Government_Hill%2C_1844%2C_12.6_x_15_x_0.3_cm%2C_National_Museum_of_Singapore.png)

*Boat Quay and the Singapore River seen from Government Hill in 1844, one of the earliest photographs taken in Singapore. (Jules Itier, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The gold ornaments today

![A gold armlet of fine chains joined by a square plaque, with two small gold earrings, on a brown display surface](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b5/At_National_Museum_of_Singapore_2023_044.jpg/1280px-At_National_Museum_of_Singapore_2023_044.jpg)

*The gold armlet and earrings found on Fort Canning Hill in 1928, as displayed in the National Museum of Singapore in 2023. (Mike Peel, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Glass beads and shards

![Strings of tiny coloured glass beads and scattered fragments of green and dark glass on a grey display stand](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fc/At_Fort_Canning_2023_033.jpg/1280px-At_Fort_Canning_2023_033.jpg)

*Strings of glass beads, glass shards and lumps of glass, on display at Fort Canning. The hill has produced more than 10,000 glass beads. (Mike Peel, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Chinese coins

![A display board with a dozen round coins with square holes, numbered, arranged around a small framed coin](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/80/At_Fort_Canning_2023_032.jpg/1280px-At_Fort_Canning_2023_032.jpg)

*Chinese coins on display at Fort Canning. Crawfurd found Chinese coins of the 10th and 11th centuries among the ruins in 1822. (Mike Peel, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The excavation under its shelter

![A roofed shelter over a sunken excavation trench with stepped earth walls, with display panels behind it](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/11/At_Fort_Canning_2023_089.jpg/1280px-At_Fort_Canning_2023_089.jpg)

*Another view of the excavation kept open near the summit of Fort Canning Hill, photographed in 2023. (Mike Peel, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Keramat Iskandar Shah

![An open wooden pavilion with a steep tiled roof among palms, with visitors standing inside around a dark block](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/ff/Keramat_Iskandar_Shah.jpg/1280px-Keramat_Iskandar_Shah.jpg)

*Visitors at the Keramat Iskandar Shah in 2012. Crawfurd found a shelter already standing over the supposed grave in 1822. (Elisa.rolle, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The split gateway in the Sang Nila Utama Garden

![A path through a garden passing between the two halves of a tall red-brick gateway, with a second gateway beyond](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/31/Sang_nila_utama_garden.jpg/1280px-Sang_nila_utama_garden.jpg)

*The Sang Nila Utama Garden on Fort Canning Hill, laid out with split brick gateways in the style of old Javanese temples. (ScribblingGeek, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Spice Garden

![Brick steps leading up through a shady garden of trees and shrubs](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/51/Singapore_Fort_Canning_Park_spice_garden_IMG_9727.jpg/1280px-Singapore_Fort_Canning_Park_spice_garden_IMG_9727.jpg)

*The Spice Garden on Fort Canning Hill, an area that archaeologists think may still hold 14th-century remains. (Bjoertvedt, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Parliament House

![A modern grey government building with a red-tiled roof and a palm tree, with office towers behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5d/Parliament_House%2C_Singapore.jpg/1280px-Parliament_House%2C_Singapore.jpg)

*Parliament House, completed in 1999. The site was excavated between November 1995 and January 1996, before it was built, and produced 14th- to 16th-century remains. (Edwin Lee, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
