More photographs of Pulau Ubin — its shores and crossings, its kampong houses, the village at work, the German Girl Shrine, and Chek Jawa at its eastern tip.

### The island from the water

![Panorama of Pulau Ubin's shore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/22/Singapore_Pulau_Ubin_02.jpg)

*Pulau Ubin from the water: a green shore, low and wooded, ten minutes and a world away from Changi. (Zairon, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![View from the bumboat on the Changi Point to Pulau Ubin crossing, August 2025](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6a/SC3619D_Changi_Point_to_Pulau_Ubin_10-08-2025%281%29.jpg)

*The crossing from Changi Point in August 2025 — the same ten-minute run the bumboats have made for generations. (LN9267, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2025)*

![Leaving the Pulau Ubin jetty by bumboat at night, 2014](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Leaving_Pulau_Ubin_jetty_on_a_bumboat_%2814043336260%29.jpg)

*Leaving the island after dark: the jetty lit up, seen from a departing bumboat in 2014. (Jnzl's Photos, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2014)*

### Kampong houses

![A traditional kampong house on Pulau Ubin](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/45/Pulau_Ubin_kampong_house.jpg)

*A traditional kampong house — timber walls, a pitched roof, and the jungle kept at the garden's edge. (Cmglee, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![A house near the northern coast of Pulau Ubin, 2005](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bb/House_near_the_northern_coast_of_Pulau_Ubin%2C_Singapore_-_20050803.jpg)

*A house near the island's northern coast, photographed in 2005, when the resident population was already down to a few hundred. (BertholdD, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2005)*

### The village at work

![Cycle hire in Ubin village, with the wayang stage behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/ce/Pulau_Ubin_cycle_hire.jpg)

*Bicycles for hire in the village, with the opera stage of the Tua Pek Kong temple behind — one of the last three Chinese wayang stages in Singapore. (Johnragla, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![One of Pulau Ubin's taxis, a Toyota Crown, 2007](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a1/Toyota_Crown_%28S110%29%2C_Pulau_Ubin%2C_Singapore_-_20071102.jpg)

*One of Ubin's own taxis — the island keeps its own vehicle registration, and its taxis are a sight in themselves. (alex.ch, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2007)*

### The German Girl Shrine

![Inside the German Girl Shrine, 2007](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ea/Inside_German_Girl_Shrine_%28525927644%29.jpg)

*Inside the shrine in 2007: the altar of the "datuk maiden", kept by the islanders long after the legend's facts had dissolved. (yuhui, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2007)*

![The German Girl Shrine in 2015, after its rebuilding](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/01/German_girl_shrine_a.k.a._Barbie_doll_shrine_-singapore_%2816664410223%29.jpg)

*The shrine in 2015, the year it was rebuilt in brick and granite — by then the offerings included the Barbie dolls that gave it its second name. (Pascal Vuylsteker, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2015)*

### Chek Jawa

![The Chek Jawa shore, August 2007](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b8/Chek_Jawa_2%2C_Aug_07.JPG)

*Chek Jawa in August 2007, weeks after the boardwalk opened over the shore that reclamation would have buried. (Sengkang, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2007)*

![House No. 1, the Chek Jawa Visitor Centre, 2009](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/01/Chek_Jawa_Visitor_Centre%2C_Pulau_Ubin_%E2%80%93_Singapore_%283817430741%29.jpg)

*House No. 1, the Tudor-style house built in the 1930s for the Chief Surveyor, now the Chek Jawa Visitor Centre. (William Cho, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2009)*

### Island life

![A tame wild boar on Pulau Ubin](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/64/Pulau_Ubin_tame_wild_boar.jpg)

*A wild boar at ease around people — Ubin's boars are part of the island's cast; the best known was Priscilla, a hand-raised boar who lived near Chek Jawa until 2004. (Cmglee, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
