Twelve more pictures of Singapore around the time of the riot, the ports in China the letters went to, and the Straits Times report on the days after.

### The day after: "The Late Riots"

![A full newspaper page of dense columns of small print, with the headings The Late Riots and The Riots](/assets/images/straits-times-overland-journal-1876-12-27-page-3.jpg)

*The Straits Times Overland Journal of the 27th of December 1876, page 3, with "The Late Riots", on the trials, the closed shops and the towkays sent out to the Pluto, and a translation of the placards. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Junks at Swatow

![A harbour crowded with Chinese junks and their tall masts, in front of a long row of low buildings](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d0/Swatow_MET_DP165628.jpg/1280px-Swatow_MET_DP165628.jpg)

*Junks in the harbour at Swatow around 1869. (John Thomson, Metropolitan Museum of Art, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Amoy, around 1870

![A wide panorama of a harbour town with tiled roofs, hills and anchored ships](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/48/Island_of_Koolansoo_and_Amoy_by_Lai_Afong%2C_c1870.jpg/1280px-Island_of_Koolansoo_and_Amoy_by_Lai_Afong%2C_c1870.jpg)

*Amoy and the island of Kulangsu, around 1870. Letters from Singapore's Hokkien workers went home through Amoy. (Lai Afong, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Boat Quay

![A small photograph mounted on an album page, showing a river bend lined with shophouses and boats](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d3/Boat_Quay_aan_de_Singaporerivier_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BK.jpg/1280px-Boat_Quay_aan_de_Singaporerivier_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BK.jpg)

*Boat Quay on the Singapore River, around 1880. The arrested towkays were marched along Boat Quay to Johnston's Pier. (Unknown photographer, Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Collyer Quay

![A small photograph mounted on an album page, showing a long row of waterfront buildings behind a sea wall with boats](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/34/Collier_Quay_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BL.jpg/1280px-Collier_Quay_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BL.jpg)

*Collyer Quay around 1880, where about 600 marines and sailors were paraded after the riot. (Unknown photographer, Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Johnston's Pier

![A long wooden pier with a pavilion reaching out over the harbour, with sailing ships and steamers beyond](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/12/KITLV_-_38150_-_Johnston%27s_pier_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_38150_-_Johnston%27s_pier_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Johnston's Pier around 1890. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Ellenborough Market

![A large market building beside a river crowded with boats and sacks of goods](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e3/Ellenborough_Market_1910.jpg)

*Ellenborough Market in 1910. Rioters attacked the police station at the market in 1876, and the market stopped trading over the weekend that followed. (Arshak Catchatoor Galstaun, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Kling Street

![A small photograph mounted on an album page, showing a long straight street of two-storey shophouses](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/44/Kling_Street_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BM.jpg/1280px-Kling_Street_in_Singapore%2C_RP-F-AA3188-BM.jpg)

*Kling Street, now Chulia Street, in the 1870s, a short walk from Market Street. (Unknown photographer, Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A street seller, around 1880

![A thin Chinese man with a carrying pole and a cooking pot, serving a seated child](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/62/John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_A_Chinese_Soup_Seller_Trading_in_Singapore_%28c_1880%2C_Wellcome_V0037508%29.jpg/1280px-John_Edmund_Taylor%2C_A_Chinese_Soup_Seller_Trading_in_Singapore_%28c_1880%2C_Wellcome_V0037508%29.jpg)
