More photographs of Singapore around the time of the 1914 run on the Chinese Commercial Bank.

### A market, 1914

![A covered market hall with stalls and traders under an iron roof](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f9/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_05_-_Market_Scene.jpg>)

*A market scene from the 1914 souvenir album. (Photo: unknown photographer, from Souvenir of Singapore (1914), public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The harbour, 1914

![Small boats moored in a calm harbour with a low shoreline and a spire in the distance](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e5/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_06_-_Harbour_View.jpg>)

*The harbour, from the same 1914 album. (Photo: unknown photographer, from Souvenir of Singapore (1914), public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Anderson Bridge, 1914

![Anderson Bridge, a steel arch bridge with ornamental stone gateposts, and a clock tower behind](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/59/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_01_-_Anderson_Bridge.jpg>)

*Anderson Bridge, opened in 1910, in the 1914 album. (Photo: unknown photographer, from Souvenir of Singapore (1914), public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Rubber tapping, 1914

![A rubber tapper at work among rows of tall rubber trees](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/99/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_10_-_Rubber_Tapping.jpg>)

*Rubber tapping, from the 1914 album. Rubber was the trade that much of the colony's credit rested on. (Photo: unknown photographer, from Souvenir of Singapore (1914), public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![Raffles Place seen from above around 1910, with a garden in the centre and rickshaws in the street](<https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c7/KITLV_-_79892_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79892_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Raffles_Place%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg>)
