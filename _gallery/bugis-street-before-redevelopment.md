No freely-licensed photographs of old Bugis Street's nightlife survive on Wikimedia Commons — the images that do are held by press archives and photographers under conventional copyright. What's here instead is what replaced it: the site today, and the mall built over the district next door.

*Another view of New Bugis Street's shopping stalls, 2014. (Photo: Bahnfrend / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
