Four more images of the household service economy that came before the amah era — the servants, washermen and door-to-door tradesmen who kept colonial-era Singapore's homes running.

### Ten domestic servants of old Singapore

![Ten domestic servants of various ethnic origins, each posed with an object relating to their task, Singapore, circa 1900](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3d/KITLV_-_29190_-_Ten_domestic_servants_of_various_ethnic_origins%2C_each_with_an_object_relating_tot_their_task%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif/lossy-page1-960px-KITLV_-_29190_-_Ten_domestic_servants_of_various_ethnic_origins%2C_each_with_an_object_relating_tot_their_task%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif.jpg)

*Ten domestic servants of various ethnic origins in Singapore, each posed with an object relating to their household role — a shoe-shiner, a gardener with a lawn mower, and others — decades before Cantonese amahs came to dominate the trade. (Photo: Royal Netherlands Institute of Southeast Asian and Caribbean Studies (KITLV) / Leiden University Library / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### Dhoby Ghaut, before it was an MRT station

![Washing and bleaching field at Dhoby Ghaut, Singapore, circa 1880](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ec/KITLV_-_103979_-_Washing_and_bleaching_field%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1880.tif/lossy-page1-960px-KITLV_-_103979_-_Washing_and_bleaching_field%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1880.tif.jpg)

*The washing and bleaching field at Dhoby Ghaut, circa 1880 — the open ground where itinerant washermen laundered Singapore households' linen, and the source of the name still carried by the MRT station built on the same site today. (Photo: KITLV collection / Leiden University Library / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The tailor who came to your door

![Portrait of an itinerant tailor in Singapore, circa 1900](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/93/KITLV_-_29191_-_Klingalese_tailor_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif/lossy-page1-960px-KITLV_-_29191_-_Klingalese_tailor_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif.jpg)

*An itinerant tailor in Singapore, circa 1900 — one of the door-to-door trades, alongside cooks, gardeners and washermen, that colonial-era households relied on before domestic work consolidated into the single live-in amah role. (Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co. / KITLV collection / Leiden University Library / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The knife sharpeners

![Two itinerant knife sharpeners at work in Singapore, circa 1900](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e4/KITLV_-_50183_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Malaysian_knife_sharpeners_in_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif/lossy-page1-960px-KITLV_-_50183_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Malaysian_knife_sharpeners_in_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif.jpg)

*Itinerant knife sharpeners at work in Singapore, circa 1900 — another of the household trades that, like the amah system after it and the foreign domestic worker scheme after that, existed to do the everyday labour Singapore's homes needed but couldn't supply themselves. (Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co. / KITLV collection / Leiden University Library / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*
