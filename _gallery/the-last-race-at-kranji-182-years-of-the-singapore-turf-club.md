Nine more images of Singapore's three racecourses, from two 1881 sketches of the course off Serangoon Road to Kranji after the last race.

### A stubborn horse at the start, 1881

![A pen and ink sketch of a jockey on a horse that has dug in its forelegs, with two men watching, one holding a starting flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/94/Singapore%3B_a_stubborn_horse_at_the_start_of_Singapore_raceco_Wellcome_V0037529.jpg/1280px-Singapore%3B_a_stubborn_horse_at_the_start_of_Singapore_raceco_Wellcome_V0037529.jpg)

*A stubborn horse at the start of a race at the Singapore racecourse, sketched by John Edmund Taylor in 1881. The caption on the sketch reads "Banyak jahat!", Malay for "very wicked". (John Edmund Taylor, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Clearing the course, 1881

![A pen and ink sketch of a police officer in a pith helmet waving two race-goers off the course](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/91/Singapore%3B_a_police_officer_clearing_two_race-goers_from_the_Wellcome_V0037530.jpg/1280px-Singapore%3B_a_police_officer_clearing_two_race-goers_from_the_Wellcome_V0037530.jpg)

*A police officer clearing two race-goers from the racecourse, sketched by John Edmund Taylor in 1881. (John Edmund Taylor, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Race Course Road

![A white three-storey corner building with an eating house on the ground floor, on Race Course Road](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9d/Race_Course_Road%2C_Aug_06.JPG/1280px-Race_Course_Road%2C_Aug_06.JPG)

*Race Course Road in August 2006. The road ran beside the first racecourse, and it kept the name after the horses left in 1933. (Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Farrer Park

![The entrance of the Farrer Park Swimming Complex, a low building with a pointed roof and a flagpole](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/38/Farrer_Park_Swimming_Complex%2C_Aug_06.JPG/1280px-Farrer_Park_Swimming_Complex%2C_Aug_06.JPG)

*The Farrer Park Swimming Complex in August 2006, at the edge of the old racecourse ground. (Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Turf City

![A white building with a McDonald's and a Hunan restaurant on its ground floor and a sign reading Welcome to Turf City](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a6/Turf_City%2C_Dec_06.JPG/1280px-Turf_City%2C_Dec_06.JPG)

*Turf City in December 2006. After the Turf Club moved to Kranji in 1999, the Bukit Timah racecourse buildings were let out as shops and restaurants until the end of 2023. (Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Under the old stands

![A covered walkway with striped awnings and café tables in front of shops at Turf City](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2e/Turf_City_7%2C_Dec_06.JPG/1280px-Turf_City_7%2C_Dec_06.JPG)

*Shops and café tables at Turf City, December 2006. (Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Turf Club Avenue

![A wide empty road curving between grass verges and trees under a cloudy sky](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7d/Turf_Club_Avenue.JPG/1280px-Turf_Club_Avenue.JPG)

*Turf Club Avenue, the road to the racecourse at Kranji, in April 2006. (Terence Ong, CC BY 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The tower at Kranji

![A tower with the words Singapore Turf Club and a statue of a horse and rider on top, rising above trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0f/Singapore_Turf_Club%2C_2015.jpg/1280px-Singapore_Turf_Club%2C_2015.jpg)

*The Singapore Turf Club's sign tower at Kranji, topped with a horse and rider, in June 2015. (dave ho, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Kranji in 2025

![The domed roof of the Kranji grandstand behind trees and a hillside, with a mural of horses on its wall](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9a/Singapore_Racecourse_09-08-2025%281%29.jpg/1280px-Singapore_Racecourse_09-08-2025%281%29.jpg)
