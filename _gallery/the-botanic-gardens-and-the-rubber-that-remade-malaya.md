A couple more faces from this story, and a look at the Gardens as they are today.

### Tan Chay Yan, Malaya's first rubber planter

![Portrait of Tan Chay Yan](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/70/Tan_Chay_Yan.png)

*Tan Chay Yan, the Malacca planter whose 1896 estate at Bukit Lintang proved rubber could actually make money. Portrait from Song Ong Siang's* One Hundred Years' History of the Chinese in Singapore *(1923 edition). (Public domain, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Ridley, remembered

![Statue of Sir Henry Nicholas Ridley, National Museum of Malaysia](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/ce/Statue_of_Sir_Henry_Nicholas_Ridley_%28DSCF0927%29.jpg)

*A bronze bust of Sir Henry Nicholas Ridley, hands cupped as if still offering seeds, in the collection of Muzium Negara, Malaysia's National Museum in Kuala Lumpur. (Photo: Yu Chu Chin, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
