Ten more images of Raffles Place, where Robinsons traded for most of its history, and of the Orchard Road and Raffles City stores that followed.

### John Little, around 1870

![A row of shophouses with a sign reading John Little & Co. over a covered walkway, on an empty street with a large tree](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/84/KITLV_-_155824_-_Shop_John_Little_%26_Co.%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1870.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_155824_-_Shop_John_Little_%26_Co.%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1870.tif.jpg)

*The shop of John Little & Co. around 1870. John Little was Robinsons' neighbour and rival on the square for more than a century. (Unknown photographer, KITLV collection, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The square around 1910

![A wide square with a fountain in the middle, carriages on the road, and grand colonial bank buildings on both sides](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/22/KITLV_-_79888_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Square_at_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79888_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Square_at_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

*Raffles Place around 1910, with the currency exchange at left and the Hongkong and Shanghai Bank at right. (C. J. Kleingrothe, KITLV collection, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Motor cars on the square

![Rows of parked motor cars in Raffles Place in front of a domed bank building and ornate shop buildings](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7c/KITLV_A723_-_Raffles_Place_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_87929.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_A723_-_Raffles_Place_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_87929.tiff.jpg)

*Raffles Place, with the domed Chartered Bank at the far end and cars parked in the middle of the square, probably in the 1920s or 1930s. (Unknown photographer, KITLV collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A parking lot of a square

![Raffles Place packed with parked cars in rows, surrounded by three- and four-storey buildings with arcades](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/87/Raffles_Place_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404977.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Raffles_Place_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404977.tiff.jpg)

*Raffles Place filled with parked cars, in a photograph of unknown date. (Unknown photographer, KITLV collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Robinsons' profit, 1937

![A full newspaper page of The Straits Times of 23 April 1937, with news reports and a large radio advertisement](/assets/images/straits-times-1937-04-23-page-12.jpg)

*The Straits Times of 23 April 1937, page 12. The short report "Robinson's Profit Last Year", on the store's first profit in seven years, is in the middle of the page. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Raffles Place in 1971

![Raffles Place in 1971 in black and white: a garden with a fountain and a man sitting on its rim, with the domed Chartered Bank behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/01/71-610a_Raffles_Place_Singapore_1971_%2851251176861%29.jpg/1280px-71-610a_Raffles_Place_Singapore_1971_%2851251176861%29.jpg)

*The garden in the middle of Raffles Place in 1971, a year before the fire. (Wilford Peloquin, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Raffles Place in 2002

![Office workers walking along a tree-lined pavement at Raffles Place, with tall office towers behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cd/Raffles_Place_in_front_of_OUB_Centre%2C_Singapore_-_20020829.jpg)

*Raffles Place in front of the OUB Centre in August 2002. The tower stands on the site of the store that burned in 1972. (Timo Sippala, CC BY-SA 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Christmas at The Centrepoint

![The Centrepoint at night covered in Christmas decorations, with a Robinsons sign and a sale banner over the entrance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/39/Centrepoint%2C_Singapore%2C_decorated_for_Christmas_-_20041231.jpg/1280px-Centrepoint%2C_Singapore%2C_decorated_for_Christmas_-_20041231.jpg)

*The Centrepoint on Orchard Road dressed for Christmas on 31 December 2004, with the Robinsons sign over the entrance. (Formulax, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Robinsons at Raffles City

![Inside a shopping centre atrium, the curved balconies of a department store with Robinsons and Marks & Spencer signs](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/47/Robinsons_and_Marks_%26_Spencer%2C_Raffles_City_Shopping_Centre%2C_Singapore_-_20051211.jpg)

*Robinsons and Marks & Spencer in Raffles City Shopping Centre in December 2005. The Raffles City store, opened in 2001, was the last to close, in January 2021. (Terence Ong, CC BY-SA 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Heeren

![The Heeren, a tall glass-and-steel building on Orchard Road, seen from street level behind trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/14/The_Heeren%2C_Dec_05.JPG)

*The Heeren on Orchard Road in December 2005. Robinsons moved its flagship here in 2013 and closed it in December 2020. (Sengkang, copyrighted free use, via Wikimedia Commons)*
