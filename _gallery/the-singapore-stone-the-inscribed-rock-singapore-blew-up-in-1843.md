More drawings, maps, portraits and photographs connected with the Singapore Stone, from the 1820s to today.

### The fragment, close up

![A drawing of a single irregular stone fragment covered in rows of looped letters](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e4/SingaporeStone-drawings-detail.jpg)

*Laidlay's 1848 drawing of the fragment that is now in the National Museum of Singapore, made after he swept charcoal dust into its grooves. (Possibly J. W. Laidlay, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The fragment today

![A slab of weathered sandstone lit from above in a dark museum case, faint letters visible across its face](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/59/Singapore_Stone_-_20121019.jpg/1280px-Singapore_Stone_-_20121019.jpg)

*The Singapore Stone in the Singapore History Gallery of the National Museum of Singapore, photographed in 2012. (Bryn Pinzgauer, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The point on the 1825 survey

![A wider view of a hand-coloured survey map of Singapore, with the river, the town, the hill and the coast, and Rocky point at the river mouth](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9f/Part_of_Singapore_Island_%28British_Library_India_Office_Records%2C_1825%2C_detail%29.jpg/1280px-Part_of_Singapore_Island_%28British_Library_India_Office_Records%2C_1825%2C_detail%29.jpg)

*A wider view of the 1825 survey of Singapore, with the old lines of the settlement, the river and Rocky Point, where the stone stood. (Unknown author, British Library India Office Records, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Singapore from the sea, 1823

![A long pencil sketch of Singapore seen from the sea, with a low line of buildings along the shore and a wooded hill behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Singapore_from_the_Sea_June_1823_-_Lt._Phillip_Jackson.jpg/1280px-Singapore_from_the_Sea_June_1823_-_Lt._Phillip_Jackson.jpg)

*Singapore from the sea, sketched by Lieutenant Philip Jackson on 5 June 1823, while the stone still stood at the river mouth. (Philip Jackson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### John Crawfurd in later life

![A black and white photograph of an elderly man in a dark coat, seated with a book in his hand](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/32/John_Crawfurd.jpg)

*John Crawfurd, who described the stone in 1822, photographed in the 1850s. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### James Prinsep

![A drawn portrait of a young man with curly hair, a high white collar and a dark coat](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/37/James_Prinsep.jpg)

*James Prinsep, the Calcutta scholar who published Bland's copy of the inscription, drawn by his sister Emily before 1840. (Mary Emily Prinsep, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### William John Butterworth

![A drawn full-length portrait of a man in military uniform, holding a plumed hat](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2f/William_J_Butterworth.jpg)

*William John Butterworth, Governor of the Straits Settlements from 1843, who rescued the fragment that had been used as a seat. (Unknown author, 1840, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Indian Museum in 1906

![A black and white photograph of a large colonial museum building with tall arched windows behind a row of trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6e/Indian_Museum%2C_Calcutta_in_1906.jpg/1280px-Indian_Museum%2C_Calcutta_in_1906.jpg)

*The museum in Calcutta where the fragments were kept, from a guidebook of 1906. (W. K. Firminger, with photographs by Bourne and Shepherd and others, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Writing in Kawi

![Several lines of curling Kawi script copied in black ink inside a ruled frame](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/45/Copy_of_a_stone_stele_written_in_Kawi_script.jpg/1280px-Copy_of_a_stone_stele_written_in_Kawi_script.jpg)

*A copy of a stone inscription in the Kawi script, then kept in the museum in Batavia. Kawi is the script most often compared with the Singapore Stone's. (Raden Wijaya, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The National Museum of Singapore

![A white colonial building with a large dome, lit up at dusk, with light trails from traffic in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/30/The_National_Museum_of_Singapore.jpg)

*The National Museum of Singapore, opened in 1887 as the Raffles Library and Museum, which asked for the fragments back in 1918. (William Cho, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
