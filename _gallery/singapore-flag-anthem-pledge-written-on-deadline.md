Five more images from the same compressed, high-pressure years that produced Singapore's flag, anthem, and pledge.

### The flag Singapore replaced

![Flag of Singapore (1952–1959)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/20/Flag_of_Singapore_%281952%E2%80%931959%29.svg)

*The flag of the Colony of Singapore, flown until 1959 — the Union Jack canton and British colonial badge that Toh Chin Chye's committee was tasked with replacing. (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### The state crest, unveiled the same day

![Coat of arms of Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/Coat_of_arms_of_Singapore.svg)

*Singapore's coat of arms, adopted alongside the flag and anthem on 3 December 1959, carries the same crescent-and-five-stars design flanked by a lion and a tiger. (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### The head of state installed that day

![Yusof Ishak, 1933](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c8/Yusof_Ishak%2C_1933.png)

*Yusof bin Ishak, photographed in 1933, decades before his installation as Singapore's first Malayan-born head of state on 3 December 1959 — the same ceremony at which the flag and anthem were unveiled. (Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### Where the anthem was first sung

![Rear entrance of Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/34/Rear_entrance_of_Victoria_Theatre_and_Concert_Hall%2C_Singapore_-_20141101-02.JPG)

*Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall, whose 1958 reopening concert was the first time Zubir Said's commissioned tune was ever performed, a year before it became a national anthem. (Photo: Jacklee / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### Where the symbols were unveiled

![Old Supreme Court Building and City Hall from the Padang, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/70/Old_Supreme_Court_Building_and_City_Hall_from_the_Padang%2C_Singapore_-_20110205.jpg)

*The Padang and the former City Hall, seen here in 2011. City Hall's Municipal Building Chamber hosted the installation ceremony where the flag, anthem, and coat of arms were all made public on the same afternoon. (Photo: Dayou_X / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0)*
