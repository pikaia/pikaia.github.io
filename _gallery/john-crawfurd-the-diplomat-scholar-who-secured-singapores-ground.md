Two more views tied to the treaty and the men it involved.

### The Sultan's compound

![Istana Kampong Glam, present day](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fe/Istana_kampong_glam_malay_heritage_centre_june_2009.jpg)

*Istana Kampong Glam, today home to the Malay Heritage Centre. The present building dates to 1843, built by Sultan Hussein's son on the compound granted to his father — the same Sultan who signed Crawfurd's 1824 treaty. (Photo: Irwan Shah Bin Abdullah, CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The whole island, on paper

![Plan of the British Settlement of Singapore, published 1828, showing the whole island and the town](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Plan_of_the_British_settlement_of_Singapore_published_1828.jpg)

*"Plan of the British Settlement of Singapore," published 1828 in Crawfurd's own account of his embassy — the full island his treaty ceded "in full sovereignty and property," together with the town plan below it. (Capt. Franklin and Lieut. Jackson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
