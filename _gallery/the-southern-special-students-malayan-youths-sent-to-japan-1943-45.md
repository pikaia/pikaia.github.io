More newspaper pages from 1943 to 1945 on the students sent from Malaya to Japan, and photographs of their university in Hiroshima from before the bombing to today.

### The scheme is announced, April 1943

![A full newspaper front page with war headlines and a report in the middle column headed Malai, Sumatra Students To Be Sent To Nippon](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-04-13-page-1.jpg)

*The Syonan Shimbun of 13 April 1943, page 1. The report "Malai, Sumatra Students To Be Sent To Nippon", in the middle of the page, sets out the military administration's plan to send fifteen young men to Japan. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Arrival in Tokyo, July 1943

![A full newspaper front page with large war headlines and a short report headed Nampo Students Arrive In Tokyo](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-07-02-page-1.jpg)

*The Syonan Shimbun of 2 July 1943, page 1. The short Domei report "Nampo Students Arrive In Tokyo", in the right-hand column, describes the first group reaching Tokyo Station on 30 June and going straight to the Imperial Palace. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The trainees left behind, July 1943

![A full newspaper page of text columns and advertisements, with a report headed Ryugakusei Kunrenjo Students Graduate](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-07-26-page-2.jpg)

*The Syonan Shimbun of 26 July 1943, page 2. The report "Ryugakusei Kunrenjo Students Graduate", in the left-hand column, covers the ceremony at Woodsville for the 18 trainees who stayed behind when the fifteen best went to Japan. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### 1,500 students in Japan, August 1943

![A full newspaper page with a large photograph at the top and a column headed 1,500 Toa Students Now Studying In Nippon](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1943-08-07-page-2.jpg)

*The Syonan Shimbun of 7 August 1943, page 2. The column "1,500 Toa Students Now Studying In Nippon" counts 20 students from Malaya, two of them women. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The second group, June 1944

![A full newspaper front page with the masthead The Syonan Shimbun and a short report headed Nampo Students on the left](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1944-06-12-page-1.jpg)

*The Syonan Shimbun of 12 June 1944, page 1. The short report "Nampo Students", on the left, records the arrival in Tokyo of a second group of 89 students, four of them from Malaya. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Home from Waseda, April 1945

![A full newspaper page with a report at the top left headed Malai Students Praise Spirit Of Nippon Youths](/assets/images/syonan-shimbun-1945-04-20-page-2.jpg)

*The Syonan Shimbun of 20 April 1945, page 2. In "Malai Students Praise Spirit Of Nippon Youths", at the top left, Ungku Aziz, Ungku Mohsin and Wan Abdul Hamid describe their two years in Japan. (The Syonan Shimbun, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*
