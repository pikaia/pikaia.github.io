More photographs of the Sikh Police Contingent on duty in Singapore, Penang and Christmas Island, the police buildings they worked from, and the Silat Road gurdwara today.

### On traffic duty

![A bearded Sikh policeman in a turban and shorts standing in a street with arms outstretched, wearing white traffic wings](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Sikh_Singapore_traffic_wings.jpg)

*A member of the Sikh Contingent wearing traffic "wings" on duty in Singapore, around 1930. (Photo: Straits Settlements Police Force, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Riot shields

![Two Sikh policemen, one holding a round woven riot shield and the other a shield on a pole](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b0/SSPF_riot_shield.png)

*Members of the Sikh Contingent with woven riot shields, around 1910. (Photo: Straits Settlements Police Force, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### On Christmas Island

![A long line of Sikh policemen in white uniforms and turbans standing on a field in front of trees and a wooden building on Christmas Island](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/89/Sikh_Contingent_at_Christmas_Island.png)

*The Sikh Contingent detachment on Christmas Island, around 1910. The island was then part of the Straits Settlements, and only members of the Sikh Contingent were routinely stationed there. (Photo: Straits Settlements Police Force, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Guard of honour in Penang

![A long line of Sikh police in uniform forming a guard of honour on a street as a couple in formal dress walk past, in front of a colonnaded building in Penang](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/Sikh_Police_Straits_Settlements_Penang_Court.jpg)

*A guard of honour of the Sikh Police Contingent is inspected at the Penang court in 1935. (Photo: Straits Settlements Police Force, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Police barracks at Tanjong Pagar

![A domed octagonal police station at a road junction with tram lines, with low barrack buildings to one side](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/Politiebureau_van_Tanjong_Pagar_in_Singapore_Police_barracks_%28titel_op_object%29%2C_RP-F-F01140-R.jpg/1280px-Politiebureau_van_Tanjong_Pagar_in_Singapore_Police_barracks_%28titel_op_object%29%2C_RP-F-F01140-R.jpg)

*The police barracks at Tanjong Pagar, from the Tanjong Pagar Dock Company's photograph album, between about 1890 and 1905. (Photo: Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Police Court, around 1900

![A wide street with rickshaws and a bullock cart in front of the grand arcaded Police Court building in Singapore](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b1/KITLV_-_50214_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Police_Court_in_Singapore_-_circa_1900.jpg/1280px-KITLV_-_50214_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Police_Court_in_Singapore_-_circa_1900.jpg)

*The Police Court in Singapore around 1900. (Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co., KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Silat Road gurdwara today

![The white domed façade of the Silat Road Sikh Temple, with cars parked in front and a modern building behind](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/88/Silat_Road_Sikh_Temple%2C_Singapore.jpg/1280px-Silat_Road_Sikh_Temple%2C_Singapore.jpg)

*The Silat Road gurdwara in 2015. The building was rebuilt behind its original façade and reopened in 1995. (Photo: ProjectManhattan, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
