More images connected with Singapore's first election, from the building where the votes were counted to the men who won and the governor who opened the new council.

### The Victoria Theatre in the 1930s

![A postcard of the Victoria Theatre and Victoria Memorial Hall with the clock tower between them](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/Victoria_Theatre_and_Victoria_Memorial_Hall_-_c_1930.jpg)

*The Victoria Theatre and Victoria Memorial Hall on a postcard of about the 1930s. (Photo: unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### C. C. Tan

![C. C. Tan in shirt sleeves and tie standing beside a microphone on a stage](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b6/Tan_Chye_Cheng.png)

*C. C. Tan (Tan Chye Cheng), president of the Progressive Party, who topped the poll in 1948, speaking during the 1951 election campaign. (Photo: Ministry of Information and the Arts Collection, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Sir Franklin Gimson

![Sir Franklin Gimson in academic robes and cap](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/dd/Sir_Franklin_Gimson_in_1951.png)

*Sir Franklin Gimson, Governor of Singapore from 1946 to 1952, in 1951. (Photo: Mike Gorrie, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The results by constituency

![A map of Singapore divided into the four 1948 constituencies, shaded by result](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b5/1948_Singapore_General_Elections_Result_Map.svg/1280px-1948_Singapore_General_Elections_Result_Map.svg.png)

### John Laycock's grave

![The gravestone of John Laycock at Bidadari Garden](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/90/Gravestone_of_John_Laycock%2C_Bidadari_Garden%2C_Singapore_-_20121008.jpg/1280px-Gravestone_of_John_Laycock%2C_Bidadari_Garden%2C_Singapore_-_20121008.jpg)
