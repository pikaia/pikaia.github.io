Twelve more images of Singapore's racecourses, the halls where lotteries were drawn, and Singapore Pools today, from an 1881 sketch to 2024.

### The racecourse in 1881

![A pen and ink sketch of a police officer in a pith helmet waving two race-goers off the course](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/91/Singapore%3B_a_police_officer_clearing_two_race-goers_from_the_Wellcome_V0037530.jpg/1280px-Singapore%3B_a_police_officer_clearing_two_race-goers_from_the_Wellcome_V0037530.jpg)

*A police officer clearing two race-goers from the Singapore racecourse, sketched by John Edmund Taylor in 1881. (John Edmund Taylor, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Gold Cup meeting, 1933

![A full newspaper page of The Sunday Times, with the report Fortunes Changed Hands At Races in the left-hand column below a headline about the Sultan of Johore](/assets/images/straits-times-1933-09-24-page-10.jpg)

*The Sunday Times of 24 September 1933, page 10. The report "Fortunes Changed Hands At Races", on the money spent on the big sweeps, cash sweeps and totes at the Turf Club's Gold Cup meeting, is in the left-hand column. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### A sweep winner and the War Fund, 1941

![A full newspaper page led by the headline Winner Of Big Sweep Helps The War Fund in the top left](/assets/images/straits-times-1941-08-27-page-10.jpg)

*The Straits Times of 27 August 1941, page 10, led by "Winner Of Big Sweep Helps The War Fund". (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Happy World in the 1940s

![A black and white photograph of the entrance arch of the Happy World amusement park, with ticket booths on either side](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d9/Happy_World_Amusement_Park.jpg)

*The entrance to the Happy World amusement park, between Geylang Road and Mountbatten Road, in the 1940s. It was renamed Gay World in 1966, and its stadium hosted a Singapore Sweep draw in May 1969. (Lim Kheng Chye, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Victoria Theatre

![The white front of the Victoria Theatre behind a bronze statue on a pedestal, with palms and office towers around it](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6b/010253_00001_Singapore%2C_Victoria_Theatre.jpg/1280px-010253_00001_Singapore%2C_Victoria_Theatre.jpg)

*The Victoria Theatre in July 2010. The first Toto draw was held here on 9 June 1968. (Matthias Bethke, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The old National Stadium

![The concourse under the stands of the old National Stadium, with a large clock on a pillar](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1f/National_Stadium%2C_Singapore_2%2C_May_07.JPG/1280px-National_Stadium%2C_Singapore_2%2C_May_07.JPG)
