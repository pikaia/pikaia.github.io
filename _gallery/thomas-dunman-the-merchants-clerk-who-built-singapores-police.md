Photographs and prints of Singapore's police buildings and the town in Dunman's time, and of the places connected with him.

### The police station and courts, 1880s

![An old sepia photograph of a wide street with large colonial buildings on both sides](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2f/Central_Police_Station_and_Magistrates_Courts_on_South_Bridge_Road%2C_Singapore_c._1880s.jpg)

*The Central Police Station (left) and the Magistrates' Courts on either side of South Bridge Road in the 1880s, near the site of Dunman's Green. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Police Court

![An old photograph of a large ornate building with a domed tower, with rickshaws and carriages on the road in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f4/The_Police_Court_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404912.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-The_Police_Court_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404912.tiff.jpg)

*The Police Court in Singapore, around the turn of the twentieth century. (Unknown author, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Behind the Police Court

![A faded photograph of a wide open square with shophouses at one side and people and carriages in the distance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e2/Gezicht_op_een_plein_achter_een_gerechtsgebouw_in_Singapore_View_at_back_of_Police_Court._S.pore_%28titel_op_object%29%2C_RP-F-F01197-AH.jpg/1280px-Gezicht_op_een_plein_achter_een_gerechtsgebouw_in_Singapore_View_at_back_of_Police_Court._S.pore_%28titel_op_object%29%2C_RP-F-F01197-AH.jpg)

*The square at the back of the Police Court, photographed between about 1870 and 1900. (Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Central Police Station, 1911

![A black and white postcard of a two-storey building with a clock tower, with people and a rickshaw in the street](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7b/Central_Police_Station_Singapore_1911.jpg)

*The Central Police Station on South Bridge Road in 1911, the force's headquarters from the 1860s to the 1930s. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A street in town, 1860

![An old sepia photograph of a street of two-storey buildings with verandahs, with a horse carriage and a few people](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/83/KITLV_-_29167_-_Shopping_in_Singapore_-_1860.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_29167_-_Shopping_in_Singapore_-_1860.tif.jpg)

*Shops along a street in Singapore in 1860, during Dunman's years in charge of the police. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

![An engraving looking down over a town of low roofs to a harbour full of ships, with hills behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/07/Singapore_January_20_1866_Frederick_Grosse.jpg/1280px-Singapore_January_20_1866_Frederick_Grosse.jpg)

### The jail at Bras Basah

![A photograph of a two-storey arched gatehouse with a guard seated outside](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c5/Main_gate_of_Singapore_Jail%2C_Bras_Basah_%28McNair_1899%2C_Plate_XI%29.jpg/1280px-Main_gate_of_Singapore_Jail%2C_Bras_Basah_%28McNair_1899%2C_Plate_XI%29.jpg)

*The main gate of the convict jail on Bras Basah Road, completed in 1860 with Indian convict labour. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Governor Orfeur Cavenagh

![An engraved portrait of a balding older man with whiskers in a military uniform with medals](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Sir_Orfeur_Cavenagh.jpg)

*Sir Orfeur Cavenagh, Governor of the Straits Settlements from 1859 to 1867, who banned the Muharram procession with Dunman in 1865. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A Sikh policeman

![A photograph of a turbaned policeman in a dark uniform and white leggings standing on the steps of a building](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b0/Sikh_Contingent_member%2C_Straits_Settlements_Police.jpg)

*A member of the Sikh Contingent of the Straits Settlements Police in about 1910. Sikh policemen were first recruited in 1881, ten years after Dunman retired. (Straits Settlements Police Force, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The shore at Tanjong Katong

![A dark old photograph of coconut palms leaning over a beach and the sea](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/Tandjong_Katong%2C_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1407451.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Tandjong_Katong%2C_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1407451.tiff.jpg)

*The shore at Tanjong Katong, where Dunman had his coconut estate and seaside bungalows. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Tanglin Club

![An old photograph of a low colonial building among tall trees beside a road](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/KITLV_-_79904_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Tanglin_Club_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79904_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Tanglin_Club_in_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

*The Tanglin Club in about 1910. Dunman chaired the committee that founded it in 1865. (C. J. Kleingrothe, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
