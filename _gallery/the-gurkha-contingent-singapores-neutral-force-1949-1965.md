More photographs of Gurkha soldiers in Malaya before the war, a Straits Times front page from the December 1950 riots, and the Gurkha Contingent in Singapore in 2005.

### Jungle training, 1941

![Gurkha soldiers kneeling with rifles among trees in the Malayan jungle](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/The_British_Army_in_Malaya_1941_FE239.jpg)

*Men of the 2/9th Gurkha Rifles training in the Malayan jungle, October 1941. (Photo: Lt Palmer, War Office official photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Camouflage instruction, 1941

![A Gurkha soldier with leaves fixed to his hat for camouflage, watched by other soldiers and an officer in the Malayan jungle](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a4/The_British_Army_in_Malaya_1941_FE248.jpg)

*Men of the 2/9th Gurkha Rifles being instructed in camouflage in the Malayan jungle, October 1941. (Photo: Lt Palmer, War Office official photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A Gurkha grave at Kranji

![A white Commonwealth war grave headstone with the crossed-kukri badge and an inscription in Devanagari, for Rifleman Dal Bahadur Rana of the 1st Gurkha Rifles, died 1 July 1942, aged 17](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a5/A_Gurkha_soldier%27s_tombstone_at_Kranji_War_Cemetery%2C_Singapore.jpg)

*The grave of Rifleman Dal Bahadur Rana of the 1st Gurkha Rifles, who died on 1 July 1942 aged 17, at Kranji War Cemetery. (Photo: Soham Banerjee, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The first day of the riots, 12 December 1950
