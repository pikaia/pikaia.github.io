More pictures of the world around Singapore's port in Erédia's time: Portuguese Malacca, the Dutch who came to fight for the straits, the ships that sailed them and the porcelain they carried.

### Matelief's attack on Malacca, 1606

![An engraving of tall Dutch ships offshore and soldiers landing from boats on a palm-lined beach before a walled town](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/12/AMH-6471-KB_Battle_near_Malacca_waged_by_Cornelis_Matelief%2C_1606.jpg/1280px-AMH-6471-KB_Battle_near_Malacca_waged_by_Cornelis_Matelief%2C_1606.jpg)

*Dutch troops land near Malacca during Cornelis Matelief's siege of 1606, the year he allied with Johor, in an engraving published in 1614. (Anonymous, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Malacca besieged

![A tall engraving of a fortified town under bombardment, with ships in the harbour and soldiers fighting in the foreground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Melaka_onder_leiding_van_Cornelis_Matelief_de_Jonge_belegerd%2C_1606%2C_RP-P-1896-A-19368-399.jpg/1280px-Melaka_onder_leiding_van_Cornelis_Matelief_de_Jonge_belegerd%2C_1606%2C_RP-P-1896-A-19368-399.jpg)

*Another view of Matelief's 1606 siege of Malacca, in a book illustration printed in 1683. The siege failed, and Malacca stayed Portuguese until 1641. (Rijksmuseum, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The fortress of Malacca, 1630s

![A hand-coloured plan of a walled fortress on a river mouth, with a church and buildings inside the walls](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8f/Malacca_1630.jpg)

*The Portuguese fortress of Malacca, from António Bocarro's book of the forts and towns of Portuguese India of the 1630s. (António Bocarro, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Malacca from the sea

![An engraving of a coastal town with a fort and a church on a hill, ships at anchor in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a7/AMH-8010-KB_View_of_Malacca.jpg/1280px-AMH-8010-KB_View_of_Malacca.jpg)

*Malacca seen from the sea in a Dutch engraving of about 1753, by which time the town had been Dutch for a century. (Jakob van der Schley and Pieter de Hondt, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The last gate of A Famosa

![A sepia photograph of a stone gateway with an arched opening, standing alone among trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/76/KITLV_-_101099_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Porta_de_Santiago_of_the_destroyed_fortress_A_Famosa_at_Malacca_%28Melaka%29_in_Malaysia_-_circa_1905.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_101099_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Porta_de_Santiago_of_the_destroyed_fortress_A_Famosa_at_Malacca_%28Melaka%29_in_Malaysia_-_circa_1905.tif.jpg)

*The Porta de Santiago, the only surviving gate of the Portuguese fortress of Malacca, photographed around 1905. (C. J. Kleingrothe, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A jong, drawn by Erédia

![A line drawing of a large sailing ship with two masts, a high stern and a long bowsprit](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a2/Figure_2_Jong_by_Manuel_Godinho_de_Eredia_circa_1613.jpg)

*A jong, one of the big trading ships of the region, drawn by Erédia in about 1613. (Manuel Godinho de Erédia, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Portuguese carracks

![A painting of several large Portuguese sailing ships with patterned sails off a rocky coast with a castle](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/40/Portuguese_Carracks_off_a_Rocky_Coast.jpg/1280px-Portuguese_Carracks_off_a_Rocky_Coast.jpg)

*Portuguese carracks in a painting of about 1540, ships of the type of the Santa Catarina, taken off Singapore in 1603. (Unknown artist, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Another kraak dish

![A blue-and-white porcelain dish with a scene of birds in the centre and a border of panels, displayed on a wall](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c3/Kraak_porcelain_dish_-_Ming_dynasty_-_Guimet_museum_-_Accesion_number_MA_4801.jpg/1280px-Kraak_porcelain_dish_-_Ming_dynasty_-_Guimet_museum_-_Accesion_number_MA_4801.jpg)

*A kraak porcelain dish of about 1580 to 1600, from the reign of the Wanli emperor, in the Musée Guimet in Paris. (Faqscl, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Johor Lama today

![A low timber museum building with a tiled roof and a banner, at the top of a short flight of steps](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/70/Kota_Johor_Lama_Museum.JPG/1280px-Kota_Johor_Lama_Museum.JPG)
