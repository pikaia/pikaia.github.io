### Tan Kah Kee and Sun Yat-sen, 1911

### The Chinese High School

![A sepia view from the Chinese High School's bell tower over trees and a path on the campus in the 1950s](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/ff/View_from_the_Bell_tower_in_the_50s.JPG)
