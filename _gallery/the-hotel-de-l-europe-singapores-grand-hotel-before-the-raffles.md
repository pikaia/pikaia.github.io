Eight more pictures of the Hotel de l'Europe — the old hotel across the Esplanade, High Street in colour, the Bird Cage dining room, the Padang it faced, and the buildings standing on its site today.

### Across the Esplanade, about 1890

![A white hotel with a gabled roof among trees behind a low wall, seen across a grassy open space, with a horse carriage at its gate](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/72/KITLV_-_377459_-_Hotel_d%27Europe_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_377459_-_Hotel_d%27Europe_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*The old Hotel de l'Europe from across the Esplanade, about 1890 — its name is on the gable, a carriage waits at the gate, and Fort Canning rises behind. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Carriages along the front

![A wide view across a green to a colonnaded hotel with its name on the pediment, carriages waiting along the road in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9b/Koetsen_voor_het_Hotel_de_l%27Europe_in_Singapore%2C_SFA002022355.jpg/1280px-Koetsen_voor_het_Hotel_de_l%27Europe_in_Singapore%2C_SFA002022355.jpg)

*The hotel from the Esplanade, its name across the pediment and carriages — the hotel kept its own, for the run to the docks — lined up along the front. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### High Street in colour

![A colour photochrom of a wide street with carriages and shophouses, a low hotel building under trees on the right](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/26/Singapore._High_Street_with_Hotel_Europe_LCCN2017657652.jpg)

*High Street with the Hotel Europe, in a photochrom of the 1890s — the hotel is on the right, and the green rise ahead is Fort Canning. The print's own caption is at lower left. (Photoglob Co., Library of Congress, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The hotel in its grounds

![A grand white building with tall windows and a tiled roof among trees, a man in white standing on the drive](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/91/KITLV_A1225_-_Hotel_d_%27Europe_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_142885.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_A1225_-_Hotel_d_%27Europe_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_142885.tiff.jpg)

*The Hotel d'Europe from its own grounds. (Unknown photographer, KITLV, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Bird Cage dining room

![A vast dining hall with tall columns, a gallery above, chandeliers, wall fans and rows of round tables with white cloths](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/96/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_02_-_Dining_Room.jpg)

*The dining room — the "Bird Cage" — as the hotel pictured it in its own souvenir album of 1914: some thousand square feet, almost free of columns, with the finest view east of Suez. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Padang in 1914

![A wide green with a statue at its centre, a clock tower on the left and a large arcaded hotel building on the right](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/41/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_12_-_Singapore_Cricket_Club%2C_Town_Hall%2C_Supreme_Court_and_Europe_Hotel.jpg)

*The hotel's front garden, from the same album: the Padang in 1914, with Raffles' statue in the middle, the Town Hall's clock tower on the left and the rebuilt Europe Hotel on the right. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Supreme Court on the site

![A grey neoclassical building with tall Corinthian columns and a green copper dome, seen from across the road](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/Former_Supreme_Court_Singapore.jpg/1280px-Former_Supreme_Court_Singapore.jpg)

*The former Supreme Court, opened in 1939 on the hotel's footprint — the building a traveller following Kipling's advice would have to sleep in today. (Philip Nalangan, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The site today

![The domed former Supreme Court building behind street trees, with modern towers behind it](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5e/National_Gallery_Singapore_1.jpg/1280px-National_Gallery_Singapore_1.jpg)

*The former Supreme Court today, now the Supreme Court wing of National Gallery Singapore — the hotel's ground, under a dome. (Uzoma Ozurumba, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
