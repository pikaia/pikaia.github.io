Paintings, prints and charts of Singapore in Thomson's time, and of his later life in New Zealand.

### Government Hill, 1846

![A watercolour of a green hill with a house on top, a red earth cutting and a row of buildings along a road and waterfront below](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/02/Charles_Dyce%2C_Government_Hill_from_the_New_Harbour_Road%2C_Singapore%2C_1846%2C_Watercolour_%26_Ink_on_paper%2C_27.2_x_44.8_cm.png/1280px-Charles_Dyce%2C_Government_Hill_from_the_New_Harbour_Road%2C_Singapore%2C_1846%2C_Watercolour_%26_Ink_on_paper%2C_27.2_x_44.8_cm.png)

*Government Hill (now Fort Canning) from the New Harbour Road, painted by a contemporary of Thomson's in Singapore. (Charles Andrew Dyce, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The harbour and town

![A sepia print of a wide bay with palm trees and figures on the shore in front and low buildings and hills along the far side](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a1/Atlas_pittoresque_pl_134.jpg/1280px-Atlas_pittoresque_pl_134.jpg)

*The harbour and town of Singapore in a plate from a French voyage's Atlas pittoresque, published in 1846. (Adolphe Jean-Baptiste Bayot, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A Chinese junk in the harbour

![A sepia print of a large Chinese junk with patterned sails and a high stern, with small boats around it and a sailing ship behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a7/Atlas_pittoresque_pl_136.jpg/1280px-Atlas_pittoresque_pl_136.jpg)

*A Chinese junk at Singapore, from the same atlas, 1846. Thomson described the roadstead as covered with European, Chinese and Malay vessels. (Louis Le Breton, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Horsburgh Lighthouse, October 1851

![A black and white lithograph of a lighthouse on a rock at sea with sailing ships nearby, with a row of place names printed along the bottom](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e2/Horsburgh_Lighthouse_by_John_T_Thomson_1851.jpg/1280px-Horsburgh_Lighthouse_by_John_T_Thomson_1851.jpg)

*Horsburgh Lighthouse on Pedra Branca in the month it was first lit, from Thomson's drawing. (John Turnbull Thomson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### His chart of the coast to Tioman, 1849

![A tall, pale nautical chart of a long coastline running north, with islands offshore and two compass roses](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7a/Singapore_to_Tioman_I._By_Mr._J._T._Thomson%2C_Government_Surveyor%2C_1849.png/1280px-Singapore_to_Tioman_I._By_Mr._J._T._Thomson%2C_Government_Surveyor%2C_1849.png)

*The east coast of the Malay Peninsula from Singapore to Pulau Tioman, "by Mr. J. T. Thomson, Government Surveyor, 1849". (J. T. Thomson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The avenue to Whampoa's gardens

![An engraving of a straight road lined with tall palm trees leading towards a gate](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fb/Avenue_to_Whampoa%27s_Gardens_%281886%29.png)

*The avenue to the gardens of Thomson's friend Whampoa, in an engraving published in 1886. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Dalhousie Obelisk

![A colour photograph of a white obelisk on a stepped base in a garden, with a large pale building behind it](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/29/EmpressPlaceBuilding-DalhousieObelisk-Singapore-1965.jpg)

*The Dalhousie Obelisk, put up in 1850 and credited to Thomson, in front of the Empress Place Building in 1965. (Steve Swayne, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Thomson Road, 1965

![A colour photograph of a two-storey shophouse with a red fire hydrant in front and a cyclist passing on the road](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/da/Thomson_Road_Singapore_January_1965_01.jpg/1280px-Thomson_Road_Singapore_January_1965_01.jpg)

*Shophouses on Thomson Road in January 1965. (Don Christie, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### His house in Dunedin

![An oval watercolour of a white house on a green slope with a valley and hills behind and two small figures in the foreground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/72/One_of_John_Turnbull_Thomson%27s_aquarelles._1860s.jpg)

*Rockyside in Caversham, Dunedin, where Thomson lived for many years, in his own watercolour from the 1860s. (John Turnbull Thomson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
