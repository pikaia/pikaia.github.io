Photographs of the Malayan campaign Tani Yutaka worked in, the places in his story and the Japanese Cemetery Park in Singapore.

### Major Fujiwara Iwaichi

![A black and white portrait of a Japanese officer in a field cap with a star badge](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Fujiwara_Iwaichi.jpg)

*Fujiwara Iwaichi, who led the F Kikan intelligence unit that Tani worked with during the Malayan campaign. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### General Yamashita in Malaya

![Japanese officers in helmets and caps crouch around a map spread on the ground among trees](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bf/Tomoyuki_yamashita_malaya.png)

*General Yamashita Tomoyuki, centre, with his officers during the invasion of Malaya, December 1941. He too was called the Tiger of Malaya. (Photographer unknown, Japan War History Office, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Bicycle troops crossing a river

![Japanese soldiers wade across a wide river carrying bicycles, beside a broken steel bridge](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9a/Japanese_bicycle_troops_crossing_a_river.%2C_Malayan_campaign%2C_1942.jpg/1280px-Japanese_bicycle_troops_crossing_a_river.%2C_Malayan_campaign%2C_1942.jpg)

*Japanese bicycle troops wade across a river beside a bridge destroyed by the retreating British, January 1942. (Kōyō Kageyama, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A destroyed bridge at Labis

![Japanese troops with bicycles file across a temporary bridge beside the twisted wreck of a steel bridge](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4f/Destroyed_bridge_and_temporary_bridge_in_Labis%2C_January_22_1942.jpg/1280px-Destroyed_bridge_and_temporary_bridge_in_Labis%2C_January_22_1942.jpg)

*Japanese troops cross a temporary bridge beside a bridge blown up by the British at Labis, Johor, 22 January 1942. (Asahi Shimbun, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A makeshift crossing

![Japanese soldiers balance across a river on a bridge of logs, holding each other up, with jungle behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/13/Japanese_soldiers_crossing_a_river_on_a_temporary_bridge.%2C_Malayan_campaign%2C_1942.jpg/1280px-Japanese_soldiers_crossing_a_river_on_a_temporary_bridge.%2C_Malayan_campaign%2C_1942.jpg)

*Japanese soldiers cross a river on a temporary bridge of logs during the Malayan campaign, January 1942. (Kōyō Kageyama, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Kota Bharu, 1941

![A riverbank lined with coconut palms and wooden sheds, with small boats pulled up on the shore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/39/Kota_Bahru_-_Kelantan_1941.jpg)

*The riverside at Kota Bharu, Kelantan, in 1941. Marai no Tora was set in the town. (Australian War Memorial, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Japanese troops in Johor

![A page from a Japanese wartime publication showing soldiers crouching on railway tracks beside a steam locomotive](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Japanese_troops_at_Johore.jpg)

*Japanese troops at the railway in Johor Bahru in a wartime Japanese publication. Tani was taken to a field hospital in the town on 1 February 1942. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The campaign map

![Two maps of the Malay Peninsula side by side, showing British positions in December 1941 and the Japanese advance to Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bf/Pacific_War_-_Malaya_1941-42_-_Map.jpg)

*Maps of the Malayan campaign: British dispositions on 7 December 1941, and the Japanese advance from the Thai border to Singapore. (United States Military Academy, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Japanese residents leave Singapore

![Men in white suits and hats and women in dresses walk past customs barriers at a wharf](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/74/Japanese_nationals_arriving_at_custom_barriers_while_leaving_Singapore%2C_1942_%2823918242273%29.jpg/1280px-Japanese_nationals_arriving_at_custom_barriers_while_leaving_Singapore%2C_1942_%2823918242273%29.jpg)

*Japanese nationals pass the customs barriers as some six hundred of them leave Singapore on an evacuation ship before the war. (National Museum of the U.S. Navy, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
