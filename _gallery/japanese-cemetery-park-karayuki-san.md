Four more views of the Japanese Cemetery Park — the men and monuments that mark its later chapters, and the building that still tends its grounds.

### Yamamoto Otokichi, the first Japanese settler

![Yamamoto Otokichi memorial at the Japanese Cemetery Park](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4f/Yamamoto_Otokichi_memorial%2C_Japanese_Cemetery_Park%2C_Singapore_-_20070526.jpg)

*The memorial to Yamamoto Otokichi, a shipwrecked sailor regarded as the first Japanese person to settle in Singapore, reinterred here in the 19th century. (Photo: Aldwin Teo / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### Field Marshal Terauchi's grave

![Hisaichi Terauchi memorial at the Japanese Cemetery Park](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/59/Hisaichi_Terauchi_memorial%2C_Japanese_Cemetery_Park%2C_Singapore_-_20070526.jpg)

*The tombstone of Field Marshal Count Hisaichi Terauchi, commander of Japan's Southern Expeditionary Army, who died in detention in 1946 and was buried here by fellow Japanese POWs. (Photo: Aldwin Teo / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### The Saiyuji prayer hall

![Saiyuji prayer hall at the Japanese Cemetery Park](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bf/Prayer_hall%2C_Japanese_Cemetery_Park%2C_Singapore_-_20070526.jpg)

*The Saiyuji Buddhist prayer hall, built on the cemetery grounds in 1911 and still standing today. (Photo: Aldwin Teo / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### The caretaker's quarters

![Caretaker's quarters at the Japanese Cemetery Park](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a4/Caretaker%27s_quarters%2C_Japanese_Cemetery_Park%2C_Singapore.jpg)

*The caretaker's quarters on-site — the cemetery has had a resident caretaker for most of its history and is still actively maintained by the Japanese Association, Singapore. (Photo: Marcus Lim / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*
