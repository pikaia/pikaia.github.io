Three more items from the mutiny and its aftermath. Photographs of the event itself are scarce, so one of them is a newspaper page.

### The French cruiser Montcalm

![The French armoured cruiser Montcalm at sea, 1902](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6d/Cruiser_Montcalm_-_Page%27s_Magazine_1902.png/1280px-Cruiser_Montcalm_-_Page%27s_Magazine_1902.png)

*The French armoured cruiser Montcalm, one of the ships that landed men in Singapore in February 1915, in an illustration from Page's Magazine, 1902. (Page's Magazine, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The report of the executions

![Page 7 of The Straits Times, 26 March 1915, with the report headed The Mutiny in the right-hand column](/assets/images/straits-times-1915-03-26-page-7.jpg)

*Page 7 of The Straits Times, 26 March 1915. The right-hand column, headed "The Mutiny," reports the executions of 25 March; its headline uses the language of the period. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Convicted men at Outram Road

![A line of men in white against the wall of Outram Road Prison, captioned as the convicted mutineers before their execution](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ab/1915_Singapore_Mutiny.jpg)

*A line of men against the prison wall at Outram Road, captioned in a 1995 collection of documents as the convicted mutineers before their execution. The photograph is grainy and its exact date is not established. (From Tilak Raj Sareen, Secret Documents on Singapore Mutiny 1915, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
