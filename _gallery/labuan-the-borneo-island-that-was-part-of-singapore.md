More pictures of Labuan, from the treaty of 1846 and the old maps and charts to the war of 1942 to 1945 and the memorials that mark it today.

### The cession, 1846

![An engraving of British naval officers seated with Malay nobles in a crowded hall, with armed men standing around the walls](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/31/Treaty_of_Labuan1846.jpg)

*Captain Rodney Mundy negotiates the cession of Labuan with the court of Brunei, December 1846, from his own published journal. (Rodney Mundy, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Labuan in 1888

![A printed map of Labuan island and the nearby coast of Brunei Bay, with the town of Victoria marked on the south side of the island](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fb/Map_of_Labuan_%281888%29.jpg)

*A map of Labuan and Brunei Bay published in 1888, two years before the North Borneo Company took over the island. (Unknown cartographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Victoria Harbour, 1913

![A detailed nautical chart of a harbour with depth soundings, the outline of a town and the coastline of a bay](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1c/Admiralty_Chart_No_947_Victoria_Harbour%2C_Published_1913.jpg/1280px-Admiralty_Chart_No_947_Victoria_Harbour%2C_Published_1913.jpg)

*The Admiralty chart of Victoria Harbour, Labuan, published in 1913, the year after the island became a separate settlement of the Straits Settlements. (United Kingdom Hydrographic Office, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Government Offices

![A sepia photograph of a long, low colonial building with a pitched roof behind an open lawn, with palm trees in the distance](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2f/Labuan_Government_Offices.jpg)

*The Government Offices on Labuan, from a British colonial photograph album (date unknown). (Unknown author, The National Archives UK, Open Government Licence, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Labuan Bill, 1907

![A full newspaper page of closely set columns of short news items, with a few headlines and advertisements](/assets/images/straits-times-1907-01-02-page-6.jpg)

*The Straits Times of 2 January 1907, page 6. Among its short notes is one reporting that the Labuan Bill had passed all its stages in the Legislative Council that afternoon. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### The Prince of Wales's visit, 1922

![A grey granite memorial stone engraved with the record of a royal visit, standing in a paved square with a yellow colonial building behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d7/Labuan_Malaysia_Memorial-Stone-Visit-Prince-of-Wales-01.jpg/1280px-Labuan_Malaysia_Memorial-Stone-Visit-Prince-of-Wales-01.jpg)

*The stone recording the Prince of Wales's landing from HMS Renown on 17 May 1922, now outside the Labuan Museum. (CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Japanese landing, January 1942

![A black-and-white photograph of a crowd of Japanese soldiers in boats and on a jetty, with a colonial house on the shore behind them](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/df/Japanese_landing_off_the_west_coast_of_British_North_Borneo%2C_Labuan.jpg)

*Japanese troops landing on the west coast of British North Borneo in January 1942. (Unknown author, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### "Maeda Island"

![A bronze plaque with raised capital letters recording a Japanese general's death in an air crash and the renaming of Labuan as Maida Island](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d5/Labuan_Malaysia_Memorial-Stone-GENERAL-MAIDA-01.jpg/1280px-Labuan_Malaysia_Memorial-Stone-GENERAL-MAIDA-01.jpg)

*The plaque recording General Maeda's death on 5 September 1942 and the renaming of Labuan as "Maida Island" on 9 December 1942. (CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### On the way to surrender, 10 September 1945

![A black-and-white photograph of a Japanese general in a peaked cap seated in a jeep, surrounded by Australian soldiers](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5d/MasaoBaba_Labuan01.jpg)

*Lieutenant-General Masao Baba in a jeep at the Labuan airstrip on his way to sign the surrender. (John Thomas Harrison, Australian War Memorial, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Surrender Point today

![A blue and white arched memorial gateway over a paved path, with a plaque on a plinth inside and trees behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5b/Labuan_Malaysia_Surrender-Point-Memorial-01.jpg/1280px-Labuan_Malaysia_Surrender-Point-Memorial-01.jpg)

*The Surrender Point memorial at Layang-Layangan, where the Japanese 37th Army surrendered on 10 September 1945. (CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
