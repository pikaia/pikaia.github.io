Two more views of the island the Straits Trading Company's smelter stood on, from the years when it was still a Malay fishing village and an industrial site side by side.

### The Malay village, circa 1900

![Stilt houses of the Malay village on Pulau Brani, circa 1900](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/50/KITLV_-_105809_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Malay_village_on_the_island_of_Pulau_Brani_near_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_105809_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Malay_village_on_the_island_of_Pulau_Brani_near_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif.jpg)

*"Malay Village, Pulo Brani," circa 1900: rows of stilt houses over the water, with the smelter's home island, and Singapore's harbour, behind. (Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co., KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Across the water, circa 1910

![Pulau Brani seen across the water through trees, circa 1910](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ad/KITLV_-_79928_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Tin_industry_at_the_island_of_Pulau_Brani%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_79928_-_Kleingrothe%2C_C.J._-_Medan_-_Tin_industry_at_the_island_of_Pulau_Brani%2C_Singapore_-_circa_1910.tif.jpg)

*Catalogued as "Tin industry at the island of Pulau Brani," circa 1910, this view looks across the water from Singapore's mainland; the smelter's smoke rises from the wooded hill at the far right. (Photo: Charles J. Kleingrothe, KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
