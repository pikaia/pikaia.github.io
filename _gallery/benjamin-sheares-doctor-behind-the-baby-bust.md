Five more images from Benjamin Sheares' life — the young academic, the family man, the president, and the newspaper front page that marked the day he died in office.

### The young obstetrician at the University of Malaya

![Benjamin Sheares at the University of Malaya in Singapore, 1951](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3f/Benjamin_Sheares_at_the_University_of_Malaya_in_Singapore%2C_1951.jpg)

*Benjamin Sheares at the University of Malaya in Singapore (present-day NUS), 19 April 1951, the year after he was appointed the first local professor of obstetrics and gynaecology. (Photo: Singapore Press Holdings / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### Sheares and Yeo Seh Geok, 1939

![Benjamin Sheares and Yeo Seh Geok, 1939](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/09/Benjamin_Sheares_and_Yeo_Seh_Geok%2C_1939.jpg)

*A studio photograph of Benjamin Sheares and Yeo Seh Geok, circa 1939. Yeo, a midwife who trained at Kandang Kerbau Hospital, married Sheares that year and later became Singapore's first First Lady. (Photo: Benjamin Henry Sheares / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The Istana, seen during his presidency

![View of the Istana from the Mandarin Hotel's rotating roof restaurant, 1973-74](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b8/Singapore-Mandarin_Hotel-Istana-1973-74-WUS08140.jpg)

*The Istana grounds seen from the Mandarin Hotel's rotating roof restaurant on Orchard Road, 1973-74 — squarely within Sheares' 1971-1981 term as president. (Photo: Rainer Halama / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### The family portrait, 1946

![Studio family portrait of Benjamin Sheares, Yeo Seh Geok, and their two children](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/ad/Benjamin_Sheares_and_his_family.jpg)

*A studio family portrait of Dr Benjamin Sheares with his wife Yeo Seh Geok and their children Constance and Joseph, circa 1946 — decades before he became the public face of a campaign urging other families to stay smaller than his own. (Photo: Benjamin Sheares / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The morning the newspapers carried his death

![Front page of The Straits Times, 13 May 1981](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b5/ST13May1981.jpg)

*The front page of The Straits Times on 13 May 1981, reporting the death of President Benjamin Sheares in office the day before. (Photo: The Straits Times / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*
