More views of the bank buildings that stood around Raffles Place and the waterfront by the turn of the 20th century.

### The Chartered Bank, Raffles Place

![The Chartered Bank building on Raffles Place, a domed commercial building facing a street crowded with parked cars and rickshaws](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/59/The_Chartered_Bank_aan_Raffles_Place_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_104795.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-The_Chartered_Bank_aan_Raffles_Place_te_Singapore%2C_KITLV_104795.tiff.jpg)

*The Chartered Bank on Raffles Place in the early 20th century. The bank opened in Singapore in 1859. (Photo: unknown photographer, KITLV/Leiden University Library, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Hongkong and Shanghai Bank, around 1900

![The Hongkong and Shanghai Bank building seen across the water from Johnston's Pier, with boats in the foreground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/81/Photographic_Views_of_Singapore_Plate_05_Hongkong_and_Shanghai_Bank.jpg)

*The Hongkong and Shanghai Bank building seen from the water, before 1900, from the book Photographic Views of Singapore. (Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co., public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Johnston's Pier and the bank
