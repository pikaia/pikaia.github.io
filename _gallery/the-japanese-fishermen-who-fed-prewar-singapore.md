Photographs of the Japanese fishing fleet itself are scarce, so this gallery pairs the working waterfront of the period with a newspaper page and the wartime story of the Krait.

### The licence controversy in the newspaper

![Page 10 of The Straits Times, 3 October 1939, with the article Japanese in Singapore: Fishing Licences Controversy in the top right corner](/assets/images/straits-times-1939-10-03-page-10.jpg)
