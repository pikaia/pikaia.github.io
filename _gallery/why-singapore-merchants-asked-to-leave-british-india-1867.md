### Seah Eu Chin

![Portrait of Seah Eu Chin, an elderly Chinese man with a white moustache, wearing a cap](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/64/Seah_Eu_Chin.jpg/1280px-Seah_Eu_Chin.jpg)

*Seah Eu Chin, the only Chinese merchant recorded at the Town Hall meeting of December 1863 in support of the transfer. (Song Ong Siang, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Whampoa

![Portrait of Hoo Ah Kay, known as Whampoa, a Chinese man in a dark robe wearing a cap and a medal](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7b/The_Hon._Hoh-Ah-Kay_Whampoa%2C_C.M.G.%2C_M.L.C.%2C_and_Consul_for_Wellcome_V0037527.jpg/1280px-The_Hon._Hoh-Ah-Kay_Whampoa%2C_C.M.G.%2C_M.L.C.%2C_and_Consul_for_Wellcome_V0037527.jpg)

*Hoo Ah Kay, known as Whampoa, later the first Chinese member of the Straits Settlements Legislative Council, photographed by J. Taylor in about 1881. (Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Governor Cavenagh

![Engraved portrait of Sir Orfeur Cavenagh, a bald man in uniform with medals](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/00/Sir_Orfeur_Cavenagh.jpg)

*Sir Orfeur Cavenagh, the last governor of the Straits under India, from 1859 to 1867, who opposed Calcutta's port dues and income tax. (Unknown artist, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### John Crawfurd

![Photographic portrait of John Crawfurd, an elderly white-haired man seated with a book](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/32/John_Crawfurd.jpg)

*John Crawfurd, a former Resident of Singapore, who took up the merchants' case against the rupee in London. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Admiral Keppel

![Photograph of Admiral of the Fleet Sir Henry Keppel standing in full dress uniform with medals](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/95/Admiral_of_the_Fleet_Sir_Henry_Keppel.jpg)

*Admiral Sir Henry Keppel, who took an ordinary chair rather than a seat on the dais at the 1867 ceremony, photographed in 1896. (A. Debenham, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
