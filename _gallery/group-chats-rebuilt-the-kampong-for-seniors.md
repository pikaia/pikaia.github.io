Five more images tracing the world the "kampong spirit" in this post's headline actually refers to — village life on the water and in the villages themselves, and the public housing that eventually replaced it.

### Kampong Bugis, on the water

![Kampong Buggis, the Buginese district of Singapore, circa 1900](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e7/KITLV_-_105810_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Kampong_Buggis%2C_the_Buginese_district_of_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif/lossy-page1-960px-KITLV_-_105810_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Kampong_Buggis%2C_the_Buginese_district_of_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif.jpg)

*Kampong Bugis, the Buginese district of Singapore, photographed by G.R. Lambert & Co. around 1900. (Photo: Leiden University Library, KITLV collection / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### Kampong Baru, a village within the city

![Kampong Baru at Singapore, circa 1890](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/91/KITLV_-_105811_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Kampong_Baru_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-960px-KITLV_-_105811_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Kampong_Baru_at_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Kampong Baru at Singapore, photographed by G.R. Lambert & Co. around 1890 — a cluster of attap-roofed houses of exactly the kind that once lined the island's rivers and coastline. (Photo: Leiden University Library, KITLV collection / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### A path through the village

![Malay Dwelling House, Singapore, hand-tinted postcard, early 20th century](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/Malay_Dwelling_House_Singapore.%2C_KITLV_1404990.tiff/lossy-page1-960px-Malay_Dwelling_House_Singapore.%2C_KITLV_1404990.tiff.jpg)

*A hand-tinted postcard titled "Malay Dwelling House Singapore," published sometime between 1906 and 1930, showing residents walking the sandy path between houses — the kind of everyday, face-to-face contact that gave kampong life its reputation for closeness. (Photo: Leiden University Library, KITLV collection / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0)*

### A kampong on the eve of redevelopment

![A kampong in Braddell Hill, Singapore, circa 1964](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/81/Kampong_in_Braddell_Hill_Singapore_about_1964.jpg)

*A kampong in Braddell Hill, Singapore, photographed around 1964 — not long before villages like this across the island were cleared to make way for public housing. (Photo: Don Christie / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### What replaced it

![Public housing under construction in Singapore, 1973-74, with older low-rise structures still visible at the base of the new blocks](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/87/Singapore-Public_Housing-1973-74-WUS08215.jpg/960px-Singapore-Public_Housing-1973-74-WUS08215.jpg)

*New HDB blocks rising in Singapore, 1973-74, with older low-rise kampong-style structures still standing at their base — the physical moment of the transition this post's "kampong spirit" metaphor looks back on. (Photo: Rainer Halama / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
