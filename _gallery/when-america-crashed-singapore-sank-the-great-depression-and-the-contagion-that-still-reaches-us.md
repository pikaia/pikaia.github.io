### Wall Street, 29 October 1929

![Wall Street seen from above, packed with people and motor cars outside the Stock Exchange](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e1/Crowd_outside_nyse.jpg)

### Free passages to China, 1932

![Page 11 of The Straits Times, 28 May 1932, with the banner headline International Conference on China Proposed and, in the third column, a report headed Free Passages to China](/assets/images/straits-times-1932-05-28-page-11.jpg)

*Page 11 of The Straits Times, 28 May 1932. "Free Passages to China," in the third column, reports that three thousand unemployed Chinese miners had left Perak in a single week. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Tapping rubber, on a stamp

![A blue six-cent Federation of Malaya postage stamp showing two rubber tappers at work among the trees, with the Malayan flag](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c3/1961tappingrubber.png)

*A six-cent Federation of Malaya stamp of 1961 showing rubber tappers. Rubber was still Malaya's largest export three decades after the Depression. (Malayan government, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
