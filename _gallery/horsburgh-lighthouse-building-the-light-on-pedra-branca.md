Paintings, charts, photographs and newspaper pages from the history of Horsburgh Lighthouse and Pedra Branca.

### Thomson's painting, 1851

![A soft sepia painting of the lighthouse on its rock with sailing ships around it](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/82/HorsburghLighthouse-JTThomson-1851-02.jpg)

*Horsburgh Lighthouse just after its completion in 1851, painted by its architect. (John Turnbull Thomson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A storm off Pedra Branca

![A painting of heavy green seas breaking over a dark rock under storm clouds](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/23/PedraBranca-TRobertson-1882.jpg)

*Pedra Branca in rough seas, painted in 1882 from an 1850 sketch by Thomson. (Captain Thomas Robertson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Pedra Branca on a 1620 map

![A detail of an old hand-coloured map with a compass rose and a label reading Pedra Branca](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/Hessel_Gerritsz%2C_Map_of_Sumatra_showing_Pedra_Branca_%281620%29.jpg)

*Pedra Branca marked on a 1620 Dutch East India Company map of Sumatra, one of the old maps put before the ICJ. (Hessel Gerritsz, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Thomson's plan of the rock

![A black and white survey plan of an irregular rock outcrop at low water spring tides](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8f/Thomson%27s_topographic_map_of_Pedra_Branca.png/1280px-Thomson%27s_topographic_map_of_Pedra_Branca.png)

*Thomson's survey of Pedra Branca at low water spring tides, published with his 1852 account. (John Turnbull Thomson, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
