More images of Syonan in the years of the food shortage, the road north into Johor, Endau itself, and the end of the Occupation.

### Syonan, 1943

![A wide street in downtown Singapore lined with parked cars beside a colonnaded building, with a domed building beyond](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/58/Downtown_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._4_%281943-02-15%29%2C_p18.jpg/1280px-Downtown_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._4_%281943-02-15%29%2C_p18.jpg)

*Downtown Syonan, from the Japanese propaganda magazine Djawa Baroe, 15 February 1943. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The Causeway, 1943

![Labourers and wagons on the Causeway between Singapore and Johore Bahru, with buildings on the far shore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/Djohor_Baru_Bridge_in_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._4_%281943-02-15%29%2C_pp18-19.jpg/1280px-Djohor_Baru_Bridge_in_Singapore_%28Syonan%29%2C_Djawa_Baroe%2C_Vol._1%2C_Iss._4_%281943-02-15%29%2C_pp18-19.jpg)

*Labourers and wagons on the Causeway to Johore Bahru, the way north out of Syonan, from Djawa Baroe, 15 February 1943. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The road from Johore Bahru

![A car driving along the Causeway road between Johore Bahru and Singapore, beside the water](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/KITLV_A1363_-_Weg_van_Djohor_Bahru_naar_Singapore%2C_KITLV_78456.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_A1363_-_Weg_van_Djohor_Bahru_naar_Singapore%2C_KITLV_78456.tiff.jpg)

### Lim Boon Keng in 1929

![Formal portrait of Lim Boon Keng with a beard and spectacles, in a suit and tie, with Chinese characters below](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cd/%E6%9E%97%E6%96%87%E6%85%B6.jpg)
