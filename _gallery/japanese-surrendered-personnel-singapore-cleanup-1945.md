More photographs from Singapore's reoccupation in September 1945 and the two years that followed — the surrender itself, the wreckage the labour battalions were set to clear, and the men who did the clearing.

### The surrender at the Municipal Building

![The Japanese delegation mounting the steps of the Municipal Building, Singapore, 12 September 1945](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/ca/The_Japanese_Southern_Armies_Surrender_at_Singapore%2C_1945_SE4702.jpg)

*The Japanese delegation and its escort climb the steps of the Municipal Building to sign the surrender of the Japanese Southern Armies, 12 September 1945. (Photo: Captain E. A. Taylor, No. 9 Army Film & Photographic Unit / Imperial War Museums / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The victory parade

![Royal Marines band playing at the victory parade after the Japanese surrender, Singapore, 1945](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7b/The_Japanese_Southern_Armies_Surrender_at_Singapore%2C_1945_SE4723.jpg)

*The band of the Royal Marines plays during the victory parade that followed the hoisting of the Union Jack outside the Municipal Building, 1945. (Photo: Frederick Wackett, No. 9 Army Film & Photographic Unit / Imperial War Museums / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### Mountbatten returns

![Admiral Lord Louis Mountbatten inspecting a naval guard of honour outside the Municipal Building, Singapore, 1945](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ea/Louis_Mountbatten_Inspection%2C_Singapore_1945.jpg)

*Admiral Lord Louis Mountbatten, Supreme Allied Commander South East Asia — whose command adopted the "surrendered personnel" designation — inspects a naval guard of honour outside the Municipal Building, September 1945. (Photo: Lt C. Trusler, Royal Navy official photographer / Imperial War Museums / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The reoccupation force

![Men of the 5th Indian Division riding through Singapore during the reoccupation, 1945](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/48/British_Reoccupation_of_Singapore%2C_1945_SE4649.jpg)

*Men of the 5th Indian Division pass through the streets of Singapore in lorries shortly after landing with the reoccupation force, 1945. (Photo: Captain E. A. Taylor, No. 9 Army Film & Photographic Unit / Imperial War Museums / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The wreckage at the docks

![A Royal Air Force driver surveying bomb damage at Singapore docks, 1945](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fd/Damage_caused_by_Allied_bombing.jpg)

*A Royal Air Force motor-transport driver surveys damage caused by Allied bombing at the Singapore docks — the kind of clearing and repair work the labour battalions were put to. (Photo: Flying Officer Ashley, Royal Air Force official photographer / Imperial War Museums / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The defeated fleet

![Captain Power visiting a damaged Japanese cruiser at Singapore, 25 September 1945](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e4/CAPTAIN_POWER_VISITS_DAMAGED_JAPANESE_CRUISER._25_SEPTEMBER_1945%2C_SINGAPORE.jpg)

*A British officer visits a damaged Japanese cruiser in Singapore, 25 September 1945. (Photo: Lt C. Trusler / Imperial War Museums / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### Surrendered personnel elsewhere in the command

![A Japanese prisoner of war watching an RAF Dakota land at Bandoeng, Java, 1946](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c4/Japanese_POW_in_Java_during_1946.jpg)

*A Japanese surrendered soldier watches an RAF Dakota land at Bandoeng, Java, May 1946 — the same labour arrangement ran across South East Asia Command, not only in Singapore. (Photo: Sergeant L. Modd, No. 9 Army Film & Photographic Unit / Imperial War Museums / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*
