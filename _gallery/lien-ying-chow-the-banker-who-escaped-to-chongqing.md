More images of the places and people in Lien Ying Chow's story, from Hong Kong in the 1920s and wartime Chongqing to the bank's opening in 1949 and the hotel he built.

### The Sunday Times, 6 February 1949

![The front page of The Sunday Times, 6 February 1949, headed Australia Tightens Up Ban, with a short report headed People's Bank Opened in the lower left](/assets/images/sunday-times-1949-02-06-page-1.jpg)

*The front page of The Sunday Times, 6 February 1949. "People's Bank Opened," lower left, reports that more than 1,000 people, mostly businessmen, attended the bank's opening the day before. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Meyer Chambers, 1930

![Page 2 of The Straits Budget, 4 December 1930, headed New Building in Raffles Place, with a large photograph of the five-storey Meyer Chambers](/assets/images/straits-budget-1930-12-04-page-2.jpg)

*Page 2 of The Straits Budget, 4 December 1930: "New Building in Raffles Place," with a photograph of the newly completed Meyer Chambers, where the bank opened in 1949. (Singapore Press Holdings, via NewspaperSG, National Library Board Singapore, public domain)*

### Hong Kong in the 1920s

![Connaught Road on the Hong Kong waterfront in the 1920s, lined with arcaded buildings, with handcarts in the street](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cc/Hong_Kong_Connaught_Road_1920s.jpg)

*Connaught Road, Hong Kong, in the 1920s. Lien worked in Hong Kong for two years before sailing to Singapore in 1920. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Fremantle in wartime

![An aerial view of Fremantle Harbour during the Second World War, with ships moored along both sides](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/Fremantle_Harbour%2C_WW2.PNG)

*Fremantle Harbour during the Second World War. The Gorgon arrived here from Singapore on 20 February 1942. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Chiang Kai-shek

![Chiang Kai-shek in military uniform and cap in the 1940s](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d0/Chiang_Kai-shek_in_the_40s.jpg)

*Chiang Kai-shek in the 1940s. Lien met him in Chongqing and was appointed to the wartime political council. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Wartime Chongqing

![A crowd in a Chongqing street cheering a parade of young volunteers under a banner](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/48/Chungking_bomb6.jpg)

*Crowds in Chongqing cheer volunteers of the Youth Army during the war. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The walls of Chongqing

![A stretch of old crenellated stone city wall with trees on top](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/95/%E9%87%8D%E6%85%B6%E5%9F%8E%E5%A2%BB1940s.jpg)

*The old walls of Chongqing, from an American soldier's album of the 1940s. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### An exile office in Chongqing

![Old wooden buildings with tiled roofs and laundry hanging on a balcony, in a Chongqing lane](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4f/Korean_Provisional_Government_%28%EB%8C%80%ED%95%9C%EB%AF%BC%EA%B5%AD_%EC%9E%84%EC%8B%9C%EC%A0%95%EB%B6%80%2C_%E5%A4%A7%E9%9F%A9%E6%B0%91%E5%9B%BD_%E8%87%A8%E6%99%82%E6%94%BF%E5%BA%9C%29_Third_Office_in_Chongqing%2C_1941-1945.jpg)

*The third office of the Korean Provisional Government in Chongqing, used from 1941 to 1945. The wartime capital was full of exiles from countries the Japanese had occupied, as Lien was. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The surrender, September 1945

![The Japanese delegation walking down the steps of the Municipal Building in Singapore, lined by Allied servicemen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9e/Japanese_surrender_at_Singapore%2C_1945.jpg)

*The Japanese delegation leaves the Municipal Building after the surrender ceremony in Singapore on 12 September 1945. Lien returned the following month. (Royal Navy official photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Aw Boon Haw

![A head-and-shoulders portrait of Aw Boon Haw in a suit and bow tie](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Hu_Wenhu.jpg)

*Aw Boon Haw, the maker of Tiger Balm, who sat on the bank's first board. (Unknown photographer, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Tan Lark Sye

![Tan Lark Sye standing in a white suit and tie in 1950](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/dd/Tan_Lark_Sye%2C_1950.jpg)

### Loke Wan Tho

### The Chinese Chamber of Commerce
