A few more images around the Post Office Savings Bank: the building where it began, the passbook it was carried in, and the branches and machines it became.

### Where it started: the General Post Office

![The Fullerton Building, Singapore, seen from the waterfront](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0a/The_Fullerton_Hotel%2C_Singapore.jpg)

*The Fullerton Building, which housed the General Post Office — and with it the Post Office Savings Bank counter — from 1928 until the 1990s. It is now a hotel. (Photo: Johan Jönsson / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### Raffles' Square in the 1890s

![Raffles' Square, Singapore, photographed in the 1890s by G. R. Lambert and Company](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Photographic_Views_of_Singapore_Plate_03_Raffles%27_Square.jpg)

*Raffles' Square (Commercial Square) in the 1890s. The General Post Office stood at the seaward end of this square, on the site later occupied by the Fullerton Building, through the bank's first fifty years. (Photo: G. R. Lambert & Co., c. 1890s, from "Photographic Views of Singapore" / Wikimedia Commons; public domain)*

### The passbook: inside the front cover

![The inside front cover and first page of a Federation of Malaya Post Office Savings Bank passbook](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/13/Federation_of_Malaya_Post_Office_Savings_Bank_%28POSB%29_Bankbook%2C_Inner_Front_Cover_%26_First_Page.png)

*The inside front cover and first page of a Post Office Savings Bank passbook from the Federation of Malaya — the sibling institution to Singapore's own bank, split from a common 1877 root in 1949. The rules of the account are printed opposite the first entries. (Image: Lousysofa / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The passbook: the back pages

![The last page and inside back cover of a Federation of Malaya Post Office Savings Bank passbook](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bb/Federation_of_Malaya_Post_Office_Savings_Bank_%28POSB%29_Bankbook%2C_Last_Page_%26_Inner_Back_Cover.png)

*The last page and inside back cover of the same passbook. (Image: Lousysofa / Wikimedia Commons, public domain)*

### The first ATM branch: Newton

![The POSB branch at Newton, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a4/POSB_Newton_Branch.png)

*The POSB branch at Newton, where the bank installed its first automated teller machine — a "Cash-On-Line" unit — in 1981. Within a year it was announcing branches run by machines alone. (Photo: Deoma12 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### Newton branch entrance

![The entrance to the POSB Newton branch](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/25/POSB_Newton_Branch_Entrance.png)

*The entrance to the Newton branch. (Photo: Deoma12 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### A branch in the estate: Taman Warna

![A small POSB branch at Taman Warna, off Holland Road, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1c/POSB_Taman_Warna.JPG)

*A small POSB branch at Taman Warna, off Holland Road, photographed in 2006 — the kind of neighbourhood outlet that gave the bank its reach. (Photo: Terence Ong / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### The machine on the way home: Bugis MRT

![A row of POSB automated teller machines at Bugis MRT station, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fb/POSB_Bugis_MRT_Station_ATM.jpg)

*POSB automated teller machines at Bugis MRT station. POSB still runs the largest ATM network in Singapore, much of it in stations and void decks rather than bank halls. (Photo: Deoma12 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
