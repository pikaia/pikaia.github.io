The malls the Japanese department stores anchored — some gone, some still trading.

### Ngee Ann City: Takashimaya

![Ngee Ann City on Orchard Road, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2e/Ngee_Ann_City%2C_Dec_05.JPG)

*Ngee Ann City, which Takashimaya has anchored since 1993, photographed in 2005. It is the last of the big Japanese department stores in Singapore still trading in close to its original form. (Photo: Sengkang / Wikimedia Commons, copyrighted free use)*

### Wisma Atria: Isetan

![Wisma Atria on Orchard Road, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/aa/Wisma_Atria_building_2011.jpg)

*Wisma Atria in 2011. Isetan moved its Orchard Road store here in 1986. By the end of 2025 the chain was down from six Singapore stores in 2013 to essentially one, on Scotts Road. (Photo: alantankenghoe / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0)*

### Shaw House: Isetan Scotts

![Shaw House at the corner of Orchard Road and Scotts Road, Singapore, decorated for Christmas](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bd/Shaw_House_2%2C_Xmas%2C_Dec_06.JPG)

*Shaw House, at the Orchard–Scotts corner, in December 2006. Its Isetan Scotts store is now the chain's last major outlet in Singapore. (Photo: Sengkang / Wikimedia Commons, copyrighted free use)*

### Liang Court: Daimaru

![Liang Court on River Valley Road, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/49/Liang_Court%2C_Feb_06.JPG)

*Liang Court in 2006. Daimaru was its anchor tenant for twenty years, from 1983 until 2003. The complex itself was demolished in 2021 for redevelopment. (Photo: Sengkang / Wikimedia Commons, copyrighted free use)*

### Raffles City: Sogo

![Raffles City, Singapore, seen from the exterior](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/21/RafflesCityExterior.JPG)

*Raffles City in 2005. Sogo opened here in 1986 and closed the store in October 2000 after going into judicial management in Singapore. (Photo: mailer_diablo / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### Plaza Singapura, inside

![The interior atrium of Plaza Singapura shopping mall, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/28/Large_interior_view_of_Plaza_Singapura_Shopping_mall_Orchard_Road_Singapore.jpg)

*Inside Plaza Singapura in 2018. Yaohan occupied several floors here from 1974 until 1997; the space has since been an assortment of other tenants. (Photo: Basile Morin / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### Don Don Donki at Jewel

![A Don Don Donki store at Jewel Changi Airport, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fa/Jewel_Changi_Airport_Don_Don_Donki_18-05-2024%285%29.jpg)

*Don Don Donki at Jewel Changi Airport in 2024. The 24-hour Japanese chain, built around cheap ready-to-eat food, opened its first Singapore store in 2017 and has spread across the island since. (Photo: LN9267 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
