Four more images tied to Lim Chong Yah's world — the campus where he taught, and three glimpses of the low-wage, labour-intensive economy his National Wages Council spent decades trying to move Singapore beyond.

### Another view of his academic home

![The Manasseh Meyer Building at the Bukit Timah campus of the National University of Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f4/Manasseh_Meyer_Building%2C_National_University_of_Singapore_Bukit_Timah_Campus%2C_October_2025.jpg)

*The Manasseh Meyer Building at NUS's Bukit Timah campus, part of the same grounds where Lim Chong Yah taught economics and served as Dean of Arts and Social Sciences. (Photo: GoAheadFan95 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### A potter at work, 1973-74

![A potter at work in Singapore, 1973-74](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ef/Singapore-Potter-1973-74-WUS08246.jpg)

*A potter at work in Singapore, 1973–74 — one of the small, low-margin trades that made up much of Singapore's economy before the NWC's push toward higher-wage, higher-skill industry. (Photo: Rainer Halama / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### A coffin maker's shop, 1973-74

![A coffin maker's shop in Singapore, 1973-74](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d3/Singapore-Coffin_Maker-1973-74-WUS08245.jpg)

*A coffin maker's shop in Singapore, 1973–74. (Photo: Rainer Halama / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### A sidewalk barber, 1973-74

![A sidewalk barber at work in Singapore, 1973-74](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/39/Singapore-Sidewalk_barber_in_1973-74-WUS08232.jpg)

*A sidewalk barber at work in Singapore, 1973–74 — the kind of informal, low-wage livelihood the National Wages Council's guidelines never directly touched. (Photo: Rainer Halama / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
