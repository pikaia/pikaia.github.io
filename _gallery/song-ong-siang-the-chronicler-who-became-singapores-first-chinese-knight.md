Song Ong Siang himself, and three of the community leaders whose portraits his 1923 book preserved.

### Song Ong Siang

![Portrait of Song Ong Siang in academic gown and mortarboard, about 1923](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4c/Sir_Song_Ong_Siang%2C_1923_%28cropped%29.png)

*Song Ong Siang, cropped from the double portrait in the frontispiece of his book, about 1923. (Song Ong Siang, One Hundred Years' History of the Chinese in Singapore, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Tan Kim Ching

![Oval halftone portrait of Tan Kim Ching in a skullcap](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cc/Tan_Kim_Ching.jpg)

*Tan Kim Ching, rice mill and ship owner and founder of the Tanjong Pagar Dock Company in 1864, in a plate from the book. (Song Ong Siang, One Hundred Years' History, 1923, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Seah Eu Chin

![Portrait of Seah Eu Chin, an older man with a white moustache and goatee in a skullcap](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/64/Seah_Eu_Chin.jpg)

*Seah Eu Chin, one of the early Chinese community leaders profiled in the book. (Song Ong Siang, One Hundred Years' History, 1923, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Wee Boon Teck

![Portrait of Wee Boon Teck in a skullcap and dark jacket](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2a/Wee_Boon_Teck.jpg)

*Wee Boon Teck, another of the pioneers whose portraits appear in the book. (Song Ong Siang, One Hundred Years' History, 1923, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*
