Six more images tracing Singapore's travel story — from Paya Lebar's control tower and Changi's first years on the tarmac to the cruise ships that dock at Marina Bay today.

### Paya Lebar, the airport before Changi

![Singapore International Airport control tower and terminal building, circa 1969-1971](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d3/Singapore_International_Airport_control_tower_and_terminal_building%2C_photographed_February_1969_%C3%97_July_1971.jpg)

*The control tower and terminal building of Singapore International Airport at Paya Lebar, photographed between February 1969 and July 1971. This was Singapore's main airport from 1955 until Changi opened in 1981. (Photo: NSBReynolds / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### An international carrier on the Paya Lebar tarmac

![SAS Caravelle airliner at Singapore International Airport, circa 1969-1971](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/SAS_airliner_at_Singapore_International_Airport%2C_photographed_February_1969_%C3%97_July_1971.jpg)

*A Scandinavian Airlines Sud Aviation Caravelle at Paya Lebar, photographed between February 1969 and July 1971 — one of the foreign carriers flying into Singapore's airport before Changi. (Photo: NSBReynolds / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*

### Malaysia-Singapore Airlines, before the split

![Malaysia-Singapore Airlines Boeing 707 at Zurich, 1972](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/Malaysia-Singapore_Airlines_B707_at_Zurich_1972.jpg)

*A Malaysia-Singapore Airlines Boeing 707 at Zurich in 1972, the year the joint national carrier split into Singapore Airlines and Malaysian Airline System — the formal, full-service era the post's opening describes. (Photo: Phillip Capper / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0)*

### Changi Airport's first year

![Singapore Airlines Boeing 727 and Garuda Indonesia DC-10 at Singapore Changi Airport, 1982](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/29/Singapore_Airlines_Boeing_727_Martin.jpg)

*A Singapore Airlines Boeing 727 and a Garuda Indonesia DC-10 on the tarmac at the newly opened Singapore Changi Airport in 1982, the year after it replaced Paya Lebar. (Photo: Stefan Martin / Wikimedia Commons, GFDL 1.2)*

### Changi in its early growth years

![Singapore Airlines Airbus A300 at Singapore Changi Airport, 1983](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4d/Singapore_Airlines_Airbus_A300_Green-1.jpg)

*A Singapore Airlines Airbus A300 at Changi Airport in January 1983, as the airport settled into the role it still holds today. (Photo: Doug Green / Wikimedia Commons, GFDL 1.2)*

### Travelling by sea, today

![The cruise ship Anthem of the Seas berthed at Marina Bay Cruise Centre, Singapore, December 2024](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/96/Royal_Caribbean_Cruises_Anthem_of_the_Seas_05-12-2024%281%29.jpg)

*Royal Caribbean's Anthem of the Seas at the Marina Bay Cruise Centre in December 2024. Flying is only part of the story — the same rise in incomes and appetite for getting away fills these ships with Singaporeans on their second or third cruise of the year. (Photo: LN9267 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)*
