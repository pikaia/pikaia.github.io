More photographs of the buildings Singapore's Indian convicts built, and of the convicts themselves as McNair photographed them.

### St Andrew's Cathedral, around 1900

![St Andrew's Cathedral seen across the Padang, with its tall spire and the Raffles statue in the foreground](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/86/Photographic_Views_of_Singapore_Plate_06_St_Andrew%27s_Cathedral_and_Raffles_Monument.jpg)

*St Andrew's Cathedral and the Raffles statue around 1900, from Photographic Views of Singapore. The convict-built church became a cathedral in 1870. (Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co., public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### The cathedral behind the Hotel de l'Europe

![An open field with low hotel buildings and trees, and the spire of St Andrew's Cathedral rising behind them](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0c/KITLV_-_89904_-_Hotel_de_l%27Europe_against_the_backdrop_of_St_Andrews_Cathedral_in_Singapore_-_before_1880.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_89904_-_Hotel_de_l%27Europe_against_the_backdrop_of_St_Andrews_Cathedral_in_Singapore_-_before_1880.tif.jpg)

*The Hotel de l'Europe with the spire of St Andrew's Cathedral behind it, before 1880. (Photo: unknown photographer, KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A watercolour of the cathedral, 1879

![A watercolour of the Singapore waterfront with boats in the foreground, Fort Canning hill and St Andrew's Cathedral behind](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2f/Malaysia%3B_view_across_the_harbour_to_Fort_Canning_and_the_ca_Wellcome_V0037489.jpg/1280px-Malaysia%3B_view_across_the_harbour_to_Fort_Canning_and_the_ca_Wellcome_V0037489.jpg)

*The view across the harbour to Fort Canning and the cathedral, a watercolour by John Edmund Taylor, 1879. (Image: John Edmund Taylor, Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Government House, around 1890

![The front of Government House, a large white colonial building with columns and verandas, with palms in front](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/KITLV_-_53172_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Government_House_in_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_53172_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Government_House_in_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Government House, now the Istana, around 1890. Convicts built most of it between 1867 and 1869. (Photo: G.R. Lambert & Co., KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Government House on its hill

![Government House seen from a distance at the top of a grassy slope](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/87/KITLV_-_103751_-_Government_House_in_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_103751_-_Government_House_in_Singapore_-_circa_1890.tif.jpg)

*Government House on its hill in the eastern suburb of the town, around 1890. (Photo: unknown photographer, KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Cavenagh Bridge, around 1870

![Cavenagh Bridge over the Singapore River in dim light, with buildings behind it](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0b/KITLV_-_155825_-_Cavanagh_Bridge_at_Singapore_-_circa_1870.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_155825_-_Cavanagh_Bridge_at_Singapore_-_circa_1870.tif.jpg)

*Cavenagh Bridge around 1870, soon after it was completed. (Photo: unknown photographer, KITLV/Leiden University Library, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Duffadar Arjoon, senior petty officer of artificers

![A portrait of Duffadar Arjoon, a convict petty officer in a turban and pale tunic with a dark sash](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/California_Digital_Library_%28IA_prisonerstheirow00mcnarich%29.pdf/page12-1280px-California_Digital_Library_%28IA_prisonerstheirow00mcnarich%29.pdf.jpg)

*Duffadar Arjoon, the senior convict petty officer in charge of the artificers, who trained other convicts in trades. (Photo: J. F. A. McNair and W. D. Bayliss, Prisoners Their Own Warders, 1899, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### A convict of the second class

![A full-length portrait of a convict in white clothes and a white turban standing against a plain backdrop](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/California_Digital_Library_%28IA_prisonerstheirow00mcnarich%29.pdf/page135-1280px-California_Digital_Library_%28IA_prisonerstheirow00mcnarich%29.pdf.jpg)

*A convict of the second class, who also served as a munshi, or clerk and interpreter. The second class held the convict petty officers and office workers. (Photo: J. F. A. McNair and W. D. Bayliss, Prisoners Their Own Warders, 1899, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### Chetoo, a convict of the fifth class

![A full-length portrait of a convict in a short shirt and cap](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/California_Digital_Library_%28IA_prisonerstheirow00mcnarich%29.pdf/page143-1280px-California_Digital_Library_%28IA_prisonerstheirow00mcnarich%29.pdf.jpg)

*Chetoo, described by McNair as an incorrigible convict of the fifth class, the class that worked in heavy irons. (Photo: J. F. A. McNair and W. D. Bayliss, Prisoners Their Own Warders, 1899, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*

### McNair's plan for a prison at Outram

![An architectural plan of a prison with long wings radiating from a central hub](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/30/J_F_A_McNair%2C_architectural_drawing_of_a_proposed_prison_at_Outram%2C_Singapore_%281880s%29.jpg/1280px-J_F_A_McNair%2C_architectural_drawing_of_a_proposed_prison_at_Outram%2C_Singapore_%281880s%29.jpg)

*An architectural drawing by J. F. A. McNair of a proposed prison at Outram, 1880s, made after the convict system ended. (Image: John Frederick Adolphus McNair, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons)*
