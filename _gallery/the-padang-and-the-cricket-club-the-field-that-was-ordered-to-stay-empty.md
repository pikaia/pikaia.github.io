Ten more views of the field and its club: the Raffles statue in its original place, the pavilion across a century, and the Padang from the 1970s to its monument year and after.

### The Raffles monument, about 1900

![The Raffles monument on the Esplanade, about 1900](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6d/KITLV_-_105827_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Rafflesmonument_in_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif/lossy-page1-1280px-KITLV_-_105827_-_Lambert_%26_Co.%2C_G.R._-_Singapore_-_Rafflesmonument_in_Singapore_-_circa_1900.tif.jpg)

*The Raffles monument on the Esplanade about 1900, photographed by Lambert & Co. — the statue stood on the field from 1887 until its move to Empress Place in 1919. (Lambert & Co., Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed c. 1900)*

### The Padang looking west, about 1914

![The Padang looking west with the Cricket Club, Town Hall, Supreme Court and Hotel de l'Europe behind, about 1914](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/41/Souvenir_of_Singapore%2C_1914_-_Plate_12_-_Singapore_Cricket_Club%2C_Town_Hall%2C_Supreme_Court_and_Europe_Hotel.jpg)

*Plate 12 of the 1914 Souvenir of Singapore: the Raffles statue in the foreground, and behind the field the Cricket Club, the Town Hall, the old Supreme Court and the Grand Hotel de l'Europe. (Unknown photographer, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed c. 1914)*

### The Cricket Club postcard, 1921

![The Singapore Cricket Club pavilion on a postcard poststamped 1921](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/Singapore_Cricket_Club%2C_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404869.tiff/lossy-page1-1280px-Singapore_Cricket_Club%2C_Singapore%2C_KITLV_1404869.tiff.jpg)

*The club as its members mailed it home: a postcard of the pavilion, poststamped 15 July 1921, a year before the wings completed the building. (K. P. Hock, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; published 1906–1921)*

### The pavilion in 1963

![The Singapore Cricket Club pavilion in 1963](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Singapore_Cricket_Club_1963.jpg)

*The pavilion on 1 June 1963, in the last years of the club's whites-on-the-Padang heyday — and the year the Recreation Club at the other end opened its membership to all communities. (Don Christie, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1963)*

### The Padang in 1973

![The Padang with the former Supreme Court and City Hall behind, 1973](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a7/Singapore-Padang-1973-74-WUS01252.jpg)

*The field in 1973–74, with the Supreme Court dome and City Hall behind it — the parade ground in the decade the parades left for the National Stadium. (Rainer Halama, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1973–74)*

### The club from the field, 1973

![The Padang and the Singapore Cricket Club, 1973](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cc/Singapore-Singapore_Cricket_Club-7973-74-WUS08188.jpg)

*The Cricket Club from across its ground in 1973–74, the pavilion of 1884 and 1922 under its trees. (Rainer Halama, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 1973–74)*

### The pavilion today

![The Singapore Cricket Club building on Connaught Drive, 2016](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/91/Singapore_Cricket_Club_building_2016.jpg)

*The clubhouse on Connaught Drive in November 2016 — the 1884 core inside, the 1922 wings either side. (Orderinchaos, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2016)*

### Bowls at the club, 2023

![Lawn bowls at the Singapore Cricket Club, 2023](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/64/Singapore_Cricket_Club_Bowlers.jpg)

*Lawn bowls at the club in January 2023 — the game the club took up in the 1870s, still played beside the Padang. (Kari.Shouur, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2023)*

### The parade rehearses, 2024

![The National Day Parade rehearsal on the Padang, July 2024](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2e/Singapore_NDP_2024_-_Rehearsal.jpg)

*The National Day Parade rehearsing on the Padang in July 2024, seen from the National Gallery — the field back in its ceremonial role for SG59. (Asalover2004, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2024)*

### The monument, 2025

![The Padang in October 2025](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3d/The_Padang%2C_October_2025.jpg)

*The Padang in October 2025, three years after its gazetting as Singapore's 75th National Monument — the first monument on the list that is a field, protected as it is: empty. (GoAheadFan95, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; photographed 2025)*
