The Raffles Place area, where the Stock Exchange of Singapore and the banks at the centre of the 1985 rescue all stood within a few streets of one another.

### Raffles Place before the towers

![Raffles Place in Singapore in 1971, with low-rise bank buildings and the dome of the old Supreme Court](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/dc/71-610_Raffles_Place_Singapore_1971_%2851252222815%29.jpg)

*Raffles Place in 1971, still low-rise and built around a central garden, before the office towers of the late 1970s and 1980s went up. (Photo: Wilford Peloquin / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0)*

### Clifford Centre

![Clifford Centre and the adjacent Ocean Towers in Raffles Place, Singapore](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/37/Clifford_Centre_and_Ocean_Towers.JPG)

*Clifford Centre, at left, on Raffles Place. The Stock Exchange of Singapore's trading floor operated here through the 1980s. (Photo: Terence Ong / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### OCBC Centre

![The OCBC Centre tower in Singapore, designed by I. M. Pei](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e6/OCBC_Centre_Tower%2C_Dec_05.JPG)

*OCBC Centre, headquarters of one of the four banks that put up the S$180 million line of credit that kept the exposed broking firms afloat during the shutdown. (Photo: Sengkang / Wikimedia Commons, copyrighted free use)*

### The Singapore River frontage

![The Singapore financial district skyline seen from Parliament House across the Singapore River](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/36/Singapore_skyline.JPG)

*The financial district seen across the Singapore River from Parliament House, with the Bank of China building, UOB Plaza and OCBC Centre. (Photo: Terence Ong / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)*

### The bank towers at dusk

![Evening view of UOB Plaza, OUB Centre and OCBC Centre near the Singapore River](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a8/Evening_view_of_UOB_Plaza%2C_OUB_Centre_and_OCBC_Centre_near_the_Singapore_River_-_20010608.jpg)

*UOB Plaza, OUB Centre (now One Raffles Place) and OCBC Centre. Three of the four rescue banks stood here; the Monetary Authority and the courts drafted the credit agreement over the three days the market was closed. (Photo: Steven Byles / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0)*
