HARD PERMISSION GATE: the Changi Murals post (pushed 2026-10-25, commit d34b5c3)
has no pictures of the murals themselves. Stanley Warren died in 1992, so his
paintings are still in copyright (UK term runs to the end of 2062), and the
originals are inside Changi Air Base (West), which is closed to the public. The
Wikipedia photos are non-free fair-use uploads and can't be reused. Nothing
showing the murals or the replicas goes into the post or the videos until a reply
gives permission.

Who: the Changi Chapel and Museum (National Heritage Board). Its media page
(https://www.heritage.sg/changichapelmuseum/visit/media-requests) gives
ccm@heritage.sg for image and filming requests. The same page allows still
photos for private, non-commercial use only, so a visit to photograph the replicas
for the blog and YouTube also needs their OK.

If they decline, or say the copyright sits with Warren's family or with MINDEF,
the next step would be the National Archives of Singapore. It holds Warren's 1982
oral history and his drawing of St Luke in Prison, and may hold photographs of the
murals or the restorations (search https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/).

The video versions would go on YouTube and TikTok, which may carry platform
advertising, so the draft doesn't call the use "non-commercial".
