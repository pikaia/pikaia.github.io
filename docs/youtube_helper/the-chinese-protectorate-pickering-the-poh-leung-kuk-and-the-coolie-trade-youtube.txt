Title:
The Chinese Protectorate: Pickering, the Poh Leung Kuk and the Coolie Trade

Description:
On the 18th of July 1887, a Teochew carpenter named Chua Ah Siok walked into the office of the Protector of Chinese in Singapore, saying he had a petition to present. When he reached the Protector, William Pickering, he threw the iron head of an axe at him. It struck Pickering on the forehead, and he never fully recovered. The attack was blamed on a secret society, and it was aimed at the man who, for ten years, had run the colony's first serious attempt to protect Chinese migrants from the trade that brought them to Singapore.
