Title:
Sir Manasseh Meyer: Singapore's Jewish Landlord and the Synagogues He Built

Description:
Sir Manasseh Meyer arrived in Singapore from Calcutta as a boy of 15 in 1861 and died at his house on Oxley Rise in 1930, reputed to be a millionaire in sterling several times over. In between he built a trading firm, became one of the largest property owners in the colony, and paid for both of the synagogues that Singapore's Jewish community still uses today. His hotels, flats and shophouses have almost all gone, but the synagogues, a college building and a school still carry his name.
