Title:
Robinsons and the 1972 Fire: 162 Years of Singapore's Oldest Department Store

Description:
Just before ten o'clock on the morning of the 21st of November 1972, an overloaded electrical circuit caught fire inside Robinsons, the department store on Raffles Place. Within minutes the four-storey building was alight, and nine people died, eight of them trapped in the store's lifts when the power failed. Robinsons was 114 years old that morning. It reopened on Orchard Road twenty days later, and it went on trading for almost half a century more, until its last Singapore store closed in January 2021.
