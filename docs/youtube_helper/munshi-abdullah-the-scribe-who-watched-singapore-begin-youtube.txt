Title:
Munshi Abdullah: The Malay Scribe Who Watched Singapore Begin

Description:
One evening in March 1823, a young Malay teacher on his way to give a language lesson met William Farquhar, the British Resident of Singapore, coming the other way with his son, his son-in-law and four armed sepoys. When Farquhar heard where he was going, he told him to turn back and come along, because a man had run amok near a merchant's house. Within the hour the teacher was holding Farquhar up as he collapsed, his coat soaked in blood. The teacher was Abdullah bin Abdul Kadir, known as Munshi Abdullah, and twenty years later he wrote the whole night down in the Hikayat Abdullah, one of the few first-hand accounts of Singapore's founding years written in Malay.
