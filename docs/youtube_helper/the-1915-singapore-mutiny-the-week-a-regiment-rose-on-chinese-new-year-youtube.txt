Title:
The 1915 Singapore Mutiny: The Week a Regiment Rose on Chinese New Year

Description:
On the afternoon of 15 February 1915, which was Chinese New Year, about 800 Indian soldiers of the 5th Light Infantry were stationed at Alexandra Barracks, some of them guarding German prisoners of war in the middle of a world war. By evening, some of them had turned their rifles on their own officers and on civilians. It took the better part of a week, and warships from Japan, France and Russia, to put it down.
