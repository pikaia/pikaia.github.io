Title:
Song Ong Siang: The Chronicler Who Became Singapore's First Chinese Knight

Description:
If you have read much history of the Singapore Chinese, you have almost certainly relied on a 602-page book published in London in 1923, at £12 a copy. Its author, Song Ong Siang, was a lawyer, a magazine editor, a volunteer captain and eventually a knight, and yet few people today know his name.
