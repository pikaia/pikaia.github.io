Title:
The Sikh Police Who Came in 1881, and the Community They Left Behind

Description:
On 26 March 1881 an assistant superintendent and 54 Sikh recruits from Punjab arrived in Singapore to join a police force that was struggling to keep order in a crowded, fast-growing port. By the end of the year the Sikh Police Contingent numbered 165 men. The contingent was disbanded in 1945, but the community that grew up around it, with its own gurdwara on Silat Road, is still in Singapore today.
