Title:
Syonan Jinja: The Forest Shrine the Japanese Army Burned Down on the Day It Surrendered

Description:
On 15 August 1945 — the day Emperor Hirohito's broadcast ended the Pacific War — retreating Japanese soldiers in Singapore did something specific with their last hours in charge: they set fire to their own shrine. Syonan Jinja, deep in the forest at MacRitchie Reservoir, had been built by thousands of Allied prisoners of war to be the grandest Shinto shrine anywhere in occupied Asia outside Tokyo. Its builders burned it rather than let the returning British see it standing.
