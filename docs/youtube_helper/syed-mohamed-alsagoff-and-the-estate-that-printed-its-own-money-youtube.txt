Title:
Syed Mohamed Alsagoff and the Estate That Printed Its Own Money

Description:
On the 1st of May 1878, a press in Singapore printed a set of banknotes that no bank had issued. They were worth 25 cents, 50 cents, one dollar and two dollars, they were printed on one side only, and each was signed by hand. They were meant to be spent in one place, the estates of an Arab merchant named Syed Mohamed Alsagoff on the island of Kukup, off the south-western tip of Johor. The Sultan of Johor had given him the land, and with it the right to issue his own money.
