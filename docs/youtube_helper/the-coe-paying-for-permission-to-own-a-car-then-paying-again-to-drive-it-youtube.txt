Title:
The COE: Paying for Permission to Own a Car, Then Paying Again to Drive It

Description:
The gantries are coming down. Since October 2026, work crews have been unbolting the grey-blue frames that have spanned Singapore's expressways and city streets for a generation, and by the middle of November a dozen were already gone. The last working gantries will go on charging until 31 December 2026; from 1 January 2027 the charge arrives by satellite instead, and the sign that a charged road lies ahead will be an oval of blue paint on the tarmac. It is the end of the most visible piece of a system that does something no other country attempts in quite the same way: it makes a driver pay for permission to own a car, and then pay again, per trip, to drive it. The two charges have different names, different histories and one shared reason. This is the story of both.
