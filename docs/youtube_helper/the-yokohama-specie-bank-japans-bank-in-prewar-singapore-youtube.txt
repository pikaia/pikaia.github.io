Title:
The Yokohama Specie Bank: Japan's Bank in Prewar Singapore

Description:
On 20 March 1942, five weeks after the British surrender, the Yokohama Specie Bank reopened for business in Singapore, now renamed Syonan. It did not go back to its own office in Meyer Chambers. A notice in the Syonan Shimbun gave its new address as the "Late Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corp.", the building of the British bank whose staff had just been interned. The Yokohama Specie Bank had been in Singapore since 1916, financing the colony's trade with Japan from a corner of Raffles Place, and for most of that time it was simply one of the foreign exchange banks in the square. Its story shows how closely a bank could be tied to the fortunes of its government.
