Title:
Blakang Mati Before Sentosa: The Island's Kampongs and Barracks

Description:
For most of its recorded history, the island we now call Sentosa was Pulau Blakang Mati, a working island of fishing villages, fruit gardens and, from 1878, British forts and barracks. By 1959 about 1,000 people lived in two kampongs on its northern shore, many of them working for the soldiers. In 1970 the island was renamed Sentosa and rebuilt as a resort, and its villagers moved to new flats across the harbour at Telok Blangah.
