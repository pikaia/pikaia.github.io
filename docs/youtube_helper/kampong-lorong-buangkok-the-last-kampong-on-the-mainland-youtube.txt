Title:
Kampong Lorong Buangkok: The Last Kampong on the Mainland

Description:
It survived. Every other kampong on the mainland is gone, but this one is still here — and as of 2024, the rent for some of its houses is $6.50 a month. The most anyone pays is $30. Those figures have barely moved in decades — the same $6.50 to $30 range reported in 2007 and again in 2024 — and if a tenant is short of money, the landlady will take rice or fruit instead. There are twenty-five families left — twelve Chinese, thirteen Malay — living in wooden houses with zinc roofs along earth lanes, behind the HDB blocks of Buangkok Green. The landlady is Sng Mui Hong, who was seventy-one when CNA profiled her in November 2024, and who has promised her late father that the kampong will not be sold. "As long as I have enough to live, that's already fine," she said. "There's no need to be greedy. Even if I get a lot of money, it will all be finished eventually." This is Kampong Lorong Buangkok, the last kampong on mainland Singapore — a village that was supposed to disappear with all the others, and didn't.
