Title:
The Marquis and the Museum: Tokugawa Yoshichika in Syonan, 1942–44

Description:
On 5 September 1942 the Syonan Times reported that Marquis Yoshichika Tokugawa, adviser to the Japanese military administration in Malaya, had been made director of the Syonan Museum, Botanical Gardens and Library. These were the old Raffles Museum and the Botanic Gardens under new names. The paper added that "no damage was caused" to them in the fighting for Singapore. For the next two years a Japanese nobleman who had once come to Malaya to hunt tigers presided over the island's two great scientific collections. Working under him, and outside the internment camps, were two British botanists, Eric Holttum and John Corner. Both collections came through the war largely intact, and their descendants are still in Singapore today.
