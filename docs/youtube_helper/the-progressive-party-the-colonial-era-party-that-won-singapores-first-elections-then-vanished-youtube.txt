Title:
The Progressive Party: The Colonial-Era Party That Won Singapore's First Elections, Then Vanished

Description:
When the results of Singapore's 1955 election were announced, the Progressive Party had won 4 of the 25 seats. It had won the two elections before that, in 1948 and 1951, and it went into 1955 as the colony's leading party. Its president, C. C. Tan, lost his own contest, in Cairnhill, to David Marshall, whose Labour Front formed the new government. Ten months later the Progressives voted to dissolve their party, and at the 1959 election the party they had merged into won no seats at all. Their story is as much about who was allowed to vote, and how the votes were divided, as about the party itself.
