Title:
The Ointment Outlasted the Empire It Paid For

Description:
There is a fair chance that somewhere in your home, or on a shelf at the pharmacy nearest you, there is a small six-sided glass jar with a leaping tiger on the lid. Tiger Balm is sold in more than a hundred countries and turns up at a suburban American drugstore as readily as at a Singapore provision shop. Almost nobody who buys it knows that the jar is the last everyday trace of a fortune that once also ran a stable of newspapers and a bank, and paid for the hillside of painted concrete demons that Singaporeans know as Haw Par Villa.
