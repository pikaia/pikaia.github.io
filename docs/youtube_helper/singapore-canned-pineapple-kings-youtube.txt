Title:
When Singapore Led the World in Canned Pineapple

Description:
In 1912, ships leaving Singapore carried more than half a million cases of canned pineapple, and for the first decades of the twentieth century the island was the largest producer and exporter of canned pineapple in the world. The fruit is almost absent from the way Singapore tells its own economic story now, which tends to start with rubber, tin and the port. Yet pineapple came first. It filled some of the island's earliest factories, and it seeded at least three of the largest Chinese business fortunes in the region — fortunes whose names are still attached to a town, a run of schools and universities, and a charitable foundation that is still giving money away today.
