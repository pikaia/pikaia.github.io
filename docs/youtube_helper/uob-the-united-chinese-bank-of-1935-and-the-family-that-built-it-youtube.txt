Title:
UOB: The United Chinese Bank of 1935 and the Family That Built It

Description:
On the morning of 1 October 1935, a new bank opened its doors at the corner of Chulia Street and Bonham Street, facing Raffles Place, in premises formerly occupied by the Yokohama Specie Bank. It was called the United Chinese Bank, it had seven founders and $1 million of paid-up capital, and it was set up to serve Singapore's Hokkien merchants in the middle of the Great Depression. Ninety years later, as United Overseas Bank, it is one of Singapore's three big local banks. Much of that growth came from buying other banks, one of them older than itself.
