Title:
Pulau Ubin: The Island Singapore Decided to Keep

Description:
Ten minutes by bumboat from Changi Point, Singapore becomes another country. There are no traffic lights on Pulau Ubin, no shopping malls, no housing blocks — just a village of bicycle shops, kampong houses among the coconut palms, flooded granite quarries turned into lakes, and a few dozen residents living much as their grandparents did. Every other island around Singapore has been remade — merged, built on, connected, closed. Ubin alone was left as it was, and in the end that was a decision, not an accident. This is the story of the island Singapore decided to keep.
