Title:
Kusu Island and the Ninth-Month Pilgrimage

Description:
On Sunday the 17th of October 1948, a record 5,000 Chinese men, women and children crossed from Singapore to Kusu Island by motor sampan. It was the birthday of the deity Tua Pek Kong, on the 15th day of the ninth lunar month, and the high point of a pilgrimage that lasts the whole month. Most of them then climbed a small hill at the other end of the island to pray at the shrine of a Muslim holy man. The pilgrimage is still made every year, and this year's season runs from the 10th of October to the 8th of November.
