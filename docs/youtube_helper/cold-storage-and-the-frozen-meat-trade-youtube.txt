Title:
Cold Storage and the Frozen-Meat Trade

Description:
On 8 June 1903, a company called Cold Storage was registered in Singapore to solve one very specific problem: there was no reliable way, in the tropics, to keep a piece of meat cold long enough to eat it safely. Everything the company became afterwards — the supermarkets, the ice cream, the dairy farm, even a shopping mall on Orchard Road — grew out of that one narrow need.
