Title:
Tani Yutaka, the “Tiger of Malaya”: The Man and the Wartime Legend

Description:
On 17 March 1942, a month after the fall of Singapore, a 30-year-old Japanese man died of malaria in a Singapore hospital. He had grown up in Kuala Terengganu, spoke Malay, had led a gang of robbers in the north of the peninsula, and had been recruited by Japanese military intelligence the year before the invasion. His name was Tani Yutaka. Within a year, newspapers in Japan had made him a hero, and a feature film called him Harimau Malai, the Tiger of Malaya. Much of what is told about him comes from those wartime accounts and from books written decades later, and the few records from his own time tell a plainer story.
