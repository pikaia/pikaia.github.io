Title:
The Company That Outlived Its Own Tin Smelter

Description:
By 1912, a company most Singaporeans today have never heard of was smelting roughly a third of the world's tin, on a small island just off the harbour. Its ingots, stamped "Straits Tin," were internationally recognised as the purest grade money could buy. The smelter that made that possible is gone, the island it stood on is a naval base, and the company itself doesn't smelt anything in Singapore anymore. It is, remarkably, still around, still listed, still making money, more than a century after the furnaces on Pulau Brani first lit.
