Title:
Kallang Airport, 'the finest airport in the British Empire'

Description:
On the north bank of the Kallang Basin, across the water from the Singapore Sports Hub, stands a low, curved building the colour of pale butter, with a round tower rising from its centre. Its address today is 9 Stadium Link, just off Nicoll Highway. Tens of thousands of people pass it every week on the way to a football match or a run along the promenade, and most of them have no idea what it is. For eighteen years it was the passenger terminal of Kallang Airport, Singapore's first purpose-built civil aerodrome, described in its day as the finest in the British Empire and, by Amelia Earhart, as "an aviation miracle of the East." The airport closed in 1955. The terminal is still there, still empty, still waiting for someone to decide what to do with it.
