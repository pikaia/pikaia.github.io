Title:
The Sisters' Islands and Singapore's First Marine Park

Description:
Two small wooded islands sit side by side south of Sentosa, so close together that from the sea they look like a pair. A Malay legend says they were once two sisters who drowned there in a storm. In the colonial era soldiers fired howitzers at targets on them, in the 1970s they were reclaimed into picnic islands with swimming lagoons, and since 2014 they have been the heart of Singapore's first marine park, where hawksbill turtles now nest and hatch.
