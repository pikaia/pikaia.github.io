Title:
From Graves to Gardens: How Singapore Makes Room for Its Dead

Description:
By 1985, Singapore had cleared 21 cemeteries and exhumed about 120,000 graves to make room for flats, roads and the MRT. The clearing went on after that: Bidadari, once the island's largest cemetery, was emptied in the early 2000s, and today even a new grave is leased for only 15 years. Over the same decades, Singaporeans changed how they lay their dead to rest, from burial to cremation, from cemeteries to columbaria, and now to gardens where the ashes go back into the soil.
