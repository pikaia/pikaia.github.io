Title:
The $2.70 Fishball Noodle That Exposed Singapore's Hawker Rent Gap

Description:
In July 2015, a 24-year-old hawker named Douglas Ng went to a tender briefing for a new government-run hawker centre in Bukit Panjang and came away furious. His handmade fishball noodles, he was told, had to sell for a minimum of $2.70. A stall selling pasta at the same centre could charge a minimum of $5.80. Ng did the math out loud on Facebook: at $2.70 a bowl, after ingredients and labour, he was clearing about fifty cents of profit.
