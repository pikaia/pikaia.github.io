Title:
The Ghee Hin and the 1890 Ban on the Secret Societies

Description:
On the 1st of January 1890, a new law came into force in the Straits Settlements, and with it the oldest Chinese society in Singapore, the Ghee Hin, had to close. For more than sixty years the secret societies had taken in new arrivals from China, found them work, settled their quarrels and fought each other in the streets, and by 1888 they counted some 65,000 members in Singapore alone. Within months, the headmen of the ten societies the government had declared dangerous handed in their seals, flags and books.
