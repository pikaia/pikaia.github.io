Title:
John Turnbull Thomson: The Surveyor Who Drew 1840s Singapore

Description:
Thomson Road, Upper Thomson, Thomson Plaza and the Thomson–East Coast Line all carry the name of a man few Singaporeans could say much about. John Turnbull Thomson arrived in 1841 as the settlement's Government Surveyor, aged twenty, and for the next twelve years he measured, mapped and built much of the settlement while also sketching and painting what he saw. His pictures of the market on the shore, the public buildings and the people at work are some of the few images of 1840s Singapore made by someone who lived there, and twenty years later he wrote a book about the place and the people he had known.
