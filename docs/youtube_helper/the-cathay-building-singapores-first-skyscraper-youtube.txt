Title:
The Cathay Building: Singapore's First Skyscraper

Description:
At the corner of Handy Road and Dhoby Ghaut, a cream Art Deco tower with the word CATHAY running down its face still stands in front of a glass-walled shopping mall. Most people walking under it are on their way to a movie or a meal and have no idea that the mall behind it isn't the original building at all — the tower is the only piece of it left, and it's technically a national monument even though the building it belonged to was torn down.
