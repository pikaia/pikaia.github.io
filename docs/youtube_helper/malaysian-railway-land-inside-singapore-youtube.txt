Title:
The Strip of Malaysia That Ran Through Singapore Until 2011

Description:
Until the middle of 2011, catching the train to Kuala Lumpur from the centre of Singapore involved something strange: you walked into Tanjong Pagar Railway Station, a few minutes from the banks of Shenton Way, and cleared Malaysian immigration. The station, its platforms and a ribbon of land running most of the length of the island were owned and run by Malaysia's railway. For forty-six years after Singapore left the Federation, a strip of foreign-administered ground cut straight through the country.
