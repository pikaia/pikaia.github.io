Title:
The Singapore Sweep: How the Lottery Went Legal

Description:
On the first Wednesday of every month, Singapore Pools draws the Singapore Sweep, a lottery with 3.5 million seven-digit numbers and a first prize of $2.3 million. Its rules still describe it as a "sweepstakes game", a word that comes from the racecourse, and the game itself began at the Singapore Turf Club in 1966. At the end of 1968 it was taken over by Singapore Pools, a government-owned company set up that year to give people a legal way to bet and to take business away from the illegal lotteries. Between them the Sweep, Toto and 4-D became fixtures of Singapore life, and all three are still drawn today.
