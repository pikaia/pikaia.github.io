Title:
The LNG Terminal: The Insurance Policy Singapore Built in 2013

Description:
When Indonesia trimmed the pipeline gas it sends to Singapore in October 2026, the official answer took one sentence: more liquefied natural gas could be brought in at any time. That sentence rested on a terminal on Jurong Island that had been built thirteen years earlier, at a cost of S$1.7 billion, against exactly this day. This post is the story of that terminal — the companion piece to The Pipelines That Feed Singapore, which told the story of the pipes it insures against.
