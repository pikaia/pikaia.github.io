Title:
Forbidden Hill: The 1984 Dig That Found 14th-Century Singapura on Fort Canning

Description:
In January 1984 the American archaeologist John Miksic and a small team spent ten days digging test pits on Fort Canning Hill, beside the shrine known as the Keramat Iskandar Shah. For the first few days they found almost nothing of interest. Then the colour of the soil changed, and out came pieces of blue-and-white Chinese porcelain made in the 1300s. It was the first hard evidence that a town had stood on the hill some five hundred years before the British arrived, and that the Singapura of the old Malay chronicles was more than a story.
