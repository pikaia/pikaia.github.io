Title:
Interned in India: Singapore's Japanese Civilians, 1941–46

Description:
In the early hours of 8 December 1941, as Japanese bombs fell on Pearl Harbor and Singapore, the police in Singapore began rounding up every Japanese resident on the island. Shopkeepers, photographers, dentists, bank clerks, fishermen and their families were taken from their homes, and within weeks most of them were on their way to India, where about 2,700 civilians from Singapore, Malaya and the rest of British Asia spent the war behind the walls of a sixteenth-century fort in Delhi and then in a desert camp in Rajasthan. Few of them were ever allowed to make Singapore their home again.
