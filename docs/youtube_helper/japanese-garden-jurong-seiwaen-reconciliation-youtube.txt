Title:
The Japanese Garden That Reconciliation Built, Then Forgot

Description:
When the Japanese Garden in Jurong reopened in September 2024 after five years of redevelopment, the new attractions were a Water Lily Pavilion with over 150 varieties, a cenote-inspired Sunken Garden, and a "Breathing Gallery" of terrariums and aquariums. Nothing in the coverage of the relaunch explained why there was a Japanese garden at Jurong Lake in the first place. Fifty years earlier, its 1973 opening had nothing to do with water lilies — it was one of the more understated acts of postwar reconciliation between Singapore and the country whose army had occupied it only three decades before.
