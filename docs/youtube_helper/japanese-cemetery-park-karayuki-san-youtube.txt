Title:
The Largest Japanese Cemetery in Southeast Asia Began as a Grave for Trafficked Women

Description:
Walk into the Japanese Cemetery Park in Hougang today and the oldest graves aren't soldiers or merchants — they belong to Japanese women trafficked into Singapore's brothels a century and a half ago. The cemetery holds 910 tombstones, and by most accounts close to half of them mark the graves of karayuki-san: girls sold or lured out of impoverished Japanese fishing villages, shipped to Singapore, and put to work on the same streets — Bugis, Malay, Hylam and Malabar — covered in an earlier post about Bugis Street. The cemetery's own founding is stranger still: it exists because a brothel keeper paid for it.
