Title:
Force Z: The Sinking of the Prince of Wales and Repulse

Description:
On the 2nd of December 1941, two of the most powerful warships in the world came up the Johore Strait and anchored at Singapore, and the island thrilled to see them. They were the first battle fleet ever based there, and they had been sent halfway round the world for one purpose: to frighten Japan into leaving Malaya alone. Eight days later both ships were on the seabed off Kuantan, sunk by aircraft in under two hours, and 840 of their men were dead. No shot from Singapore's famous guns had been fired in their defence, because the guns faced the sea, and the blow had come from the air. This is the story of the deterrent that deterred nothing — and of the two wrecks, war graves both, that are still being fought over today.
