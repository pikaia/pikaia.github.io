Title:
The Hotel de l'Europe, Singapore's Grand Hotel Before the Raffles

Description:
In 1889, a young Rudyard Kipling passed through Singapore and left its hotels with a piece of advice that travellers repeated for decades: "Feed at Raffles and sleep at the Hotel de l'Europe." The Raffles Hotel's managers liked the first half so much that they quietly borrowed it for their own advertising, trimmed to just "Feed at Raffles". The second half pointed across the Padang to the Raffles' great rival — a hotel that was, for much of the nineteenth century, the grandest address in Singapore, and that today has vanished so completely that most people walk over its site without knowing it was ever there.
