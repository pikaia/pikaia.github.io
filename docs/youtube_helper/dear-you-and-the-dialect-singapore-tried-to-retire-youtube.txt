Title:
Dear You and the Dialect Singapore Spent 45 Years Trying to Retire

Description:
A lot of Singaporeans in their 40s and 50s can understand Teochew — enough to follow a grandmother's stories, or catch the gist of a hawker's banter — without being able to hold a real conversation in it. That gap between hearing and speaking isn't an accident. It's the direct, measurable result of a language policy that started in 1979 and largely succeeded. Which is what made it strange, this year, to watch a Teochew-language film called Dear You sell out its original-dialect screenings in Singapore faster than the Mandarin-dubbed version everyone expected to dominate.
