The Ageism Gap: What Singapore Employers Say vs. What They Do

Description:
Somewhere in the CBD right now, a 61-year-old is putting together a resume for the first time in twenty years — not because she wants a hobby, but because her CPF payouts don't quite stretch to cover the month, and re-employment at her old company runs out at 68. On paper, the system is built for exactly this: government messaging, tripartite guidelines, and a formal re-employment law all say older workers are welcome to keep working.
