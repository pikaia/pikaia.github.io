Title:
When Telok Ayer Street Was the Beach: Singapore's First Land Reclamations

Description:
When the Thian Hock Keng temple was built on Telok Ayer Street between 1839 and 1842, it stood on the beach. Sailors landing from China came up from the water to give thanks to Mazu, the goddess of the sea, for a safe crossing. By the end of the century it was four streets from the sea, and today the water is further away still. The land in front of it was made by the colonial government, which levelled hills, built seawalls and filled in the bay.
