Title:
Wee Bin, the Shipping Magnate Whose Firm Outlived Its Own Name

Description:
In March 1911, the Straits Times announced the end of one of Singapore's oldest Chinese businesses. "Steps are in progress," it reported, "to wind up the well-known and long-established business of Messrs. Wee Bin and Co., of 106 Market St." Three weeks later the firm's steamers were sold by public auction on the waterfront they had served for half a century.
