Title:
The Selarang Square Squeeze, 1942

Description:
On the 2nd of September 1942, somewhere between 15,000 and 17,000 British and Australian prisoners of war were marched into the barracks square at Selarang, in Changi, and kept there until they agreed to sign a promise not to escape. The square had been built for a single battalion. For the next four days the men lived on it and on the roofs and stairs of the barrack blocks around it, with a few taps for water and latrines dug through the parade ground, while dysentery spread. One of them, an Australian private named George Aspinall, photographed the square in secret with a small folding camera, and his pictures are the reason we can still see it.
