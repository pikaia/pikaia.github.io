Title:
The Gurkha Contingent: Singapore's Neutral Force, 1949–1965

Description:
In February 1949 an officer of the Singapore police flew to New Delhi to bring back 148 Gurkhas recruited in the hills of Nepal. They were to form a new riot squad, replacing the Sikh contingent that had kept order in Singapore since 1881, and they were valued for one quality above all: they belonged to none of the communities they might be called on to police. Within two years that quality was tested in the worst communal riots Singapore had seen, and the Gurkha Contingent has been part of the Singapore police ever since.
