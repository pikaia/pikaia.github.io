Title:
The Japanese Fishermen Who Fed Prewar Singapore

Description:
In the 1930s, Japanese fishermen supplied 40 to 50 per cent of the fresh fish sold at Singapore's municipal markets, even though there were only about a third as many of them as local Chinese and Malay fishermen. Within a few years, licence limits, a boycott and growing suspicion had cut the fleet down to a fraction of its size, and by December 1941 it was gone.
