Title:
Haw Par Villa: The Tiger Balm Garden of Statues

Description:
On a hill above Pasir Panjang Road stands the strangest garden in Singapore: a terraced hillside crowded with painted concrete statues — gods and demons, monkeys and mermaids, sinners being sawn in half — built by the Tiger Balm fortune as part gift, part advertisement and part moral textbook. It has outlived the mansion it was built around, the company town of theme parks that tried to replace it, and the family that made it. Today it waits behind a partial closure while the state decides what to do with it. This is the garden's own story; the brothers who paid for it, Aw Boon Haw and Aw Boon Par, have their own post on this blog.
