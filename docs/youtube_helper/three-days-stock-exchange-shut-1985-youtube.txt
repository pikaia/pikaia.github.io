Title:
The Three Days Singapore's Stock Market Closed

Description:
On Monday, the 2nd of December 1985, the Stock Exchange of Singapore did not open for trading. Neither did the Kuala Lumpur Stock Exchange. Both stayed shut for three days — the only time Singapore's market has ever closed completely. What forced it was a single company, Pan-Electric Industries, a refrigerator maker that had grown into a sprawling conglomerate, and a web of undisclosed contracts that had been propping up its share price. At the centre of it was Tan Koon Swan, a businessman who three weeks earlier had been elected leader of Malaysia's main Chinese political party.
