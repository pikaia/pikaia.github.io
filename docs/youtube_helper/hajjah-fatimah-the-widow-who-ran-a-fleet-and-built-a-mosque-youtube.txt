Title:
Hajjah Fatimah: The Widow Who Ran a Fleet and Built a Mosque

Description:
In the early years of British Singapore, a widow from Malacca ran one of the trading businesses on the Kampong Glam shore, with ships of her own and houses across the town. Robbers broke into her home on Java Road twice, and the second time they set it on fire while she was away. She gave thanks for her escape by building a mosque on the site of her house, and in 1845 and 1846 it went up beside the sea. The mosque still stands on Beach Road and still bears her name, Hajjah Fatimah, but the sea has long since moved away.
