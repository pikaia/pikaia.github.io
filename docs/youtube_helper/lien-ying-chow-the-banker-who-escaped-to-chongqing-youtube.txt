Title:
Lien Ying Chow, the Banker Who Escaped to Chongqing

Description:
In the last days before Singapore surrendered to the Japanese in February 1942, a Blue Funnel cargo liner called the Gorgon left the harbour for Australia with more than 350 refugees on board. One of them was Lien Ying Chow, the 35-year-old president of the Singapore Chinese Chamber of Commerce, who was carrying some cash and two diamonds hidden in the lining of his clothes. The ship survived a Japanese dive-bombing attack on the way and reached Fremantle on 20 February. Seven years later, Lien opened the first new bank in postwar Singapore, Overseas Union Bank, and for half a century it was one of the island's four big local banks.
