=== slide 16 (POTTER): 34.6s [279.4s - 314.1s] ===
Lim, by then 80 and long retired from the NWC, publicly broke with the wage restraint he'd spent later years counselling and proposed what economists half-jokingly called "Shock Therapy Two": a cumulative 50 per cent pay rise over three years for everyone earning under $1,500 a month, paired with a voluntary freeze on salaries above $1 million a year, to be extended to anyone earning over $15,000 a month if income inequality kept widening.
