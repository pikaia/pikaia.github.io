Beside the legal games ran illegal ones. The best known was chap-ji-kee, Hokkien for "12 cards", a numbers lottery run by syndicates. Punters bet on a pair of numbers from 1 to 12, and the syndicates added up the bets and declared the least-backed pair the winner. It was known as the "housewives' opium", because many of its players were women who staked a few cents at a time in the hope of a little extra money for the household.
