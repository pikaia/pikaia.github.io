What followed was decades of steady expansion, tracked in the official numbers from 2009 onward. What the chart doesn't show is quite as interesting as what it does. Over this period, Singapore's total foreign workforce grew from about 1.05 million to 1.64 million — and foreign domestic workers have held fairly steady at somewhere between 16% and 20% of that total the whole way through.
