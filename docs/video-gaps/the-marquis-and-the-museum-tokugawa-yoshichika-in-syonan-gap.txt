=== slide 14 (TOK1913): 34.4s [195.1s - 229.5s] ===
Yoshichika was born in Tokyo in 1886, the youngest son of Matsudaira Shungaku, a former feudal lord. In 1908 he was adopted into the Owari branch of the Tokugawa family, one of the three senior branches of the clan that had ruled Japan until 1868, and became its 19th head. He studied history at Tokyo Imperial University and then turned to biology, and in 1918 he founded his own research institute in Tokyo.

=== slide 19 (SS420905): 30.2s [294.0s - 324.2s] ===
The Syonan Times report of the 5th of September 1942 said that the institutions had been "greatly enlarged under the direction of Dr. Hideso Tanakadate, who will continue his duties under Marquis Tokugawa". The Gardens' 1947 report describes the Marquis's position as Honorary President of the Museum and Gardens, which he held until he went back to Japan in the middle of 1944.
