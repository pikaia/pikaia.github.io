Long-hold report for elizabeth-choy-canteen-operator-who-wouldnt-break

=== slide 4 (WEDDING1941): 30.4s [81.2s - 111.6s] ===
Her mother died in 1931, and with the Great Depression squeezing the family, Choy gave up a shot at a college scholarship and went to work instead, to help raise her six younger siblings. She began teaching at the Church of England Zenana Mission School (today's Saint Margaret's School) in 1933, then transferred to Saint Andrew's School in 1935, by her own account probably the only untrained teacher on the staff at the time.
