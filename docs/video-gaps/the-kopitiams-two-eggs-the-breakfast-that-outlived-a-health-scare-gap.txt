Long-hold report for the-kopitiams-two-eggs-the-breakfast-that-outlived-a-health-scare

=== slide 36 (EGGS): 34.2s [573.8s - 608.0s] ===
In March 2019 a JAMA analysis pooling six American cohorts — 29,615 people followed for a median of seventeen and a half years — reported that each additional 300 milligrams of dietary cholesterol a day was associated with a higher risk of cardiovascular disease and death, and each additional half egg a day with a smaller but real increase (though the egg associations faded once dietary cholesterol itself was accounted for, and the study's critics noted that diet had been measured only once, at the start).
