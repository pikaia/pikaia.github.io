=== slide 12 (PEOPLE): 30.1s [190.8s - 220.9s] ===
The round-up also took in people from Japan's empire: Taiwanese and Koreans, who were then Japanese subjects, as well as a few Chinese and Malays. When the camp in India was counted in May 1943, its 2,101 internees were made up of 1,889 Japanese, including Okinawans, 184 Taiwanese, 10 Chinese, 7 Thais, 5 Malays, 5 Koreans and one Russian.

=== slide 47 (SUN1946): 30.1s [722.5s - 752.6s] ===
The internees finally left Deoli in May 1946, nine months after the surrender. They were not allowed to go home to Singapore. An official notice in January 1946 had announced that Japanese, Korean and Taiwanese civilians would not be permitted to remain in Malaya and would be evacuated in the same way as Japanese troops, and that no applications to stay on compassionate grounds would be considered.
