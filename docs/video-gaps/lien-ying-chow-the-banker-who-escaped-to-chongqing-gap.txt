Long-hold report for lien-ying-chow-the-banker-who-escaped-to-chongqing

=== slide 23 (ST1949): 30.3s [307.2s - 337.5s] ===
The new bank was called Overseas Union Bank. It had $2 million in paid-up capital and 27 staff, including Lien and its general manager, Chi-Owyang, who had run the Chongqing bank. The Straits Times noted the day before it opened that there were "more than a dozen multi-millionaires" on its 15-man board. The chairman was Tan-Sia-Kuang, a banker and rubber company owner, and Lien was managing director.
