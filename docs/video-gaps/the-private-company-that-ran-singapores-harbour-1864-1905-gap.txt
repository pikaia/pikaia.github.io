Long-hold report for the-private-company-that-ran-singapores-harbour-1864-1905

=== slide 53 (BAR): 31.7s [622.6s - 654.4s] ===
The questioner, Josiah Wedgwood, put the other side of the case to the House: that the shares had been quoted at about $240 when expropriation was decided on, that the directors had mentioned $700 to the Governor, and that with a total capital of $3.7 million on which 12 per cent was paid, the award came to about $28.5 million, or sixty-five years' purchase of the annual dividend, against the fourteen years' purchase the Governor had said was usual.
