There were campaigns as well. The first Nippon-go Popularizing Week ran for the first seven days of June 1942, and the second opened on the 14th of November 1943. For that week the paper asked its readers to "speak Nippon-Go on every occasion", suggested that employers arrange picnics or parties "at which conversation should be entirely in Nippon-Go", and proposed Nippon-go speaking societies whose members would pay a fine whenever they spoke another language.
