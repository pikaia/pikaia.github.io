Long-hold report for the-chinese-protectorate-pickering-the-poh-leung-kuk-and-the-coolie-trade

=== slide 26 (ST87): 31.9s [380.3s - 412.2s] ===
According to Song Ong Siang's history of the Chinese in Singapore, published in 1923, Chua Ah Siok had been put up to the attack by members of the Ghee Hok society. He was arrested on the spot, and said that although he had been hired to kill, he had decided not to kill a public officer. At the November 1887 assizes he pleaded guilty to voluntarily causing grievous hurt and was sentenced to seven years' rigorous imprisonment.
