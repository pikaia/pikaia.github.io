=== slide 47 (COBBOLD): 34.6s [590.5s - 625.0s] ===
In 1962 a commission led by Lord Cobbold went to North Borneo and Sarawak to find out whether their people supported joining a new federation with Malaya and Singapore. Its report, signed in June and published on the 1st of August 1962, found that about a third of the people in each territory strongly favoured the plan and another third favoured it with safeguards, while the remaining third were divided between those who wanted independence first and those who preferred British rule to continue.
