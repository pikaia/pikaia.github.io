# Privacy Policy — Seven YouTube Upload

*Last updated: 22 November 2026*

Seven YouTube Upload is a private tool used by the owner of this site to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

## What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

- upload videos to the owner's channel, and
- read the channel's identity, so each video is placed on the correct channel.

## What the app does with data

- The app does not collect personal data, and it does not run a server that stores any.
- The authorisation token created when the owner approves access is stored only on the owner's own computers, and is used only to perform the uploads the owner directs.
- No data is sold, shared, or sent to any third party through the app. Sign-in and uploads are processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately stops the app from acting on the channel.
